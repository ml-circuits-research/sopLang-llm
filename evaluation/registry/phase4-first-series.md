# First series — comparison of the completed arms

`training/PLAN.md` T8 asks for one table that compares the first-series arms on the four rates of `DS009`. This is it, for every arm whose evaluation has landed, including the widened-suite and structure arms that followed. Numbers come from the registry folders named in the last column (`run-manifest.json`, `selection.md`, `items/holdout.jsonl`, `metrics.json`, `probes.md`) and from `training/checkpoints/<experiment>/run-manifest.json`; the plan split is reproduced from the per-item records by `node evaluation/analyze-holdout.mjs --experiment <id>`.

The holdout is the `eval/` examples of the seven books, 225 items whose 16 plan fingerprints occur nowhere in the export, plus the 40 instances of the one withheld synthetic family once the procedural source entered the tree: `exp-002` and `exp-003` were measured on the 225 book-only items, every later arm on the same 265. The validation slice is the same 339 D11 rows in every row, of which 323 sit on plan fingerprints that also occur in the training rows and 16 do not.

Every full-fine-tuning arm uses the same recipe apart from the suite: 3 epochs at effective batch 32 (per-device batch 4 with gradient accumulation 8), AdamW, cosine schedule with 3% warmup, gradient checkpointing, seed 3407, max sequence length 4096, BF16, declared device-memory budget of `training/PLAN.md` (fraction 0.75, floor 16 GiB, stop-and-resume). `exp-002` through `exp-007` ran under chat profile `compiled-plan-chat-1`; `exp-008` runs under `compiled-plan-chat-2`, whose system prompt names the intermediate wires its targets may carry (DS008 requires the profile to match the suite it describes). Each row's accuracy and every speed number of a row come from the same artifact named in that row's own registry folder.

Reading the ladder: the untuned base cannot emit a parseable program at all; after fine-tuning, parse and graph validity are perfect and the executed answers are correct for rows whose plan fingerprint the trainer saw (98.8% at the higher learning rate), partially correct for unseen plans of the same books (12.5-18.8% on 16 rows), and never reliably correct for families absent from the export: the best holdout result of the series is 1 of 265, and the arm that reached it is not the same one in every case (`exp-005` answered compare-two-groups-by-percentage-not-absolute-count, `exp-007` answered plant-growth-rate-as-change-per-day). The gap between the validation column and the holdout column is therefore not a slice artifact: it is the plan-coverage boundary, and `evaluation/registry/phase4-analysis.md` records the failure mechanisms behind it, the decision it implies for the next series, and the structure arm this table's last row measures.

Series status (2026-09-21): complete for every arm — the baseline, the two overfit gates, the two full-fine-tuning arms of the book-only suite, the LoRA arm, the widened-suite arm, and the structure arm — each with a run manifest, a selection table with the plan-seen/plan-unseen split, one holdout run of its winner, and the capability-probe score on the same served artifact; `exp-008-sft-shapes` is in flight on the widest suite. The capability column is the second finding of the series: the untuned base passes 4 of 10 probes and every fine-tuned arm passes 0 or 1, so the narrow SOP Lang mixture costs the language and code substrate that `DS009` warns about, and preservation material is the next recipe variable. Two instrument rows of the plan remain open: the split of `evaluation/run-eval.mjs` to the DS001 size rule, and T11's generalization probes from generated families (the knowledge-enrichment part of T11 stays deferred because knowledge rows do not fail disproportionately).
