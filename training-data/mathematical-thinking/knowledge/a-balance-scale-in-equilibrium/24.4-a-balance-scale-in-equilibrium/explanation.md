# Explanation 24.4 — A balance scale in equilibrium

## Explanation

1. The statement says the balance is level but never defines what a level balance means, so the circuit states the fact that level means equal total mass on the two pans.
2. With that fact the 2 identical cubes together weigh the 10-unit weight, and splitting the total into equal parts gives 5 units for one cube.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 24, 4 steps):

1. Balance means the total masses are equal.
2. The two identical cubes together have mass 10 units.
3. Divide the total into two equal parts: 10÷2=5.
4. Each cube has the same mass.

## Result

**Answer.** 5 units.
