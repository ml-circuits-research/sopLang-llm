# Explanation 10.15 — Meters and Centimeters 5

## Explanation

1. The ribbon is 2 m plus another 70 cm.
2. A meter is a larger unit than a centimeter, so the two parts cannot be added before the meters are converted.
3. The unit rule 1 m = 100 cm turns 2 m into 200 cm.
4. Adding the converted part and the extra part gives 270 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 10, 2 steps):

1. 2 m = 2×100=200 cm.
2. Add the 70 cm: 200+70=270 cm.
