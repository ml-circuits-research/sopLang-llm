# Explanation 10.12 — Meters and Centimeters 2

## Explanation

1. The ribbon is 3 m plus another 20 cm.
2. A meter is a larger unit than a centimeter, so the two parts cannot be added before the meters are converted.
3. The unit rule 1 m = 100 cm turns 3 m into 300 cm.
4. Adding the converted part and the extra part gives 320 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 10, 2 steps):

1. 3 m = 3×100=300 cm.
2. Add the 20 cm: 300+20=320 cm.

**Answer.** 320 cm
