# Explanation 10.13 — Meters and Centimeters 3

## Explanation

1. The ribbon is 1 m plus another 85 cm.
2. A meter is a larger unit than a centimeter, so the two parts cannot be added before the meters are converted.
3. The unit rule 1 m = 100 cm turns 1 m into 100 cm.
4. Adding the converted part and the extra part gives 185 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 10, 2 steps):

1. 1 m = 1×100=100 cm.
2. Add the 85 cm: 100+85=185 cm.
