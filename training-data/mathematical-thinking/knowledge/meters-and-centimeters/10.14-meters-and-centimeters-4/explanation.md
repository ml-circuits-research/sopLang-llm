# Explanation 10.14 — Meters and Centimeters 4

## Explanation

1. The ribbon is 4 m plus another 5 cm.
2. A meter is a larger unit than a centimeter, so the two parts cannot be added before the meters are converted.
3. The unit rule 1 m = 100 cm turns 4 m into 400 cm.
4. Adding the converted part and the extra part gives 405 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 10, 2 steps):

1. 4 m = 4×100=400 cm.
2. Add the 5 cm: 400+5=405 cm.
