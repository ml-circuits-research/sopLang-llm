# Explanation 35.11 — Plant growth rate as change per day

## Explanation

1. The plant is 10 cm tall on Monday and 16 cm tall after 3 days.
2. The total growth is 16 - 10 = 6 cm.
3. Equal growth each day divides that change evenly: 6 / 3 = 2 cm/day.
