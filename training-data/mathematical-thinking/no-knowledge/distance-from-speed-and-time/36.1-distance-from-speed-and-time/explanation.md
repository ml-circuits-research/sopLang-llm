# Explanation 36.1 — Distance from speed and time

## Explanation

1. The statement defines a constant speed of 6 km/h as 6 km traveled every hour.
2. A constant rate makes distance the product of speed and time: 6 × 4.
3. The traveled distance is therefore 24 km.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 36, 4 steps):

1. In one hour, 6 km are traveled.
2. In 4 hours there are four groups of 6 km.
3. 6×4=24.
4. The constant rate justifies multiplication.

## Result
