# Explanation 35.5 — Conservation of mass during transfer

## Explanation

1. The two containers hold 900 g of sand together before the transfer.
2. Moving 200 g takes that amount out of the first container and puts the same amount into the second.
3. The loss and the gain cancel exactly, and nothing leaves the system, so the total is unchanged at 900 g.
