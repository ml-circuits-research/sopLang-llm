# Explanation 24.8 — Estimating by bounding

## Explanation

1. The ruler gives only two facts: the stick is longer than 8 cm and shorter than 9 cm, and both hold at the same time.
2. The most accurate description the data supports is the interval between 8 cm and 9 cm; no single value is guaranteed.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 24, 4 steps):

1. The information does not give an exact value.
2. Every allowed value must be greater than 8.
3. Every allowed value must be less than 9.
4. The most accurate description is the interval between 8 and 9 cm.

## Result

**Answer.** The length is between 8 cm and 9 cm.
