# Explanation 14.6 — Side Length from a Known Area 1

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule area = number of rows × number of squares per row, so the unknown is the number of rows.
2. The rectangle covers 36 unit squares and every row holds 9 of them, so the rows are the missing factor of the product.
3. Dividing the area by the row length recovers that factor: 36 ÷ 9.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 14, 3 steps):

1. Divide the 36 squares into rows of 9.
2. 36÷9=4.
3. Therefore, the other side is 4 units long.
