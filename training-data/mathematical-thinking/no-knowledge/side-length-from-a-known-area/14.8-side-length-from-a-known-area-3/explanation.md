# Explanation 14.8 — Side Length from a Known Area 3

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule area = number of rows × number of squares per row, so the unknown is the number of rows.
2. The rectangle covers 56 unit squares and every row holds 8 of them, so the rows are the missing factor of the product.
3. Dividing the area by the row length recovers that factor: 56 ÷ 8.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 14, 3 steps):

1. Divide the 56 squares into rows of 8.
2. 56÷8=7.
3. Therefore, the other side is 7 units long.
