# Explanation 24.1 — Measuring with equal sticks

## Explanation

1. Both pencils are measured with the same stick, so the stick is the common unit u and the lengths are 7u and 5u.
2. Because the unit is the same, comparing the counts 7 and 5 compares the lengths, and their difference is the difference in units.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 24, 4 steps):

1. Both objects are measured with the same unit u.
2. Compare the numbers of units: 7>5.
3. The difference is 7−5=2 units.
4. Using the same unit makes the comparison valid.

## Result

**Answer.** The first pencil, by 2 units.
