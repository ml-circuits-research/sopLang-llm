# 19.14 — Remainder upon Division by 3 as an Invariant 4

Definition: the “remainder upon division by 3” is what remains after removing as many groups of 3 as possible; the remainder can be 0, 1, or 2. We start at 25 and may add only 3. Can we reach 29? Observe that adding a complete group of 3 does not change the remainder.
