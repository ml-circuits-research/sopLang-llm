# Explanation 35.4 — Mass of contents by subtraction

## Explanation

1. The container together with the material weighs 800 g, and the empty container weighs 250 g.
2. The material is what is left when the container's own mass is removed, so the masses are subtracted.
3. The mass of the material is 550 g.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 35, 4 steps):

1. The total includes both the container and the material.
2. Remove the container's mass from the total.
3. 800-250=550.
4. Check: 250+550=800.

## Result

**Answer.** 550 g.
