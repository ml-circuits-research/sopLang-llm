# Explanation 18.6 — Perimeter of an L-Shaped Figure 1

## Explanation

1. The perimeter is defined as the sum of all segments of the outer boundary, so every listed side is used exactly once.
2. Adding the boundary segments in order gives 8 + 3 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 7 = 30 cm.
3. Nothing is subtracted for the concave corners: an L-shaped figure still contributes each boundary segment to its perimeter.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 18, 2 steps):

1. Add all 6 segments: 8+3+3+4+5+7.
2. The sum is 30 cm.
