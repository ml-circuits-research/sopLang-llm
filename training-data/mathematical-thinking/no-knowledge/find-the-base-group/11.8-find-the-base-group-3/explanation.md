# Explanation 11.8 — Find the Base Group 3

## Explanation

1. The large collection has 25 objects and equals 5 equal groups, each as large as the small collection.
2. Since the groups are equal and together fill the large collection, the small collection is the size of one group.
3. Dividing the large collection by the number of groups, 25 ÷ 5 = 5, gives the size of the small collection.
