# Explanation 35.1 — Temperature as a position on a scale

## Explanation

1. The reading starts at 12°C, which is the position of the temperature on the scale.
2. A rise of 5 means that the position moves 5 steps upward, so the two values are added.
3. The final position is 17°C.

1. Start at 12.
2. An increase means addition.
3. 12+5=17.
4. The unit remains degrees Celsius.
