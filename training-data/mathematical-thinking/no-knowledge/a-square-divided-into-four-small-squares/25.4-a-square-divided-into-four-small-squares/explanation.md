# Explanation 25.4 — A square divided into four small squares

## Explanation

1. The dividing vertical line and horizontal line each run from one side of the square to the opposite side, so each is a whole straight segment.
2. That gives 2 interior segments plus the 4 outer sides of the square.
3. Altogether 6 complete straight segments have been drawn.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 25, 4 steps):

1. The outer boundary of the square has 4 complete segments.
2. The division adds one complete vertical line.
3. It also adds one complete horizontal line.
4. Under the convention in the problem, their intersection does not split them into new segments for counting.

## Result

**Answer.** 6 complete segments.
