# Explanation 31.1 — Equally likely outcomes

## Explanation

1. The bag holds 2 balls in total, all of identical size, so the choice is equally likely between them.
2. Exactly 1 of those balls are red, so red is favorable in 1 of the 2 equally likely outcomes.
3. The chance of red is therefore 1/2.
