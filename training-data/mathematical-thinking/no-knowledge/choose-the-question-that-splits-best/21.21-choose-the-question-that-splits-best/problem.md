# 21.21 — Choose the Question That Splits Best

The secret number is one of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. Compare two questions: Q1 “Is the number 1?” and Q2 “Is the number at most 4?”. A question is more balanced if, regardless of the answer, as few candidates as possible remain. Which question is more balanced?
