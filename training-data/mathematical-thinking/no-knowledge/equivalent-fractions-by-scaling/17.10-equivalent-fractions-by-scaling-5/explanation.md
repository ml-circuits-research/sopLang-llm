# Explanation 17.10 — Equivalent Fractions by Scaling 5

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule: multiplying the numerator and the denominator of a fraction by the same nonzero number represents the same portion of the whole.
2. Applying the factor 5 to both numbers changes 3/10 into (3×5)/(10×5).
3. That is the fraction 15/50, which cuts the whole into 5 times as many pieces and takes 5 times as many of them.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 17, 3 steps):

1. Numerator: 3×5=15.
2. Denominator: 10×5=50.
3. The equivalent fraction is 15/50.
