# Explanation 17.7 — Equivalent Fractions by Scaling 2

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule: multiplying the numerator and the denominator of a fraction by the same nonzero number represents the same portion of the whole.
2. Applying the factor 4 to both numbers changes 3/5 into (3×4)/(5×4).
3. That is the fraction 12/20, which cuts the whole into 4 times as many pieces and takes 4 times as many of them.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 17, 3 steps):

1. Numerator: 3×4=12.
2. Denominator: 5×4=20.
3. The equivalent fraction is 12/20.
