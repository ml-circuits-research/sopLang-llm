# Explanation 17.9 — Equivalent Fractions by Scaling 4

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule: multiplying the numerator and the denominator of a fraction by the same nonzero number represents the same portion of the whole.
2. Applying the factor 3 to both numbers changes 5/8 into (5×3)/(8×3).
3. That is the fraction 15/24, which cuts the whole into 3 times as many pieces and takes 3 times as many of them.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 17, 3 steps):

1. Numerator: 5×3=15.
2. Denominator: 8×3=24.
3. The equivalent fraction is 15/24.
