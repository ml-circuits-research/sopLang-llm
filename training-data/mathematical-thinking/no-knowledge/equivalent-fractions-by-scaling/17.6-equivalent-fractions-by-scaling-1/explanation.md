# Explanation 17.6 — Equivalent Fractions by Scaling 1

## Explanation

1. The statement gives the rule: multiplying the numerator and the denominator of a fraction by the same nonzero number represents the same portion of the whole.
2. Applying the factor 3 to both numbers changes 2/3 into (2×3)/(3×3).
3. That is the fraction 6/9, which cuts the whole into 3 times as many pieces and takes 3 times as many of them.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 17, 3 steps):

1. Numerator: 2×3=6.
2. Denominator: 3×3=9.
3. The equivalent fraction is 6/9.
