# Explanation 30.15 — Central symmetry on a number line

## Explanation

1. Point A is at 7 and the center is at 10, so A is 3 units to the left of the center.
2. The centrally symmetric point lies the same distance on the other side, as the statement says, so it is at 10 + 3 = 13.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 30, 4 steps):

1. A's distance from the center is 10−7=3.
2. Central symmetry preserves the distance and changes the side.
3. Move 3 units right from 10.
4. This gives 13.
