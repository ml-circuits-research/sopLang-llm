# Explanation 30.22 — Two figures congruent through rigid transformations

## Explanation

1. The problem defines congruent as being able to overlap by translation, rotation, or reflection, without enlargement.
2. The two triangles have the same three side lengths and their orientations differ, but rotation and reflection change orientation while preserving lengths.
3. Orientation alone therefore cannot prevent congruence, so the answer is no.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 30, 4 steps):

1. The definition allows rotations and reflections.
2. These operations can change orientation.
3. They do not change lengths.
4. Therefore a different orientation alone does not prevent overlap.
