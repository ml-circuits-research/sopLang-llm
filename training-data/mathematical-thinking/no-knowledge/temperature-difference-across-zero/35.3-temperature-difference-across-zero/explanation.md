# Explanation 35.3 — Temperature difference across zero

## Explanation

1. The temperature stands at -2°C in the morning and at 5°C at noon.
2. The rise is the distance between the two positions on the same scale, so it is 5 - (-2).
3. Crossing zero is handled by the sign of the result, and the rise is 7°C.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 35, 4 steps):

1. From -2 to 0 is 2 degrees.
2. From 0 to 5 is 5.
3. The total increase is 2+5=7.
4. We do not add the signs as labels; we measure distance on the scale.

## Result

**Answer.** 7°C.
