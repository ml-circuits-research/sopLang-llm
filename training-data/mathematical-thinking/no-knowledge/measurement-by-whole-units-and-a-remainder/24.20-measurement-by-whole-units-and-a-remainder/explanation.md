# Explanation 24.20 — Measurement by whole units and a remainder

## Explanation

1. The ribbon covers 4 complete units of 10 cm plus 0.5 of one more unit.
2. The whole part is 40 cm and the remainder is 5 cm; both are in the same unit, so they add to 45 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 24, 4 steps):

1. Four complete units mean 4×10=40 cm.
2. Half of 10 cm is 5 cm.
3. Add 40+5=45 cm.
4. We have expressed all parts in the same unit.

## Result

**Answer.** 45 cm.
