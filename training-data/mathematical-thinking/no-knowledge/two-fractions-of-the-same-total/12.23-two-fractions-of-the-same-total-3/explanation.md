# Explanation 12.23 — Two Fractions of the Same Total 3

## Explanation

1. Both fractions cut the same total of 36 objects into 9 equal parts.
2. One part holds 36 ÷ 9 = 4 objects.
3. Group A takes 4 parts and group B takes 3 parts, 4 + 3 = 7 parts together.
4. That is 4 · 7 = 28 objects.
