# Explanation 12.25 — Two Fractions of the Same Total 5

## Explanation

1. Both fractions cut the same total of 40 objects into 8 equal parts.
2. One part holds 40 ÷ 8 = 5 objects.
3. Group A takes 3 parts and group B takes 2 parts, 3 + 2 = 5 parts together.
4. That is 5 · 5 = 25 objects.
