# Explanation 12.24 — Two Fractions of the Same Total 4

## Explanation

1. Both fractions cut the same total of 28 objects into 7 equal parts.
2. One part holds 28 ÷ 7 = 4 objects.
3. Group A takes 2 parts and group B takes 4 parts, 2 + 4 = 6 parts together.
4. That is 4 · 6 = 24 objects.
