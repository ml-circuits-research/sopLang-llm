# Explanation 7.17 — The Balance with an Unknown Box 2

## Explanation

1. Equilibrium means the two pans weigh the same, so the unknown box plus 7 cubes must equal 18 cubes.
2. Removing the 7 loose cubes from both sides keeps the balance and leaves the box alone on the left.
3. The box therefore holds 18 - 7 = 11 cubes, and 11 + 7 = 18 restores the equilibrium as a check.
