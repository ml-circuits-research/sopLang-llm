# Explanation 7.18 — The Balance with an Unknown Box 3

## Explanation

1. Equilibrium means the two pans weigh the same, so the unknown box plus 6 cubes must equal 19 cubes.
2. Removing the 6 loose cubes from both sides keeps the balance and leaves the box alone on the left.
3. The box therefore holds 19 - 6 = 13 cubes, and 13 + 6 = 19 restores the equilibrium as a check.
