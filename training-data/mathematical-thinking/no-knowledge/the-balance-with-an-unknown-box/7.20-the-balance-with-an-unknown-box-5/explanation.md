# Explanation 7.20 — The Balance with an Unknown Box 5

## Explanation

1. Equilibrium means the two pans weigh the same, so the unknown box plus 8 cubes must equal 25 cubes.
2. Removing the 8 loose cubes from both sides keeps the balance and leaves the box alone on the left.
3. The box therefore holds 25 - 8 = 17 cubes, and 17 + 8 = 25 restores the equilibrium as a check.
