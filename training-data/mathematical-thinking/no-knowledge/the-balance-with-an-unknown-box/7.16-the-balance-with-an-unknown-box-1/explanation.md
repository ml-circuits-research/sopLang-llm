# Explanation 7.16 — The Balance with an Unknown Box 1

## Explanation

1. Equilibrium means the two pans weigh the same, so the unknown box plus 5 cubes must equal 13 cubes.
2. Removing the 5 loose cubes from both sides keeps the balance and leaves the box alone on the left.
3. The box therefore holds 13 - 5 = 8 cubes, and 8 + 5 = 13 restores the equilibrium as a check.
