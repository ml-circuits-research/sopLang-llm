# Explanation 36.22 — Rate on a segment from a table

## Explanation

1. A constant speed on a segment equals the change of position divided by the change of time.
2. Between minutes 2 and 5 only those two rows of the table matter.
3. The speed on the segment is 4 m/min.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 36, 4 steps):

1. It goes from 8 m to 20 m: distance 12 m.
2. The time is 5-2=3 minutes.
3. 12÷3=4.
4. The speed on the segment is 4 m/min.

## Result

**Answer.** 4 m/min.
