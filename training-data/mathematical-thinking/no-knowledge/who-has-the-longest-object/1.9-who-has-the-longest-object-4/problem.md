# 1.9 — Who Has the Longest Object? 4

Ioana, Sofia, Tudor, and Vlad each have a ribbon. The rule used is: if X’s ribbon is longer than Y’s, and Y’s is longer than Z’s, then X’s ribbon is longer than Z’s. We know that Sofia’s ribbon is longer than Tudor’s; Vlad’s ribbon is shorter than Tudor’s; and Ioana’s ribbon is longer than Sofia’s. Who has the longest ribbon, and who has the shortest?
