# 1.8 — Who Has the Longest Object? 3

Radu, Ioana, Sofia, and Tudor each have a ribbon. The rule used is: if X’s ribbon is longer than Y’s, and Y’s is longer than Z’s, then X’s ribbon is longer than Z’s. We know that Ioana’s ribbon is longer than Sofia’s; Tudor’s ribbon is shorter than Sofia’s; and Radu’s ribbon is longer than Ioana’s. Who has the longest ribbon, and who has the shortest?
