# 1.7 — Who Has the Longest Object? 2

Matei, Radu, Ioana, and Sofia each have a ribbon. The rule used is: if X’s ribbon is longer than Y’s, and Y’s is longer than Z’s, then X’s ribbon is longer than Z’s. We know that Radu’s ribbon is longer than Ioana’s; Sofia’s ribbon is shorter than Ioana’s; and Matei’s ribbon is longer than Radu’s. Who has the longest ribbon, and who has the shortest?
