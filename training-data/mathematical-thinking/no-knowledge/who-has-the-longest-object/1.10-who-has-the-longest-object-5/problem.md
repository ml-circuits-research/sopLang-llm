# 1.10 — Who Has the Longest Object? 5

Sofia, Tudor, Vlad, and Ilinca each have a ribbon. The rule used is: if X’s ribbon is longer than Y’s, and Y’s is longer than Z’s, then X’s ribbon is longer than Z’s. We know that Tudor’s ribbon is longer than Vlad’s; Ilinca’s ribbon is shorter than Vlad’s; and Sofia’s ribbon is longer than Tudor’s. Who has the longest ribbon, and who has the shortest?
