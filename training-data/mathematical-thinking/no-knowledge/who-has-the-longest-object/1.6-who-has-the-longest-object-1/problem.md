# 1.6 — Who Has the Longest Object? 1

Luca, Matei, Radu, and Ioana each have a ribbon. The rule used is: if X’s ribbon is longer than Y’s, and Y’s is longer than Z’s, then X’s ribbon is longer than Z’s. We know that Matei’s ribbon is longer than Radu’s; Ioana’s ribbon is shorter than Radu’s; and Luca’s ribbon is longer than Matei’s. Who has the longest ribbon, and who has the shortest?
