# Explanation 39.7 — A product with an even factor

## Explanation

1. The even factor is 2m, so the product is (2m)b = 2(mb).
2. The product is twice an integer, which is the definition of even, whatever value the free factor takes.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 39, 4 steps):

1. Replace a by 2m.
2. ab=2mb.
3. Group as 2×(mb).
4. mb is an integer, so the product is even.
