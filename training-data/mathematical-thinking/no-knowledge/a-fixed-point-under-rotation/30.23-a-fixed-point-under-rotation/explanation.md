# Explanation 30.23 — A fixed point under rotation

## Explanation

1. The figure rotates around point O, and the statement says that the center of rotation does not move.
2. That is the defining property of the center of rotation, so every rotation leaves it where it is.
3. After any rotation point O is again in the same position.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 30, 4 steps):

1. O is the point around which the rotation is performed.
2. The rule declares it fixed.
3. The angle may be 90°, 180°, or something else; that does not matter.
4. O remains in the same position.
