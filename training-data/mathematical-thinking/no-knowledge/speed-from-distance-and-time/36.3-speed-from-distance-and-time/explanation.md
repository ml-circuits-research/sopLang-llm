# Explanation 36.3 — Speed from distance and time

## Explanation

1. A constant rate means the same distance is covered in every minute.
2. Dividing the 18 m traveled by the 3 minutes gives 6 m/min.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 36, 4 steps):

1. Total distance is 18 m.
2. It is divided equally among 3 minutes.
3. 18÷3=6.
4. The rate is 6 m/min.
