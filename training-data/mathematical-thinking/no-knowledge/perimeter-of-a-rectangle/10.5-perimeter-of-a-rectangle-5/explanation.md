# Explanation 10.5 — Perimeter of a Rectangle 5

## Explanation

1. A rectangle has two sides of 10 cm and two sides of 6 cm.
2. The statement defines the perimeter as the sum of the lengths of all sides, so each of the two lengths appears twice.
3. Adding the four sides is the same as 2 · (10 + 6).
4. The perimeter is 32 cm.

Reference solution as printed in the source (chapter 10, 3 steps):

1. Add the four sides: 10+6+10+6.
2. Group equal sides: 2×10+2×6=20+12.
3. The result is 32 cm.
