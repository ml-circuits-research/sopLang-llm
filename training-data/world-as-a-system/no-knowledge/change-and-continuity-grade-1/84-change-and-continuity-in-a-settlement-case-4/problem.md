# 84 — Change and continuity in a settlement: case 4

Knowledge context. History studies both change and continuity. Comparing two snapshots is a systematic way to separate what persisted from what appeared or disappeared.

Given facts. Period 1 features: footpath, market, well, wooden bridge. Period 2 features: market, road, stone bridge.

Rules. A continuity is a feature present in both periods. A disappearance is present only in Period 1. An appearance is present only in Period 2. A replacement can be described when two differently named features serve the same stated role.

Task. Identify the continuities, disappearances, and appearances. Which conclusions are direct set comparisons rather than interpretations?
