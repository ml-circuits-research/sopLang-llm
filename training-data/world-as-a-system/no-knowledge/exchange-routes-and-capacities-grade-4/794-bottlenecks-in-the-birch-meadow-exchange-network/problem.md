# 794 — Bottlenecks in the Birch–Meadow exchange network

Knowledge context. Trade and transport networks have capacities. A single narrow connection can limit a much larger network.

Given facts. Goods move from Birch to Meadow along two branches: Birch→Northfield capacity 10, Northfield→Meadow capacity 6; Birch→Willow capacity 5, Willow→Meadow capacity 8. The final warehouse at Meadow can accept at most 10 units.

Rules. A branch cannot carry more than its smallest edge capacity. Independent branch capacities can be added, but a shared destination capacity can cap the total.

Task. What is the maximum number of units that can reach Meadow in one period? Identify the bottleneck(s).
