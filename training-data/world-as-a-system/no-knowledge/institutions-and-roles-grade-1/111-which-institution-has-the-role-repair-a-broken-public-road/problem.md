# 111 — Which institution has the role? Repair a broken public road

Knowledge context. Civic institutions divide responsibilities. A problem can be solved by matching an action to a clearly defined function.

Given facts. Roles are defined as follows: Town Council makes local policy and approves the budget; Public Works Office maintains roads; Local Court decides legal disputes; Election Committee administers elections; School Board sets school policies. A resident asks which institution should repair a broken public road.

Rules. Match the requested action to the stated role. Do not choose an institution merely because it is powerful or familiar.

Task. Should the request go first to the Public Works Office or the School Board? Why?
