# 431 — Reasoning across generations: case 1

Knowledge context. Genealogies are relational graphs. Generation distance can be counted through parent links.

Given facts. Maya is a parent of Noah. Noah is a parent of Lina. Maya is also a parent of Owen. Owen is a parent of Sara. Cross-domain check: researchers collected 7 reports, but 4 are exact duplicate copies that add no new independent information.

Rules. A parent is one generation above a child. A grandparent is two parent-links above. People who share a parent are siblings under this simplified model. For the cross-domain check, independent reports = total reports − duplicate copies.

Task. What family relation between Maya and Lina follows from the stated links? Cross-domain check: how many non-duplicate reports remain?
