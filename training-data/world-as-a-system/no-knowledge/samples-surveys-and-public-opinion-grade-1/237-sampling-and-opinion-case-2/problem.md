# 237 — Sampling and opinion: case 2

Knowledge context. Surveys use samples to learn about larger populations. The selection process matters as much as the number of answers.

Given facts. A school wants opinions on lunch. It randomly selects 10 students from each grade.

Rules. A sample is more representative when selection does not systematically favor people connected to the answer. Random or broad systematic sampling can reduce selection bias; voluntary or topic-specific locations may create bias.

Task. Is the sampling method likely to be biased for the stated question? Explain the direction of the possible bias.
