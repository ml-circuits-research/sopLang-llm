# 333 — Change and continuity in a settlement: case 3

Knowledge context. History studies both change and continuity. Comparing two snapshots is a systematic way to separate what persisted from what appeared or disappeared.

Given facts. Period 1 features: footpath, market, mill, well, wooden bridge. Period 2 features: market, mill, road, stone bridge, well. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. A continuity is a feature present in both periods. A disappearance is present only in Period 1. An appearance is present only in Period 2. A replacement can be described when two differently named features serve the same stated role. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Identify the continuities, disappearances, and appearances. Which conclusions are direct set comparisons rather than interpretations? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
