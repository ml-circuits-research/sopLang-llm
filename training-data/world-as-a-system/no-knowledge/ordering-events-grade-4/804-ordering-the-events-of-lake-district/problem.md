# 804 — Ordering the events of Lake District

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Flood happened before Founding. Founding happened before Bridge opening. New school happened after Flood. Market charter happened after Bridge opening.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Flood and Bridge opening? Can the exact order of New school and Founding be determined?
