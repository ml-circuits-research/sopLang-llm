# 805 — Ordering the events of Southplain

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. New school happened before Market charter. Market charter happened before Bridge opening. Flood happened after New school. Founding happened after Bridge opening.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of New school and Bridge opening? Can the exact order of Flood and Market charter be determined?
