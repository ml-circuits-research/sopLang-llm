# 593 — Applying a rule with an exception: Arun

Knowledge context. Rules often have conditions and exceptions. Logical reading prevents us from applying a general rule when an overriding exception is active.

Given facts. Arun is 14 years old. A library rule says: a child may enter the archive room if age ≥ 13 AND an adult permit is present. Exception: during an evacuation drill, nobody may enter. Permit present: yes. Evacuation drill: no.

Rules. Check exceptions first if they override the general rule. Otherwise every condition joined by AND must be true.

Task. May Arun enter the archive room under these facts? Show which conditions succeed or fail.
