# 248 — Meta-reasoning: case 3

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Claim: “Every town with more roads has shorter average travel time.” Town X has 6 roads and average travel time 35 minutes. Town Y has 4 roads and average travel time 20 minutes.

Rules. A universal claim is disproved by one case that satisfies its condition but not its conclusion.

Task. Do X and Y provide a counterexample to the claim?
