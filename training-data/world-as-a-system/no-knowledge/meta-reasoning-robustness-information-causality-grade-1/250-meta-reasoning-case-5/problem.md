# 250 — Meta-reasoning: case 5

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Two transport plans are compared. A costs 8, takes 20 minutes, and has safety score 9. B costs 10, takes 25 minutes, and has safety score 9. Lower cost/time and higher safety are better.

Rules. A Pareto-dominates B if A is at least as good on every stated criterion and strictly better on at least one.

Task. Does one plan dominate the other?
