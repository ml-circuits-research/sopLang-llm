# 246 — Meta-reasoning: case 1

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Four equally likely locations A,B,C,D remain possible. Question Q1 asks “Is it A?”; Q2 asks “Is it A or B?” A yes/no question is more balanced when its two answer branches contain more nearly equal numbers of possibilities.

Rules. Use branch sizes as the information criterion in this exercise.

Task. Which question is more balanced and therefore more informative by the stated criterion?
