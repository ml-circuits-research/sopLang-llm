# 496 — Meta-reasoning: case 1

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Four equally likely locations A,B,C,D remain possible. Question Q1 asks “Is it A?”; Q2 asks “Is it A or B?” A yes/no question is more balanced when its two answer branches contain more nearly equal numbers of possibilities. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 3 km and two checkpoints are 8 cm apart.

Rules. Use branch sizes as the information criterion in this exercise. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. Which question is more balanced and therefore more informative by the stated criterion? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
