# 500 — Meta-reasoning: case 5

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Two transport plans are compared. A costs 8, takes 20 minutes, and has safety score 9. B costs 10, takes 25 minutes, and has safety score 9. Lower cost/time and higher safety are better. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 2 km and two checkpoints are 5 cm apart.

Rules. A Pareto-dominates B if A is at least as good on every stated criterion and strictly better on at least one. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. Does one plan dominate the other? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
