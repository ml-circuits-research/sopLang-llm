# 724 — Fair allocation by the rule: minimum guarantee then equal remainder

Knowledge context. Fairness is not one formula. Equal shares, proportional shares, and need-based shares embody different principles.

Given facts. A relief fund has 45 units for three districts A, B, C. Populations are [10, 20, 30]; need scores are [4, 5, 6]. Three fairness principles are defined: equal = same amount each; population-proportional = shares in ratio 10:20:30; need-proportional = shares in ratio 4:5:6.

Rules. Different fairness principles answer different questions. Apply the principle named in the task; do not mix them unless asked to compare.

Task. Give each district a 5-unit minimum guarantee, then divide the remainder equally.
