# 721 — Fair allocation by the rule: equal

Knowledge context. Fairness is not one formula. Equal shares, proportional shares, and need-based shares embody different principles.

Given facts. A relief fund has 45 units for three districts A, B, C. Populations are [10, 20, 30]; need scores are [1, 5, 3]. Three fairness principles are defined: equal = same amount each; population-proportional = shares in ratio 10:20:30; need-proportional = shares in ratio 1:5:3.

Rules. Different fairness principles answer different questions. Apply the principle named in the task; do not mix them unless asked to compare.

Task. Allocate the fund equally.
