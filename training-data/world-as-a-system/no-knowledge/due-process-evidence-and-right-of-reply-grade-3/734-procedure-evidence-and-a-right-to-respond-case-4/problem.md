# 734 — Procedure, evidence, and a right to respond: case 4

Knowledge context. Fair procedures often combine evidentiary standards with an opportunity for the affected person to answer the case.

Given facts. A school rule says a serious penalty requires two independent pieces of evidence and a chance to respond. In this case there are two friends repeating the same rumor from one original speaker, plus a chance to respond. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 13 members, requires at least 7 present for quorum, and 11 are present. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 75 map sheets and receives 5 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. Independence concerns distinct information sources, not merely the number of people repeating one source. Every required procedural condition must be satisfied. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. May the serious penalty be imposed under the stated procedure? Identify the condition that succeeds or fails. Cross-domain check: is quorum met? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
