# 617 — Choosing a public budget package: case 2

Knowledge context. Public budgets require choices under limited resources. A transparent model separates costs, benefits, and mandatory obligations.

Given facts. A town has a budget of 17 units. Projects (cost, benefit): tree planting=(5,7); bridge repair=(6,9); library books=(3,4); drainage=(5,8). Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. Choose a subset whose total cost does not exceed the budget. Maximize total benefit. If a mandatory project is stated, every feasible plan must include it. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Which set of projects gives the greatest total benefit while satisfying every constraint? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
