# 473 — Fair allocation by the rule: proportional to need

Knowledge context. Fairness is not one formula. Equal shares, proportional shares, and need-based shares embody different principles.

Given facts. A relief fund has 40 units for three districts A, B, C. Populations are [10, 20, 30]; need scores are [3, 4, 5]. Three fairness principles are defined: equal = same amount each; population-proportional = shares in ratio 10:20:30; need-proportional = shares in ratio 3:4:5.

Rules. Different fairness principles answer different questions. Apply the principle named in the task; do not mix them unless asked to compare.

Task. Allocate in proportion to need score.
