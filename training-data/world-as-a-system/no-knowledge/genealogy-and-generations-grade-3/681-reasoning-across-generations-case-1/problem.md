# 681 — Reasoning across generations: case 1

Knowledge context. Genealogies are relational graphs. Generation distance can be counted through parent links.

Given facts. Maya is a parent of Noah. Noah is a parent of Lina. Maya is also a parent of Owen. Owen is a parent of Sara. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. A parent is one generation above a child. A grandparent is two parent-links above. People who share a parent are siblings under this simplified model. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. What family relation between Maya and Lina follows from the stated links? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
