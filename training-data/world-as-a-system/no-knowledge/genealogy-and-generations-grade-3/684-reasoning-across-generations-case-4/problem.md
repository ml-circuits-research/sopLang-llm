# 684 — Reasoning across generations: case 4

Knowledge context. Genealogies are relational graphs. Generation distance can be counted through parent links.

Given facts. Maya is a parent of Noah. Noah is a parent of Lina. Maya is also a parent of Owen. Owen is a parent of Sara. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 6 km and two checkpoints are 7 cm apart.

Rules. A parent is one generation above a child. A grandparent is two parent-links above. People who share a parent are siblings under this simplified model. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. What family relation between Maya and Sara follows from the stated links? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
