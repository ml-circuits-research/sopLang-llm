# 502 — Position chain from Juniper to Iris

Knowledge context. A map can be modeled with relative positions. When several moves are given, their effects can be combined rather than memorized as a picture.

Given facts. Meadow is 1 map unit(s) north of Juniper; Iris is 1 map unit(s) north of Meadow. A museum in Elm opens on Saturday.

Rules. Treat east as +x, west as −x, north as +y, and south as −y. Add the moves in order. A fact that does not affect any coordinate is irrelevant.

Task. Where is Iris relative to Juniper? What is the straight grid displacement measured as |x|+|y|? Identify any irrelevant fact.
