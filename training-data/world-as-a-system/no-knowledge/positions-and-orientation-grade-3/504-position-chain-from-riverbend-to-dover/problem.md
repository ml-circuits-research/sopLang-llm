# 504 — Position chain from Riverbend to Dover

Knowledge context. A map can be modeled with relative positions. When several moves are given, their effects can be combined rather than memorized as a picture.

Given facts. Harbor is 1 map unit(s) west of Riverbend; Dover is 1 map unit(s) south of Harbor. A museum in Quartz opens on Saturday.

Rules. Treat east as +x, west as −x, north as +y, and south as −y. Add the moves in order. A fact that does not affect any coordinate is irrelevant.

Task. Where is Dover relative to Riverbend? What is the straight grid displacement measured as |x|+|y|? Identify any irrelevant fact.
