# 503 — Position chain from Northfield to Pine

Knowledge context. A map can be modeled with relative positions. When several moves are given, their effects can be combined rather than memorized as a picture.

Given facts. Dover is 2 map unit(s) east of Northfield; Riverbend is 2 map unit(s) north of Dover; Pine is 1 map unit(s) west of Riverbend. A museum in Oak opens on Saturday.

Rules. Treat east as +x, west as −x, north as +y, and south as −y. Add the moves in order. A fact that does not affect any coordinate is irrelevant.

Task. Where is Pine relative to Northfield? What is the straight grid displacement measured as |x|+|y|? Identify any irrelevant fact.
