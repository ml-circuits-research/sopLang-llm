# 505 — Position chain from Grove to Oak

Knowledge context. A map can be modeled with relative positions. When several moves are given, their effects can be combined rather than memorized as a picture.

Given facts. Fern is 2 map unit(s) north of Grove; Kite is 2 map unit(s) east of Fern; Yarrow is 2 map unit(s) south of Kite; Oak is 1 map unit(s) west of Yarrow. A museum in Yarrow opens on Saturday.

Rules. Treat east as +x, west as −x, north as +y, and south as −y. Add the moves in order. A fact that does not affect any coordinate is irrelevant.

Task. Where is Oak relative to Grove? What is the straight grid displacement measured as |x|+|y|? Identify any irrelevant fact.
