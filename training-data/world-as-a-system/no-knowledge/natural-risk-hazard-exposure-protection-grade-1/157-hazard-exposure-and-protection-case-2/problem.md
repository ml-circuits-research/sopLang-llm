# 157 — Hazard, exposure, and protection: case 2

Knowledge context. Natural risk depends on more than the physical hazard. Exposure and protective measures also affect outcomes.

Given facts. For this classroom model, risk index = hazard × exposure ÷ protection. Data: Site A: hazard=3, exposure=4, protection=1; Site B: hazard=4, exposure=3, protection=2; Site C: hazard=4, exposure=3, protection=1.

Rules. Use the formula exactly as stated. A higher hazard does not necessarily mean higher risk if exposure or protection differs.

Task. Compute the risk index for each site. Which site has the lowest modeled risk?
