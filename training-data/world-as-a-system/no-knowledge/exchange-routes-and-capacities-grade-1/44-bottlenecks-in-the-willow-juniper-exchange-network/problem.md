# 44 — Bottlenecks in the Willow–Juniper exchange network

Knowledge context. Trade and transport networks have capacities. A single narrow connection can limit a much larger network.

Given facts. Goods move from Willow to Juniper along two branches: Willow→Yarrow capacity 7, Yarrow→Juniper capacity 3; Willow→Elm capacity 5, Elm→Juniper capacity 5.

Rules. A branch cannot carry more than its smallest edge capacity. Independent branch capacities can be added, but a shared destination capacity can cap the total.

Task. What is the maximum number of units that can reach Juniper in one period? Identify the bottleneck(s).
