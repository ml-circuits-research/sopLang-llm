# Explanation 627 — Map scale 1:25000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:25000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 25000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 6 cm by 25000 gives 150000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 150000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 1.5 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=6×25000=150000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 150000÷100,000=1.5.
