# Explanation 628 — Map scale 1:50000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:50000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 50000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 7 cm by 50000 gives 350000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 350000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 3.5 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=7×50000=350000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 350000÷100,000=3.5.
