# Explanation 630 — Map scale 1:200000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:200000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 200000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 9 cm by 200000 gives 1800000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 1800000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 18 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=9×200000=1800000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 1800000÷100,000=18.
