# 808 — Duration and centuries: 1724–1766

Knowledge context. Dates can be used as numbers, but historical labels such as centuries follow explicit conventions.

Given facts. An institution was founded in 1724 and reorganized in 1766. Use the rule: years 1–100 are the 1st century, 101–200 the 2nd, and so on. Duration is end year minus start year.

Rules. Apply the century rule mechanically; do not infer a century from the first two digits alone. Compute elapsed time by subtraction.

Task. How many years passed between the two events? In which centuries did 1724 and 1766 fall?
