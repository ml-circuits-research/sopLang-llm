# 400 — Density and capacity in a public space: case 5

Knowledge context. Density compares an amount with the space available. It can reveal crowding even when two places have similar total populations.

Given facts. A park zone covers 8 hectares and currently has 46 visitors. For this exercise, comfortable density is at most 7 visitors per hectare.

Rules. Density = number ÷ area. A zone exceeds comfortable capacity if density is greater than the stated threshold.

Task. What is the visitor density, and is the zone above the stated comfortable capacity?
