# 997 — Meta-reasoning: case 2

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Option A scores between 49 and 53 after measurement error; option B scores between 50 and 52. A ranking is robust only if every allowed value of A remains above every allowed value of B. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. Test worst cases: robust A>B requires min(A)>max(B). For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Is the ranking A>B robust? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
