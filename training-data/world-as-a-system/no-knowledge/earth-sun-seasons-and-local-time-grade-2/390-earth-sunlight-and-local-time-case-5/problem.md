# 390 — Earth, sunlight, and local time: case 5

Knowledge context. Earth–Sun relationships explain broad patterns such as day length and local time, but a problem should state the simplified model it expects children to use.

Given facts. Use this simplified model: Earth’s axis is tilted. In the Northern Hemisphere, days are generally longer around June than around December. Compare June and December for the same place. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 11 members, requires at least 6 present for quorum, and 11 are present.

Rules. Use the stated seasonal relation; do not infer daily weather from season. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. Which of the two months has the longer daylight period in this model, and can that fact alone tell us whether it will rain? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
