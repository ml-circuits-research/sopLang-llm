# 484 — Procedure, evidence, and a right to respond: case 4

Knowledge context. Fair procedures often combine evidentiary standards with an opportunity for the affected person to answer the case.

Given facts. A school rule says a serious penalty requires two independent pieces of evidence and a chance to respond. In this case there are two friends repeating the same rumor from one original speaker, plus a chance to respond. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 6 km and two checkpoints are 3 cm apart.

Rules. Independence concerns distinct information sources, not merely the number of people repeating one source. Every required procedural condition must be satisfied. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. May the serious penalty be imposed under the stated procedure? Identify the condition that succeeds or fails. Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
