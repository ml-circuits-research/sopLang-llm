# 201 — Reasoning with uncertain intervals: case 1

Knowledge context. Measurements and historical dates can be uncertain. An interval represents a range of possibilities rather than one exact number.

Given facts. Two measurements are uncertain intervals. A is between 8 and 12. B is between 11 and 15. A statement “A is definitely greater than B” is valid only if A’s minimum is greater than B’s maximum.

Rules. For a definite comparison under interval uncertainty, test the worst cases. Overlapping intervals usually prevent a strict guaranteed ordering.

Task. Can we conclude that A is definitely greater than B? What comparison is justified?
