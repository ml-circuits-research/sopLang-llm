# 419 — Regeneration and harvest: case 4

Knowledge context. Renewable does not mean unlimited. A resource can decline when use exceeds regeneration.

Given facts. A forest resource starts at 120 units. Each year it regenerates 18 units and people harvest 19 units. Assume these amounts stay constant for 3 years and no other changes occur.

Rules. Annual net change = regeneration − harvest. In this simplified model, use is sustainable over the period if stock does not decline.

Task. What is the stock after 3 years, and is the harvest sustainable under this classroom definition?
