# 530 — Choosing a safe settlement site: case 5

Knowledge context. Human settlements depend on several factors at once. Some conditions may be compulsory while others are preferences.

Given facts. Candidate sites: Site A: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=2; Site B: water=yes, road=no, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=4; Site C: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=yes, nearby farmland score=4; Site D: water=no, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=6. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 16 members, requires at least 9 present for quorum, and 14 are present. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 55 map sheets and receives 1 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. A valid site must have water, road access, and must not be in the flood zone. Among valid sites, choose the one with the highest farmland score. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. Which site should be selected under the stated mandatory conditions and preference rule? Cross-domain check: is quorum met? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
