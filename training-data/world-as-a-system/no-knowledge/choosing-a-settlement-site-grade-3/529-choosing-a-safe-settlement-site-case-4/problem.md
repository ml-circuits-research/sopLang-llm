# 529 — Choosing a safe settlement site: case 4

Knowledge context. Human settlements depend on several factors at once. Some conditions may be compulsory while others are preferences.

Given facts. Candidate sites: Site A: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=3; Site B: water=yes, road=no, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=3; Site C: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=yes, nearby farmland score=5; Site D: water=no, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=5. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. A valid site must have water, road access, and must not be in the flood zone. Among valid sites, choose the one with the highest farmland score. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Which site should be selected under the stated mandatory conditions and preference rule? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
