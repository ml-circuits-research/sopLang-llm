# Explanation 207 — Counterfactual intervention: remove heavy rain

## Explanation

1. The actual facts are a heavy rain and an unclosed gate, which the stated rules turn into a high river, a flood of the square, and a market closure.
2. The intervention "remove heavy rain" sets exactly that one condition and leaves every other rule in place, so the recomputation starts from the actual chain.
3. Without the heavy rain the first rule generates no high river, so neither the flood nor the market closure follows.
4. The answer therefore names the consequences the recomputed model still generates, not the causes that were held fixed.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N17, 3 steps):

1. Intervene: heavy rain=false.
2. Then high river is not generated by the first rule.
3. Without high river, flooding and market closure are not generated.

## Result

**Answer.** Without heavy rain, the model generates neither high river, flood, nor market closure.
