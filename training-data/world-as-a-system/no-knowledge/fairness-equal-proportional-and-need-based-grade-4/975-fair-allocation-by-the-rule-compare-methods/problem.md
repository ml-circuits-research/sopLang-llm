# 975 — Fair allocation by the rule: compare methods

Knowledge context. Fairness is not one formula. Equal shares, proportional shares, and need-based shares embody different principles.

Given facts. A relief fund has 50 units for three districts A, B, C. Populations are [10, 20, 30]; need scores are [5, 6, 7]. Three fairness principles are defined: equal = same amount each; population-proportional = shares in ratio 10:20:30; need-proportional = shares in ratio 5:6:7.

Rules. Different fairness principles answer different questions. Apply the principle named in the task; do not mix them unless asked to compare.

Task. Compare equal allocation with need-proportional allocation: which gives more to the highest-need district?
