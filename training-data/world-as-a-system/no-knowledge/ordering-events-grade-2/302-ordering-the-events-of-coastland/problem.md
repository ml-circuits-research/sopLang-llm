# 302 — Ordering the events of Coastland

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Market charter happened before Bridge opening. Bridge opening happened before Founding. Flood happened after Market charter. New school happened after Founding.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Market charter and Founding? Can the exact order of Flood and Bridge opening be determined?
