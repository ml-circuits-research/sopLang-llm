# 301 — Ordering the events of Eastvale

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Bridge opening happened before Founding. Founding happened before New school. Market charter happened after Bridge opening. Flood happened after New school.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Bridge opening and New school? Can the exact order of Market charter and Founding be determined?
