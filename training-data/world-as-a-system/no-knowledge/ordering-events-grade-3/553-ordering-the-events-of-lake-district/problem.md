# 553 — Ordering the events of Lake District

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Market charter happened before New school. New school happened before Bridge opening. Flood happened after Market charter. Founding happened after Bridge opening.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Market charter and Bridge opening? Can the exact order of Flood and New school be determined?
