# 552 — Ordering the events of Coastland

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Market charter happened before Bridge opening. Bridge opening happened before Founding. Flood happened after Market charter. New school happened after Founding. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 4 km and two checkpoints are 8 cm apart.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. What must be true about the order of Market charter and Founding? Can the exact order of Flood and Bridge opening be determined? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
