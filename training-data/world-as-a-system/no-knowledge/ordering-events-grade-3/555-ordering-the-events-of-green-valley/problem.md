# 555 — Ordering the events of Green Valley

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Flood happened before Market charter. Market charter happened before New school. Bridge opening happened after Flood. Founding happened after New school.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Flood and New school? Can the exact order of Bridge opening and Market charter be determined?
