# 551 — Ordering the events of Westmoor

Knowledge context. Historical chronology often starts from partial information: some events are ordered relative to others even when exact dates are not known.

Given facts. Founding happened before New school. New school happened before Market charter. Flood happened after Founding. Bridge opening happened after Market charter.

Rules. “Before” is transitive: if A is before B and B is before C, then A is before C. If two events are not connected by the facts, their relative order may be undetermined.

Task. What must be true about the order of Founding and Market charter? Can the exact order of Flood and New school be determined?
