# 76 — Courier journey from Willow to Pine

Knowledge context. Historical travel can be analyzed with ordinary distance, speed, and time reasoning when the needed values are supplied.

Given facts. A courier leaves Willow for Pine at 7:00. Distance is 60 km and the stated average speed is 15 km/h. A council meeting in Pine begins at 10.0:00. Ignore stops and use travel time = distance ÷ speed.

Rules. Arrival time equals departure time plus travel time. To attend an event from its beginning, arrival must be no later than the event start.

Task. When does the courier arrive, and can the courier attend the meeting from its beginning?
