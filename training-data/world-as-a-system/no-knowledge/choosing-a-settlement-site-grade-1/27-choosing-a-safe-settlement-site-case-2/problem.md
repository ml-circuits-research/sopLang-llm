# 27 — Choosing a safe settlement site: case 2

Knowledge context. Human settlements depend on several factors at once. Some conditions may be compulsory while others are preferences.

Given facts. Candidate sites: Site A: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=3; Site B: water=yes, road=no, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=3; Site C: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=yes, nearby farmland score=5; Site D: water=no, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=5.

Rules. A valid site must have water, road access, and must not be in the flood zone. Among valid sites, choose the one with the highest farmland score.

Task. Which site should be selected under the stated mandatory conditions and preference rule?
