# 165 — Indirect effects in a food chain: case 5

Knowledge context. Ecosystems contain dependency networks. A change at one point can propagate indirectly through several links.

Given facts. Food relation: rabbit eats grass; fox eats rabbit; eagle eats fox. Classroom rule: if a food source sharply decreases and no alternative food is given, the consumer may also decrease. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. Follow dependency arrows from food to consumer. The rule supports possible directional effects, not exact population numbers. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. If rabbit sharply decreases, which later populations may be indirectly affected according to the rule? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
