# 162 — Indirect effects in a food chain: case 2

Knowledge context. Ecosystems contain dependency networks. A change at one point can propagate indirectly through several links.

Given facts. Food relation: small fish eats algae; large fish eats small fish; heron eats large fish. Classroom rule: if a food source sharply decreases and no alternative food is given, the consumer may also decrease.

Rules. Follow dependency arrows from food to consumer. The rule supports possible directional effects, not exact population numbers.

Task. If small fish sharply decreases, which later populations may be indirectly affected according to the rule?
