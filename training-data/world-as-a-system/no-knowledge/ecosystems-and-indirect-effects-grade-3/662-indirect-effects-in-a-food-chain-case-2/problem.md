# 662 — Indirect effects in a food chain: case 2

Knowledge context. Ecosystems contain dependency networks. A change at one point can propagate indirectly through several links.

Given facts. Food relation: small fish eats algae; large fish eats small fish; heron eats large fish. Classroom rule: if a food source sharply decreases and no alternative food is given, the consumer may also decrease. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 13 members, requires at least 7 present for quorum, and 11 are present.

Rules. Follow dependency arrows from food to consumer. The rule supports possible directional effects, not exact population numbers. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. If small fish sharply decreases, which later populations may be indirectly affected according to the rule? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
