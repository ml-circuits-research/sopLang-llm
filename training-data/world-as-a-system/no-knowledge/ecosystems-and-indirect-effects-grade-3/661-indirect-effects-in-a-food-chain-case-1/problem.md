# 661 — Indirect effects in a food chain: case 1

Knowledge context. Ecosystems contain dependency networks. A change at one point can propagate indirectly through several links.

Given facts. Food relation: rabbit eats grass; fox eats rabbit; eagle eats fox. Classroom rule: if a food source sharply decreases and no alternative food is given, the consumer may also decrease. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 68 map sheets and receives 2 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. Follow dependency arrows from food to consumer. The rule supports possible directional effects, not exact population numbers. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. If rabbit sharply decreases, which later populations may be indirectly affected according to the rule? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
