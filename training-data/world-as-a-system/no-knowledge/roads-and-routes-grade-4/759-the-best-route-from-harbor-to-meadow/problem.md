# 759 — The best route from Harbor to Meadow

Knowledge context. A road map can be modeled as a graph. The best route depends on the stated criterion, not on how short it looks on paper.

Given facts. Harbor–Riverbend–Meadow has segment costs [9, 10] (total 19); Harbor–Iris–Yarrow–Meadow has segment costs [8, 5, 6] (total 19); Harbor–Oak–Meadow has segment costs [12, 7] (total 19). The road Harbor–Iris is closed.

Rules. A route is feasible only if all of its roads are open. The cost of a route is the sum of its segment costs. Choose the feasible route with the smallest total.

Task. Which route should a traveler use from Harbor to Meadow, and why?
