# 749 — Meta-reasoning: case 4

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Across several months, ice-cream sales and sunburn cases rise together. Temperature also rises in those months. No intervention is described. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. Correlation between X and Y does not by itself prove X causes Y; a third variable may influence both. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Can we conclude that ice-cream sales cause sunburn? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
