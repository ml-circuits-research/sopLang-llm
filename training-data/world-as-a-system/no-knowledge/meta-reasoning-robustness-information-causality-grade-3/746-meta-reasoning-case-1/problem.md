# 746 — Meta-reasoning: case 1

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Four equally likely locations A,B,C,D remain possible. Question Q1 asks “Is it A?”; Q2 asks “Is it A or B?” A yes/no question is more balanced when its two answer branches contain more nearly equal numbers of possibilities. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 16 members, requires at least 9 present for quorum, and 14 are present.

Rules. Use branch sizes as the information criterion in this exercise. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. Which question is more balanced and therefore more informative by the stated criterion? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
