# 750 — Meta-reasoning: case 5

Knowledge context. Good reasoning includes checking how informative, robust, falsifiable, causal, or dominated a conclusion is.

Given facts. Two transport plans are compared. A costs 8, takes 20 minutes, and has safety score 9. B costs 10, takes 25 minutes, and has safety score 9. Lower cost/time and higher safety are better. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 11 members, requires at least 6 present for quorum, and 11 are present.

Rules. A Pareto-dominates B if A is at least as good on every stated criterion and strictly better on at least one. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. Does one plan dominate the other? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
