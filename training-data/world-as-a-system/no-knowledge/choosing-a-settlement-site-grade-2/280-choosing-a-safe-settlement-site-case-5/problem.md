# 280 — Choosing a safe settlement site: case 5

Knowledge context. Human settlements depend on several factors at once. Some conditions may be compulsory while others are preferences.

Given facts. Candidate sites: Site A: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=2; Site B: water=yes, road=no, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=4; Site C: water=yes, road=yes, flood-zone=yes, nearby farmland score=4; Site D: water=no, road=yes, flood-zone=no, nearby farmland score=6. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 2 km and two checkpoints are 2 cm apart.

Rules. A valid site must have water, road access, and must not be in the flood zone. Among valid sites, choose the one with the highest farmland score. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. Which site should be selected under the stated mandatory conditions and preference rule? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
