# 489 — Sampling and opinion: case 4

Knowledge context. Surveys use samples to learn about larger populations. The selection process matters as much as the number of answers.

Given facts. A library surveys every 20th visitor entering over a full week. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. A sample is more representative when selection does not systematically favor people connected to the answer. Random or broad systematic sampling can reduce selection bias; voluntary or topic-specific locations may create bias. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Is the sampling method likely to be biased for the stated question? Explain the direction of the possible bias. Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
