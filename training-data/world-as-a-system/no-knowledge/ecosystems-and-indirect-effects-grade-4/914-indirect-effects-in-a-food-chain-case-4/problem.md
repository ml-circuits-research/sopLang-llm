# 914 — Indirect effects in a food chain: case 4

Knowledge context. Ecosystems contain dependency networks. A change at one point can propagate indirectly through several links.

Given facts. Food relation: small fish eats algae; large fish eats small fish; heron eats large fish. Classroom rule: if a food source sharply decreases and no alternative food is given, the consumer may also decrease. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 13 members, requires at least 7 present for quorum, and 11 are present. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 93 map sheets and receives 5 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. Follow dependency arrows from food to consumer. The rule supports possible directional effects, not exact population numbers. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. If small fish sharply decreases, which later populations may be indirectly affected according to the rule? Cross-domain check: is quorum met? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
