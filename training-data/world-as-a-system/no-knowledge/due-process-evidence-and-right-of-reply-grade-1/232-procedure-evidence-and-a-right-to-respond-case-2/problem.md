# 232 — Procedure, evidence, and a right to respond: case 2

Knowledge context. Fair procedures often combine evidentiary standards with an opportunity for the affected person to answer the case.

Given facts. A school rule says a serious penalty requires two independent pieces of evidence and a chance to respond. In this case there are two friends repeating the same rumor from one original speaker, plus a chance to respond.

Rules. Independence concerns distinct information sources, not merely the number of people repeating one source. Every required procedural condition must be satisfied.

Task. May the serious penalty be imposed under the stated procedure? Identify the condition that succeeds or fails.
