# 234 — Procedure, evidence, and a right to respond: case 4

Knowledge context. Fair procedures often combine evidentiary standards with an opportunity for the affected person to answer the case.

Given facts. A school rule says a serious penalty requires two independent pieces of evidence and a chance to respond. In this case there are two friends repeating the same rumor from one original speaker, plus a chance to respond. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 8 members, requires at least 5 present for quorum, and 8 are present.

Rules. Independence concerns distinct information sources, not merely the number of people repeating one source. Every required procedural condition must be satisfied. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. May the serious penalty be imposed under the stated procedure? Identify the condition that succeeds or fails. Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
