# 694 — Provenance and chain of custody: case 4

Knowledge context. Provenance records where an object came from and how it moved between custodians.

Given facts. An artifact record states: found by Excavator on day 1; transferred to Museum A on day 2; sent from Museum A to Research Lab on day 5; returned by Research Lab to Museum B on day 8. Every transfer must name both sender and receiver. The record for day 5 names Research Lab as receiver but does not name the sender. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 9 members, requires at least 5 present for quorum, and 8 are present. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 71 map sheets and receives 5 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. A continuous chain of custody requires each handoff to connect the previous holder to the next. A missing sender or receiver creates a gap that limits certainty about continuous custody. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. Is the stated chain of custody complete under the rule? If not, identify the gap. Cross-domain check: is quorum met? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
