# 691 — Provenance and chain of custody: case 1

Knowledge context. Provenance records where an object came from and how it moved between custodians.

Given facts. An artifact record states: found by Excavator on day 1; transferred to Museum A on day 2; sent from Museum A to Research Lab on day 5; returned by Research Lab to Museum B on day 8. Every transfer must name both sender and receiver. Cross-domain check: researchers collected 7 reports, but 4 are exact duplicate copies that add no new independent information.

Rules. A continuous chain of custody requires each handoff to connect the previous holder to the next. A missing sender or receiver creates a gap that limits certainty about continuous custody. For the cross-domain check, independent reports = total reports − duplicate copies.

Task. Is the stated chain of custody complete under the rule? If not, identify the gap. Cross-domain check: how many non-duplicate reports remain?
