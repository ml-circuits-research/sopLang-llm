# Explanation 380 — Map scale 1:200000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:200000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 200000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 8 cm by 200000 gives 1600000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 1600000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 16 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=8×200000=1600000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 1600000÷100,000=16.
