# Explanation 379 — Map scale 1:100000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:100000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 100000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 7 cm by 100000 gives 700000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 700000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 7 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=7×100000=700000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 700000÷100,000=7.
