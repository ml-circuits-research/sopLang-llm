# Explanation 128 — Map scale 1:50000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:50000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 50000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 5 cm by 50000 gives 250000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 250000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 2.5 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=5×50000=250000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 250000÷100,000=2.5.
