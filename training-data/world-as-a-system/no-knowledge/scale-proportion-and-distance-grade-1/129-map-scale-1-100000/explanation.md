# Explanation 129 — Map scale 1:100000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:100000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 100000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 6 cm by 100000 gives 600000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 600000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 6 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=6×100000=600000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 600000÷100,000=6.
