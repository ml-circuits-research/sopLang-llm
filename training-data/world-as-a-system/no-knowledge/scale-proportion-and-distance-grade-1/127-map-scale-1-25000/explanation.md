# Explanation 127 — Map scale 1:25000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:25000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 25000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 4 cm by 25000 gives 100000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 100000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 1 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=4×25000=100000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 100000÷100,000=1.
