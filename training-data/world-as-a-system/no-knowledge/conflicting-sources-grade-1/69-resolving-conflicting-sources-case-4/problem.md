# 69 — Resolving conflicting sources: case 4

Knowledge context. Historical accounts can disagree. Reasoning requires evaluating how a source knows what it claims, not simply counting statements.

Given facts. Source A, an eyewitness diary, says the bridge opened on Monday. Source B, a later textbook, says Tuesday. Source C, an independent dated toll record, begins charging on Monday. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 12:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. When sources conflict, compare proximity to the event, independence, and whether a source has a reason or mechanism to record the specific fact. Agreement between independent relevant sources increases support but does not create absolute certainty. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Which account is better supported by the evidence given, and why? Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
