# 193 — Provenance and chain of custody: case 3

Knowledge context. Provenance records where an object came from and how it moved between custodians.

Given facts. An artifact record states: found by Excavator on day 1; transferred to Museum A on day 2; sent from Museum A to Research Lab on day 5; returned by Research Lab to Museum B on day 8. Every transfer must name both sender and receiver. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break.

Rules. A continuous chain of custody requires each handoff to connect the previous holder to the next. A missing sender or receiver creates a gap that limits certainty about continuous custody. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration.

Task. Is the stated chain of custody complete under the rule? If not, identify the gap. Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish?
