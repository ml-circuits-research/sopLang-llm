# 862 — Which institution has the role? Decide a dispute under law

Knowledge context. Civic institutions divide responsibilities. A problem can be solved by matching an action to a clearly defined function.

Given facts. Roles are defined as follows: Town Council makes local policy and approves the budget; Public Works Office maintains roads; Local Court decides legal disputes; Election Committee administers elections; School Board sets school policies. A resident asks which institution should decide a dispute under law. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 15 members, requires at least 8 present for quorum, and 14 are present.

Rules. Match the requested action to the stated role. Do not choose an institution merely because it is powerful or familiar. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. Should the request go first to the Local Court or the School Board? Why? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
