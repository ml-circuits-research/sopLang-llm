# 892 — Reservoir stock and flow: case 2

Knowledge context. Many geographic and civic quantities are stocks and flows: water in a reservoir, people in a city, or goods in a warehouse.

Given facts. A reservoir starts with 43 water units. Each day 6 units flow in and 8 units flow out. Assume these rates stay constant for 6 days.

Rules. Stock at the end = starting stock + total inflow − total outflow. Flow is an amount per time period; stock is the amount currently stored.

Task. How much water is stored after 6 days? Is the stock increasing or decreasing each day?
