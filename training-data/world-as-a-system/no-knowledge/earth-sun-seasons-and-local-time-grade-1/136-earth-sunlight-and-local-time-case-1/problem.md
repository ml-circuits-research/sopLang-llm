# 136 — Earth, sunlight, and local time: case 1

Knowledge context. Earth–Sun relationships explain broad patterns such as day length and local time, but a problem should state the simplified model it expects children to use.

Given facts. For this classroom model, Eastport is 1 time zone(s) east of Westport. Each zone east is one hour later. At Westport it is 9:00.

Rules. Moving east adds one hour per stated time zone; moving west subtracts. Use the given model only.

Task. What local time is it in Eastport?
