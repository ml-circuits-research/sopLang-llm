# 139 — Earth, sunlight, and local time: case 4

Knowledge context. Earth–Sun relationships explain broad patterns such as day length and local time, but a problem should state the simplified model it expects children to use.

Given facts. Use this simplified model: Earth’s axis is tilted. In the Northern Hemisphere, days are generally longer around June than around December. Compare June and December for the same place.

Rules. Use the stated seasonal relation; do not infer daily weather from season.

Task. Which of the two months has the longer daylight period in this model, and can that fact alone tell us whether it will rain?
