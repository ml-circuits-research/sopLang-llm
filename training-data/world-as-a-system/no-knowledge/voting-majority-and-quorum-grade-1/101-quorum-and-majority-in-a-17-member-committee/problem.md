# 101 — Quorum and majority in a 17-member committee

Knowledge context. Voting rules often contain more than one threshold. Quorum concerns participation; majority concerns the votes cast according to the stated rule.

Given facts. A committee has 17 members. 17 are present. Votes: yes=8, no=8, abstain=1. Rule: quorum is at least 60% of all members; if quorum exists, a motion passes when yes votes are strictly more than no votes.

Rules. First test quorum using all members as the denominator. Only then compare yes and no; abstentions count for presence but not as no votes under this rule.

Task. Is quorum met, and does the motion pass?
