# 832 — Change and continuity in a settlement: case 2

Knowledge context. History studies both change and continuity. Comparing two snapshots is a systematic way to separate what persisted from what appeared or disappeared.

Given facts. Period 1 features: footpath, market, well, wooden bridge. Period 2 features: market, road, school, stone bridge, well. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 4 km and two checkpoints are 8 cm apart.

Rules. A continuity is a feature present in both periods. A disappearance is present only in Period 1. An appearance is present only in Period 2. A replacement can be described when two differently named features serve the same stated role. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. Identify the continuities, disappearances, and appearances. Which conclusions are direct set comparisons rather than interpretations? Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
