# 441 — Provenance and chain of custody: case 1

Knowledge context. Provenance records where an object came from and how it moved between custodians.

Given facts. An artifact record states: found by Excavator on day 1; transferred to Museum A on day 2; sent from Museum A to Research Lab on day 5; returned by Research Lab to Museum B on day 8. Every transfer must name both sender and receiver. Cross-domain check: a survey team starts work at 8:00 and works for 2 hour(s) without a break. Mixed-domain verification: a regional archive has 46 map sheets and receives 2 additional sheets during the exercise.

Rules. A continuous chain of custody requires each handoff to connect the previous holder to the next. A missing sender or receiver creates a gap that limits certainty about continuous custody. For the cross-domain check, finishing time = starting time + duration. For this verification, the final number of sheets equals starting sheets plus received sheets.

Task. Is the stated chain of custody complete under the rule? If not, identify the gap. Cross-domain check: at what time does the team finish? Mixed-domain verification: how many sheets are available after the delivery?
