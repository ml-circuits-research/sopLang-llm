# Explanation 880 — Map scale 1:200000

## Explanation

1. The scale 1:200000 means that one centimetre on the map represents 200000 centimetres in reality.
2. Multiplying the measured 10 cm by 200000 gives 2000000 real centimetres.
3. Dividing 2000000 cm by 100,000 cm per kilometre gives 20 km, and a scale is a ratio rather than an added distance.

Reference solution as printed in the source (family N1, 2 steps):

1. Real centimetres=10×200000=2000000.
2. Convert to kilometres: 2000000÷100,000=20.
