# 614 — Which institution has the role? Organize the ballot count

Knowledge context. Civic institutions divide responsibilities. A problem can be solved by matching an action to a clearly defined function.

Given facts. Roles are defined as follows: Town Council makes local policy and approves the budget; Public Works Office maintains roads; Local Court decides legal disputes; Election Committee administers elections; School Board sets school policies. A resident asks which institution should organize the ballot count. Cross-domain check: a local committee has 10 members, requires at least 6 present for quorum, and 8 are present.

Rules. Match the requested action to the stated role. Do not choose an institution merely because it is powerful or familiar. For the cross-domain check, quorum exists when present members ≥ the stated threshold.

Task. Should the request go first to the Election Committee or the Public Works Office? Why? Cross-domain check: is quorum met?
