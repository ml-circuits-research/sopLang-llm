# 944 — Provenance and chain of custody: case 4

Knowledge context. Provenance records where an object came from and how it moved between custodians.

Given facts. An artifact record states: found by Excavator on day 1; transferred to Museum A on day 2; sent from Museum A to Research Lab on day 5; returned by Research Lab to Museum B on day 8. Every transfer must name both sender and receiver. The record for day 5 names Research Lab as receiver but does not name the sender. Cross-domain check: on a field sketch, 1 cm represents 6 km and two checkpoints are 8 cm apart.

Rules. A continuous chain of custody requires each handoff to connect the previous holder to the next. A missing sender or receiver creates a gap that limits certainty about continuous custody. For the cross-domain check, real distance = sketch distance × kilometres per centimetre.

Task. Is the stated chain of custody complete under the rule? If not, identify the gap. Cross-domain check: what real distance separates the checkpoints?
