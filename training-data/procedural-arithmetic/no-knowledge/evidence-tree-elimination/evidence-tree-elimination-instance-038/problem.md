# evidence-tree-elimination-38 — Evidence Tree Elimination

Scenario. In a dispatch depot, a failure occurred while handling 11 invoices. H1 - the invoices steps ran in the wrong order; H2 - a measurement error slipped into the invoices count; H3 - the invoices handling had insufficient capacity. The capacity log shows at least 14 units were available throughout. A time-stamped record shows a dependent action occurred before its prerequisite had been completed. An independent record reproduces the measured quantity within the stated tolerance. A separate comment says the shift ran long; this may be true but does not discriminate among the three hypotheses. Which hypothesis is the best-supported explanation?
