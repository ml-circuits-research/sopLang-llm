# evidence-tree-elimination-23 — Evidence Tree Elimination

Scenario. In a warehouse, a failure occurred while handling 16 consignments. H1 - the consignments handling had insufficient capacity; H2 - a measurement error slipped into the consignments count; H3 - the consignments steps ran in the wrong order. The capacity log shows at least 17 units were available throughout. A time-stamped record shows a dependent action occurred before its prerequisite had been completed. An independent record reproduces the measured quantity within the stated tolerance. A separate comment says the shift ran long; this may be true but does not discriminate among the three hypotheses. Which hypothesis is the best-supported explanation?
