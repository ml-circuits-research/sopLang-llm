# 1.3.10 — Mean versus median

Nine observations are: 60, 45, 60, 50, 40, 40, 50, 40, 140. The last value, 140, is genuine and must not be deleted, but it is much larger than the others. The team wants (a) the average amount per observation, with every value included, and (b) a robust description of a 'typical' observation that is less sensitive to the extreme value.

Question. Calculate the mean and the median. Which statistic better serves each purpose, and how can the influence of the extreme value on the mean be shown numerically?
