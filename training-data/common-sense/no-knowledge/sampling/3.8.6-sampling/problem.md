# 3.8.6 — Sampling

The target population contains 4000 people, divided approximately equally among four areas. The team can choose one of three methods: M1: post an open link and keep the first 1200 voluntary responses; M2: randomly select 120 people from each area and track nonresponse separately; M3: interview 2000 people entering one shopping center on a single Saturday. The goal is to estimate an opinion for the whole population, not only for users of one channel or visitors to one location.

Question. Which method provides the best basis for generalization, and why does the larger number of responses in M1 or M3 not remove the selection problem?
