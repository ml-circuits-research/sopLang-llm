# 8.2.9 — Base rates

In a model population of 10000 cases, the target event occurs in 2% of cases. A detector is positive in 90% of cases where the event is present (sensitivity) and correctly negative in 94% of cases where the event is absent (specificity). Treat these rates as exact for this exercise.

Question. If a case is marked positive, what is the probability that the event is actually present? Build the frequencies from the population of 10000 rather than relying on intuition.
