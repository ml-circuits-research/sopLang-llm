# 2.5.1 — Chained yields

A flow begins with 1200 units and passes through three stages. Stage 1 retains 82%, Stage 2 retains 82% of what it receives, and Stage 3 retains 95% of what it receives. The team can increase the retention rate of exactly one stage by 5 percentage points, capped at 100%.

Question. What is the current final output? To maximize final output, which stage should receive the 5-percentage-point improvement? Compare absolute downstream gains rather than only the local percentages.
