# 5.10.5 — Successive percentage changes

A central indicator starts at 1200 operations. First, a favorable change increases it by 20% of the value that exists at that time. Second, a disruption reduces the remaining value by 15%. One colleague proposes applying '+20% − 15%' once to the original value; another says each percentage must use its own current base. The team treats a difference greater than 1 operations between the two methods as materially relevant.

Question. What is the correct final value? Is the direct add/subtract percentage method acceptable under the stated criterion? Show both transformations and explain the base used by each percentage.
