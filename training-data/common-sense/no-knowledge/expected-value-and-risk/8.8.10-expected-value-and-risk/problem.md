# 8.8.10 — Expected value and risk

Without a protective measure, there is a 12% probability of a loss of 4000 CU and otherwise no loss. The protective measure costs 320 CU for certain and, if the adverse event occurs, reduces the loss to 20% of its original amount. A hard risk rule rejects any option whose total loss in the adverse scenario exceeds 1600 CU.

Question. Compute the expected cost of both options and then apply the risk rule separately. Which option is justified by the combination of the two criteria?
