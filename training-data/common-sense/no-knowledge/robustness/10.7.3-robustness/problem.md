# 10.7.3 — Robustness

Three strategies A, B, and C have outcomes (higher is better) in Normal, Difficult, and Severe scenarios: A: 50, 70, 55; B: 70, 85, 75; C: 55, 100, 95. Weighted-average scenario probabilities are 50%, 30%, and 20%. A robustness rule also requires a result of at least 65 in every scenario.

Question. Compute each weighted average, apply the robustness rule, and choose the best strategy among those that remain acceptable. Explain why rejecting a strategy with a high average would not be a contradiction.
