# 6.2.6 — Robustness

Three strategies A, B, and C have outcomes (higher is better) in Normal, Difficult, and Severe scenarios: A: 70, 50, 50; B: 85, 100, 80; C: 100, 60, 60. Weighted-average scenario probabilities are 50%, 30%, and 20%. A robustness rule also requires a result of at least 65 in every scenario.

Question. Compute each weighted average, apply the robustness rule, and choose the best strategy among those that remain acceptable. Explain why rejecting a strategy with a high average would not be a contradiction.
