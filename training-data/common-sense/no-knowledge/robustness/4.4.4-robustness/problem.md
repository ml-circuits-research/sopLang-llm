# 4.4.4 — Robustness

Three strategies A, B, and C have outcomes (higher is better) in Normal, Difficult, and Severe scenarios: A: 90, 80, 95; B: 65, 75, 80; C: 70, 80, 85. Weighted-average scenario probabilities are 50%, 30%, and 20%. A robustness rule also requires a result of at least 60 in every scenario.

Question. Compute each weighted average, apply the robustness rule, and choose the best strategy among those that remain acceptable. Explain why rejecting a strategy with a high average would not be a contradiction.
