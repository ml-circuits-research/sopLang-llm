# 7.9.8 — Budget and constraints

The total budget is 1300 currency units (CU). There is an unavoidable fixed cost of 350 CU. Each implemented unit then costs 40 CU and yields 8 benefit points. At least 18 units are required for a valid plan. An optional add-on module costs 230 CU and adds 110 benefit points regardless of the number of units. Fractional units cannot be purchased.

Question. Compare the best feasible plan without the add-on with the best feasible plan with it. Which plan maximizes benefit without exceeding the budget and while meeting the minimum-unit requirement?
