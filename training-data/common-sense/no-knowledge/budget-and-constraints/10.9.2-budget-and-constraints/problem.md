# 10.9.2 — Budget and constraints

The total budget is 1100 currency units (CU). There is an unavoidable fixed cost of 230 CU. Each implemented unit then costs 50 CU and yields 9 benefit points. At least 13 units are required for a valid plan. An optional add-on module costs 160 CU and adds 120 benefit points regardless of the number of units. Fractional units cannot be purchased.

Question. Compare the best feasible plan without the add-on with the best feasible plan with it. Which plan maximizes benefit without exceeding the budget and while meeting the minimum-unit requirement?
