# 5.4.7 — Budget and constraints

The total budget is 1500 currency units (CU). There is an unavoidable fixed cost of 280 CU. Each implemented unit then costs 45 CU and yields 13 benefit points. At least 14 units are required for a valid plan. An optional add-on module costs 240 CU and adds 60 benefit points regardless of the number of units. Fractional units cannot be purchased.

Question. Compare the best feasible plan without the add-on with the best feasible plan with it. Which plan maximizes benefit without exceeding the budget and while meeting the minimum-unit requirement?
