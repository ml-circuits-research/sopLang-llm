# 8.10.3 — Budget and constraints

The total budget is 900 currency units (CU). There is an unavoidable fixed cost of 230 CU. Each implemented unit then costs 35 CU and yields 12 benefit points. At least 17 units are required for a valid plan. An optional add-on module costs 180 CU and adds 90 benefit points regardless of the number of units. Fractional units cannot be purchased.

Question. Compare the best feasible plan without the add-on with the best feasible plan with it. Which plan maximizes benefit without exceeding the budget and while meeting the minimum-unit requirement?
