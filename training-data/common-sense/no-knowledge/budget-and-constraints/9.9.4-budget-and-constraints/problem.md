# 9.9.4 — Budget and constraints

The total budget is 1100 currency units (CU). There is an unavoidable fixed cost of 230 CU. Each implemented unit then costs 45 CU and yields 10 benefit points. At least 15 units are required for a valid plan. An optional add-on module costs 220 CU and adds 110 benefit points regardless of the number of units. Fractional units cannot be purchased.

Question. Compare the best feasible plan without the add-on with the best feasible plan with it. Which plan maximizes benefit without exceeding the budget and while meeting the minimum-unit requirement?
