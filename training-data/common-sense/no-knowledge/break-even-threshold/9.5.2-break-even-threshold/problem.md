# 9.5.2 — Break-even threshold

Two alternatives have total costs: Plan A = 900 CU fixed + 10 CU per unit; Plan B = 150 CU fixed + 18 CU per unit. There are no other costs. Treat volume as continuous when finding the threshold and as whole units when making a final discrete decision.

Question. At what volume do the two plans have the same cost? Which plan is cheaper below and above the threshold? If volume must be an integer, explain how to use a non-integer threshold.
