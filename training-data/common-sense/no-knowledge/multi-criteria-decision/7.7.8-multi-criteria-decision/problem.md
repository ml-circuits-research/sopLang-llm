# 7.7.8 — Multi-criteria decision

Three options A, B, and C are evaluated on three criteria, each scored from 0 to 10 where 10 is better. Weights are 50% for Criterion 1, 30% for Criterion 2, and 20% for Criterion 3. Scores are: A: 5, 6, 6; B: 6, 6, 7; C: 5, 10, 9. No other mandatory criteria exist in the model.

Question. Compute the weighted score of each option and select the maximum. Then explain why the ranking depends on the weights and should not be presented as a universal truth.
