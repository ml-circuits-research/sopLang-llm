# 2.3.1 — Data consistency

A table reports three disjoint categories: A=170, B=100, and C=80 units. The methodology states that every unit belongs to exactly one of the three categories and that the overall total must equal their sum. The same page prints an overall total of 380 units. A second rule states that A+B must be greater than C.

Question. Is the table internally consistent? Which relationship is violated, by how much does the printed total differ from the calculated total, and what can—and cannot—be inferred about the exact source of the error?
