# 9.5.9 — Integer capacity threshold

A system must cover demand of 820 uses and, under a resilience rule, maintain an additional 25% reserve above estimated demand. Each identical module can supply at most 90 uses. Modules are indivisible, and reserve capacity must exist physically rather than only on average.

Question. What is the minimum number of modules required? First compute total required capacity including reserve, then explain why the ratio must be rounded upward.
