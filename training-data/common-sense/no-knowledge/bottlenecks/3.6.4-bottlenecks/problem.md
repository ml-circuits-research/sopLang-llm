# 3.6.4 — Bottlenecks

A process must pass through four stages in series with maximum capacities A=130, B=90, C=110, and D=100 participants/hour. There is no unlimited intermediate storage, so long-run final throughput cannot exceed the smallest stage capacity. Only stage B can be improved, by 30%; all other capacities remain unchanged.

Question. What is the initial capacity of the whole process, what is the capacity after the improvement, and which stage or stages are the final bottleneck? Explain why the stage capacities are not added.
