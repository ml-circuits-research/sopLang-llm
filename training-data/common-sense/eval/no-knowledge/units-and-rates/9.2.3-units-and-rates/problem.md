# 9.2.3 — Units and rates

A process has a nominal throughput of 48 uses per hour. An overhead equal to 0% of the nominal throughput produces no useful output. The process runs for 45 minutes. Assume the overhead applies uniformly and there are no other losses.

Question. How much useful output is produced? Convert minutes to hours, calculate the useful rate, and use the units to verify that the final expression represents a quantity rather than a rate.
