# 102 — Order and sequence

Problem world. In the model, Earth is approximated as a sphere rotating around its axis. The side facing the Sun is illuminated, while the opposite side is in night. As Earth rotates, a place can move from the illuminated region to the dark region and back again. These problems do not require astronomical distances or real durations; we use only the stated geometric model.

Case data. The stages were written in mixed order: the location enters zone of night / Earth rotates / the location is in zone illuminated / the location reaches toward the edge of the zone illuminated. The model rule is that each stage in the knowledge sheet must prepare the next stage.

Question. Put the stages in logical order. For each transition, explain why the next stage cannot be placed before the previous one.
