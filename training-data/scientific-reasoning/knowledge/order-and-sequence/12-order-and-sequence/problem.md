# 12 — Order and sequence

Problem world. In the small meadow described here, grass is eaten by rabbits, and rabbits can be eaten by foxes. If the food available to one group decreases greatly, that group has fewer resources; the effect can then continue along the chain. We will not assume that animal numbers change instantly: we track only the likely direction of the effect after some time.

Case data. The stages were written in mixed order: the rabbit eats plants / energy from food reaches the fox / plants grow / the fox finds the rabbit. The model rule is that each stage in the knowledge sheet must prepare the next stage.

Question. Put the stages in logical order. For each transition, explain why the next stage cannot be placed before the previous one.
