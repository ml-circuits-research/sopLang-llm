# 42 — Order and sequence

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. The stages were written in mixed order: hands touch the mouth / microbes can enters body / microbes reach the surface / has transferred onto the hands. The model rule is that each stage in the knowledge sheet must prepare the next stage.

Question. Put the stages in logical order. For each transition, explain why the next stage cannot be placed before the previous one.
