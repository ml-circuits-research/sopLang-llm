# 32 — Order and sequence

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. The stages were written in mixed order: air enters the lungs / oxygen passes into the blood / the blood transports oxygen / oxygen reaches the cells. The model rule is that each stage in the knowledge sheet must prepare the next stage.

Question. Put the stages in logical order. For each transition, explain why the next stage cannot be placed before the previous one.
