# 7 — Order and sequence

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. The stages were written in mixed order: water moves up through the stem / the leaf uses water and light / water reaches the leaf / water enters the root. The model rule is that each stage in the knowledge sheet must prepare the next stage.

Question. Put the stages in logical order. For each transition, explain why the next stage cannot be placed before the previous one.
