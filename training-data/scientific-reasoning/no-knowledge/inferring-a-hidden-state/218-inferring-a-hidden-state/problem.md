# 218 — Inferring a hidden state

Problem world. In the model, air occupies space, water flows through open spaces, and soils differ in how quickly they allow water to pass. Soil with different particles and spaces may retain more or less water; this property is measured in the test rather than guessed. To compare two soils, we pour the same amount of water and wait the same amount of time.

Case data. In this model, each sign listed below can be produced only by the causes listed: soil very permeable → water drains quickly, little water remains. soil compacted → water collects at the surface, drainage is slow. Test incorrect → quantities of water different, the results have not comparable. We observe the sign “water collects at the surface”.

Question. Which hidden state remains possible? Note: we may reason from effect to cause only because the problem declares the list of causes complete within this model.
