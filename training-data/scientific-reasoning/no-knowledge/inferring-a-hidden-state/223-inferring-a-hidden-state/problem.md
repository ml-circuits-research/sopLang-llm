# 223 — Inferring a hidden state

Problem world. In the water-cycle model, liquid water can evaporate, water vapor can cool and condense into droplets, and sufficiently large droplets can fall as precipitation. Fallen water can collect in rivers, lakes, or soil and later re-enter the cycle. Clouds are not explained as “water smoke,” but as very small droplets formed after cooling and condensation.

Case data. In this model, each sign listed below can be produced only by the causes listed: evaporation reduced → little water vapor, the water surface is cold. Condensation reduced → the air does not cool enough, much water vapor. Collection blocked → water does not reach the tank, rains. We observe the sign “the air does not cool enough”.

Question. Which hidden state remains possible? Note: we may reason from effect to cause only because the problem declares the list of causes complete within this model.
