# 133 — Inferring a hidden state

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. In this model, each sign listed below can be produced only by the causes listed: dry soil → the leaves wilt, the soil is dry. Blocked stem → the leaves wilt, the soil is moist. Lack of light → the leaves do not produce enough food, the stem still can transport water. We observe the sign “the soil is moist”.

Question. Which hidden state remains possible? Note: we may reason from effect to cause only because the problem declares the list of causes complete within this model.
