# 857 — Counterexample and falsification of a rule

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. The table of observations is: the route A: is short, remains open during rain; the route B: has a small elevation gain; the route C: is short, has a small elevation gain; the route D: has a small elevation gain, remains open during rain; the route E: is short, has a small elevation gain, remains open during rain. A peer states: “any case with the property “is short” also has the property “remains open during rain”.”

Question. Is the universal statement true in the given data? If not, provide a counterexample and explain exactly which part of the statement it refutes.
