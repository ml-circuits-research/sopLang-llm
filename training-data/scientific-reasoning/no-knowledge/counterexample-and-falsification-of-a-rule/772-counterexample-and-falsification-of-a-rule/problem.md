# 772 — Counterexample and falsification of a rule

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. The table of observations is: the animal A: migrates, stores food, has a protected shelter; the animal B: migrates, has a protected shelter; the animal C: stores food; the animal D: migrates; the animal E: stores food, has a protected shelter. A peer states: “Any case with the property “stores food” also has the property “migrates”.”

Question. Is the universal statement true in the given data? If not, provide a counterexample and explain exactly which part of the statement it refutes.
