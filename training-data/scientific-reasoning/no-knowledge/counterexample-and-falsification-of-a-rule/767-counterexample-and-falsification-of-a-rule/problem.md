# 767 — Counterexample and falsification of a rule

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. The table of observations is: the sample A: retains air-filled pores; the sample B: drains quickly, contains humus; the sample C: drains quickly, retains air-filled pores; the sample D: contains humus, retains air-filled pores; the sample E: drains quickly, contains humus, retains air-filled pores. A peer states: “Any case with the property “drains quickly” also has the property “retains air-filled pores”.”

Question. Is the universal statement true in the given data? If not, provide a counterexample and explain exactly which part of the statement it refutes.
