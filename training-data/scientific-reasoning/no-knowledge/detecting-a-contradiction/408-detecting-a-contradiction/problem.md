# 408 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “Case A meets all mandatory conditions, including oxygen reaches the cells.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case A, “oxygen reaches the cells” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
