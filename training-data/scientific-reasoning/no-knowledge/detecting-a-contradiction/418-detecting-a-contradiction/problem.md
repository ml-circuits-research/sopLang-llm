# 418 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “Case A meets all mandatory conditions, including hands do not touch the mouth before washing.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case A, “hands do not touch the mouth before washing” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
