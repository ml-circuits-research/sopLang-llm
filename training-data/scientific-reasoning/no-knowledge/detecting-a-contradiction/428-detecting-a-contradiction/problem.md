# 428 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. Our model mixture contains iron filings, pebbles, sand, and salt dissolved in water. A magnet attracts the iron filings; a sieve separates larger particles from smaller ones; filtration can retain sand while water passes through; evaporating the water can leave the salt behind. A method must be chosen according to the property that differs between the components.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “The pebble case meets all mandatory conditions, including that the component is attracted by a magnet.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case pebbles, “the component is attracted by a magnet” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
