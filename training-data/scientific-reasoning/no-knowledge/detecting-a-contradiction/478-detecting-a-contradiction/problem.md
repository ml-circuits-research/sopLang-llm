# 478 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. In the model, Earth is approximated as a sphere rotating around its axis. The side facing the Sun is illuminated, while the opposite side is in night. As Earth rotates, a place can move from the illuminated region to the dark region and back again. These problems do not require astronomical distances or real durations; we use only the stated geometric model.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “Case A meets all mandatory conditions, including the observer remains at the same place on the surface.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case A, “the observer remains at the same place on the surface” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
