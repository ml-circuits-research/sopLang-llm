# 398 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “Case A meets all mandatory conditions, including has passed through the pupal stage.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case A, “has passed through the pupal stage” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
