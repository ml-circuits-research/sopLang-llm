# 458 — Detecting a contradiction

Problem world. In the model, a magnet can attract certain magnetic materials without direct contact. Two magnet ends can attract or repel depending on orientation. We do not assume that all metals are magnetic; the result of the test is stated explicitly for every object. A thin nonmagnetic barrier does not necessarily stop the magnet’s effect in our model.

Case data. A student writes the conclusion: “Case A meets all mandatory conditions, including the orientation produces attraction.” The problem data includes: In the data, for case A, “the orientation produces attraction” is NO.

Question. Find the exact contradiction. Which statements cannot be true at the same time, and what minimum correction must be made?
