# 169 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: transmission is through hands predicts [microbes on hands, washing reduces cases, surface is reached], while H2: transmission is direct through food predicts [covering of the food reduces cases, the food are exposed, the hands can be clean]. The shared observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
