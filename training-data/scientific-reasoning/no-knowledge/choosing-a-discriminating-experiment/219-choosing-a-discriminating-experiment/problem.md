# 219 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. In the model, air occupies space, water flows through open spaces, and soils differ in how quickly they allow water to pass. Soil with different particles and spaces may retain more or less water; this property is measured in the test rather than guessed. To compare two soils, we pour the same amount of water and wait the same amount of time.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: the soil is compacted predicts [loosening the soil helps, water at the surface, drainage slow], while H2: too much water was poured predicts [loosening the soil is not necessary, larger initial quantity, drainage apparently slow]. The shared observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
