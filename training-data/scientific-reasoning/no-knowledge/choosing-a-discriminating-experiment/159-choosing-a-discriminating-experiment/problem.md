# 159 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: air not reaches sufficient in lungs predicts [little air, low blood oxygen, ventilation helps], while H2: the transfer in blood is problem predicts [normal air, low blood oxygen, ventilation alone does not solve]. The shared observation “low blood oxygen” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
