# 149 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: food of the larvae is insufficient predicts [the larvae increase slowly, many larvae, few pupae], while H2: few eggs are viable predicts [few larvae, few eggs hatches, ratio normal larvae-pupae]. The shared observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
