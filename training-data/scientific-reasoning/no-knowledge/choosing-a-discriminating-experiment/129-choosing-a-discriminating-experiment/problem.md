# 129 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. For the imaginary bean species F in this problem, a seed begins germination only if it has water, air, and a suitable temperature. Light is not required for the first step of germination, but it becomes important after the green shoot appears. In our model, if any required condition is missing, germination stops at that point.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: the problem is a lack of air predicts [the rootlet does not appear, improves after ventilation, humid], while H2: the problem is a temperature that is too low predicts [the rootlet does not appear, improves after warming, humid]. The shared observation “the rootlet does not appear” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
