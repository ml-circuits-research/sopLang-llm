# 134 — Choosing a discriminating experiment

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. We have two explanations: H1: the soil is too dry predicts [wilted leaves, dry soil, watering helps], while H2: the stem does not transport water predicts [wilted leaves, moist soil, watering alone does not help]. The shared observation “wilted leaves” does not distinguish them.

Question. Choose a test that would produce a different observation for the two hypotheses. What should we check, and what result would support each hypothesis?
