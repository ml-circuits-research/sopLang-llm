# 941 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “the opening is closed” prepares “volume is recorded”, which allows “the piston is pushed”, which in turn produces or prepares “the volume decreases”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
