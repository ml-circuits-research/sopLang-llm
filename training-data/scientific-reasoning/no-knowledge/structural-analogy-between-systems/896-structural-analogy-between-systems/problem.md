# 896 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “the day becomes shorter” prepares “the resources decrease”, which allows “the animal starts the strategy”, which in turn produces or prepares “energy is saved or shifted”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
