# 966 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “energy is stored” prepares “the circuit closes”, which allows “energy is transferred”, which in turn produces or prepares “the device transforms it”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
