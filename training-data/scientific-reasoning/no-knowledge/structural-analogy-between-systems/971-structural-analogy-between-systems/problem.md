# 971 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. In the model, the Moon does not produce its own visible light; it reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “light leaves the Sun” prepares “light reaches the Moon”, which allows “the half facing the Sun is illuminated”, which in turn produces or prepares “the Moon reflects light”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
