# 901 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. Bones support the body, joints allow movement between certain bones, and muscles pull on bones when they contract. In the arm model, bending the forearm requires a movable joint, a muscle that contracts, and a bone through which the force is transmitted. Muscles pull; they do not push a bone from a distance. Different joints allow different types of movement.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “the signal reaches muscle” prepares “the muscle contracts”, which allows “the tendon is pulled”, which in turn produces or prepares “the bone rotates at the joint”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
