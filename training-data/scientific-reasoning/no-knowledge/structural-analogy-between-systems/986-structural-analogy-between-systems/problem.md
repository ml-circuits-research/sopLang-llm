# 986 — Structural analogy between systems

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. System 1 has the following functional chain: “temperature is measured” prepares “natural light is observed”, which allows “the devices have been selected”, which in turn produces or prepares “energy is recorded”. System 2, completely artificial, has this structure: the Source sends an object to the Preparation Station; the station sends it through the Channel; at the end, the Collector receives it. The analogy must be based on roles and links, not on appearance.

Question. Build a correspondence between the four roles in System 1 and Source / Preparation Station / Channel / Collector in System 2. What structural property does the analogy preserve?
