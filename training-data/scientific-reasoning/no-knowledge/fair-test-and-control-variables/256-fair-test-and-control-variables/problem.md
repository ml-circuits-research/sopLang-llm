# 256 — Fair test and control variables

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. We want to test the effect of the factor “amount of water received per day”. The group A receives level 10, the group B level 50. For a fair test, the following must be kept constant: the same species, the same light, the same temperature.

Question. Design the correct test and explain why changing another condition at the same time would make the conclusion ambiguous.
