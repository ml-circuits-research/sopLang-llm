# 346 — Fair test and control variables

Problem world. In the water-cycle model, liquid water can evaporate, water vapor can cool and condense into droplets, and sufficiently large droplets can fall as precipitation. Fallen water can collect in rivers, lakes, or soil and later re-enter the cycle. Clouds are not explained as “water smoke,” but as very small droplets formed after cooling and condensation.

Case data. We want to test the effect of the factor “energy received by the water surface”. The group A receives level 1, the group B level 3. For a fair test, the following must be kept constant: the same amount of water, the same surface, the same time.

Question. Design the correct test and explain why changing another condition at the same time would make the conclusion ambiguous.
