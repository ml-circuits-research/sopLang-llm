# 207 — Predicting a change

Problem world. In the model, a magnet can attract certain magnetic materials without direct contact. Two magnet ends can attract or repel depending on orientation. We do not assume that all metals are magnetic; the result of the test is stated explicitly for every object. A thin nonmagnetic barrier does not necessarily stop the magnet’s effect in our model.

Case data. Before the change, the system is stable. Now we activate or increase “short distance”. The causal rules are: short distance → effect magnetic detectable. Orientation of attraction → force toward magnet. Force toward magnet → the object moves. Object magnetic → answer test.

Question. Predict the effects that may appear downstream. Separate the direct effect from the indirect effects.
