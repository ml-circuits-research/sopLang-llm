# 242 — Predicting a change

Problem world. In the teaching model, foods can provide energy and substances needed to build and operate the body. A balanced meal is not decided by a single food but by the combination and amount. For this problem, each option has explicitly stated properties; no real food values need to be memorized. We track three requirements at the same time: enough energy, a source of protein, and a source of fiber.

Case data. Before the change, the system is stable. Now we activate or increase “mass with enough energy”. The causal rules are: mass with enough energy → energy available. Protein in mass → material for increase and repair. Fiber in mass → transit supported in the model. Excess of sugar over limit → Option does not meet Rule.

Question. Predict the effects that may appear downstream. Separate the direct effect from the indirect effects.
