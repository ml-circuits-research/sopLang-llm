# 157 — Predicting a change

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. Before the change, the system is stable. Now we activate or increase “efficient breathing”. The causal rules are: efficient breathing → oxygen in lungs. Oxygen in lungs → oxygen in blood. Oxygen in blood → oxygen transported. Oxygen transported → oxygen at the cells.

Question. Predict the effects that may appear downstream. Separate the direct effect from the indirect effects.
