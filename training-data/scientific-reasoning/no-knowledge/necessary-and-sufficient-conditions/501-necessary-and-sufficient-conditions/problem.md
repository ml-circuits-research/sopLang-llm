# 501 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. In the simplified model, pollination succeeds when compatible pollen reaches a flower’s stigma. A visitor can touch a flower without carrying pollen, and carrying pollen does not help if the correct part of the flower is not touched. Nectar may attract insects, but nectar is not pollen. Some plants are pollinated mainly by animals, others mainly by wind.

Problem data. The target result is “pollination succeeds”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the visitor carries compatible pollen; B=the visitor touches the stigma; C=the flower is open. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition A=“the visitor carries compatible pollen”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
