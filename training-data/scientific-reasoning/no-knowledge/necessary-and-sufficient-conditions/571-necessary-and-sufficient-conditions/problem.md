# 571 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. The target result is “the material dries quickly in the model”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=exposed surface is large; B=air circulates; C=air is not already very moist. Case P has only C=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has C=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition C=“air is not already very moist”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
