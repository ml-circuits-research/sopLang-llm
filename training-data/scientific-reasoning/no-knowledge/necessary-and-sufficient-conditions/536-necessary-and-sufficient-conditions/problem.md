# 536 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. The target result is “restoration follows the expected pattern”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=measurements have been taken at equal intervals; B=the effort has ended; C=the values gradually approach the resting level. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if B is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without B, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
