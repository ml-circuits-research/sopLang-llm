# 601 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. In the greenhouse model, plants need light and water, temperature must remain within a stated range, and fruit formation in the species used requires pollination. Windows can cool and ventilate, watering adds water, and insect access can increase pollination. Too much water can reduce the air in the soil. Ventilation can lower temperature and humidity.

Problem data. The target result is “the plants form healthy fruits in the model”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the water is within the range; B=temperature is within range; C=pollination occurs. Case P has only C=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has C=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition C=“pollination occurs”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
