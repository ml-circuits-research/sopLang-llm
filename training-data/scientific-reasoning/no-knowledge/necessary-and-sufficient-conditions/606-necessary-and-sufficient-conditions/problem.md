# 606 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. The target result is “the team reaches the destination within the model limits”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there is sufficient water; B=the route is open; C=the total time fits within the weather window. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if A is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without A, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
