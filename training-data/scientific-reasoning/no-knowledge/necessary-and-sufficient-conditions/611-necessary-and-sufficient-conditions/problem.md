# 611 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. The target result is “the room reaches a comfortable state with reduced consumption”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=heat losses have been limited; B=there is sufficient light; C=unnecessary devices are switched off. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition B=“there is sufficient light”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
