# 546 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. A lever has a fulcrum, a load, and a place where effort is applied. In the numerical model, for the same load, moving the effort farther from the fulcrum can reduce the force required. The fulcrum is the point around which the lever rotates. The effort arm is the distance between the applied effort and the fulcrum.

Problem data. The target result is “the load can be lifted with the available force”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there is a fulcrum; B=effort is applied on the correct side; C=effort arm is large enough. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if A is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without A, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
