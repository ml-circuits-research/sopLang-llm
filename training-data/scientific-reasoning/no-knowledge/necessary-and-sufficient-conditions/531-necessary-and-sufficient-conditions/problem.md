# 531 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. The target result is “the food is prepared for swallowing”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the piece is cut or torn; B=the piece is broken into smaller pieces; C=the path for swallowing is clear. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition A=“the piece is cut or torn”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
