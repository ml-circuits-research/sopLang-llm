# 521 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. The target result is “the winter strategy succeeds”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there is sufficient energy; B=there is protection from the cold; C=the strategy provides access to resources. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition B=“there is protection from the cold”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
