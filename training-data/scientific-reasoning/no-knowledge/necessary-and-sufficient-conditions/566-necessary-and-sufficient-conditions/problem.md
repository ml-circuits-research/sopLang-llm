# 566 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. The target result is “pressure of the trapped air increases”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=air is trapped; B=the volume available decreases; C=the container has no leak. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if B is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without B, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
