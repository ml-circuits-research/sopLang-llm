# 516 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. The target result is “the roots have good conditions”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the soil retains enough water; B=the soil retains air spaces; C=there is humus. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if A is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without A, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
