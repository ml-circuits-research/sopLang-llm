# 551 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Two gears that touch directly rotate in opposite directions. In the model, if a small gear drives a larger gear, the larger gear makes fewer rotations in the same time; an odd or even number of contacts can change the final direction. The teeth prevent slipping in the ideal model. An intermediate gear can change direction without being the useful output.

Problem data. The target result is “the output has the required direction and speed”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=all required gears are in contact; B=no part is blocked; C=the gear ratio is appropriate. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition B=“no part is blocked”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
