# 506 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Seeds can be dispersed by wind, water, or animals. In the model, a seed reaches a new place only if it leaves the parent plant and remains capable of germinating after transport. Light seeds or seeds with wings can be carried more easily by wind. Some fruits have hooks that attach to fur.

Problem data. The target result is “the seed reaches a new place while still viable”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the seed leaves the parent plant; B=the agent can transport it; C=the seed remains viable. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if B is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without B, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
