# 591 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. The target result is “the device produces the required useful output”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there is a source of energy; B=the transfer path works; C=the suitable converter is connected. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition A=“there is a source of energy”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
