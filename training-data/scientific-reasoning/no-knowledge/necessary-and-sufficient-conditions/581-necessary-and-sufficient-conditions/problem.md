# 581 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. The target result is “the echo is reduced”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there is absorbent material; B=the reflective surface is sufficiently covered; C=the source is tested at the same intensity. Case P has only B=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has B=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition B=“the reflective surface is sufficiently covered”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
