# 511 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. The target result is “decomposition is active”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=there are decomposers; B=there is enough moisture; C=the temperature is suitable. Case P has only C=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has C=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. Analyze the condition C=“the temperature is suitable”. Is it necessary for the result? Is it sufficient on its own? What do cases P, Q, and R show?
