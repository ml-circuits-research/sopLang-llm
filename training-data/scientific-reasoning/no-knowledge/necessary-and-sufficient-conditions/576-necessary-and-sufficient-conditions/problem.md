# 576 — Necessary and sufficient conditions

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. The target result is “the ray reaches the target”. In this model, it occurs exactly when three conditions are met simultaneously: A=the ray meets the mirror; B=the mirror has a suitable orientation; C=the route reflected is not blocked. Case P has only A=YES and the other two conditions NO; Case Q has A=NO and the other two YES; Case R has A=B=C=YES.

Question. A peer state: “if A is true, the result is guaranteed”. Another peer state: “without A, the result is impossible”. Which statement is correct and why?
