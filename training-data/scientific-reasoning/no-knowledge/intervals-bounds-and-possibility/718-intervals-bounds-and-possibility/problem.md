# 718 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “useful energy” is the closed interval [6,10] J-model; each result is known only as interval: A=[9,11]; B=[11,13]; C=[4,5]; D=[6,7].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
