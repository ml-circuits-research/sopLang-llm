# 638 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “remaining mass” is the closed interval [8,12] g; each result is known only as interval: A=[11,13]; B=[13,15]; C=[6,7]; D=[8,9].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
