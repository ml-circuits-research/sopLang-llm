# 648 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “energy reserve” is the closed interval [7,11] energy units; each result is known only as interval: A=[5,6]; B=[7,8]; C=[10,12]; D=[12,14].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
