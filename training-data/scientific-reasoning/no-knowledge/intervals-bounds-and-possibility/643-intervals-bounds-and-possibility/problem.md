# 643 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “time of infiltration” is the closed interval [6,10] s; each result is known only as interval: A=[11,13]; B=[4,5]; C=[6,7]; D=[9,11].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
