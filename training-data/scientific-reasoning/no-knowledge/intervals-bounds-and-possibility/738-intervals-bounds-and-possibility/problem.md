# 738 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “energy consumption” is the closed interval [7,11] units/hour; each result is known only as interval: A=[10,12]; B=[12,14]; C=[5,6]; D=[7,8].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
