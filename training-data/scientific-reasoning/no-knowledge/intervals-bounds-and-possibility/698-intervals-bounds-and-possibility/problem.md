# 698 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “water remaining” is the closed interval [8,12] mL; each result is known only as interval: A=[11,13]; B=[13,15]; C=[6,7]; D=[8,9].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
