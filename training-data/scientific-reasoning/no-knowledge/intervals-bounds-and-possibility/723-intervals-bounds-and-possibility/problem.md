# 723 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. In the model, the Moon does not produce its own visible light; it reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “visible illuminated fraction” is the closed interval [7,11] quarters; each result is known only as interval: A=[12,14]; B=[5,6]; C=[7,8]; D=[10,12].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
