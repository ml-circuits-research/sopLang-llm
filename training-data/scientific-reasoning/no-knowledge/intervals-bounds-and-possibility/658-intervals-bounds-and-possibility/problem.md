# 658 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “average piece size” is the closed interval [6,10] mm; each result is known only as interval: A=[9,11]; B=[11,13]; C=[4,5]; D=[6,7].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
