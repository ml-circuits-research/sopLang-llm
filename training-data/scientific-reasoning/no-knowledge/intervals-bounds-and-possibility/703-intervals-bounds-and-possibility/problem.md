# 703 — Intervals, bounds, and possibility

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. The accepted zone for “route length” is the closed interval [6,10] squares; each result is known only as interval: A=[11,13]; B=[4,5]; C=[6,7]; D=[9,11].

Question. For each sample, say whether it is certainly accepted, certainly rejected, or still uncertain. Then identify which sample would benefit most from a more precise measurement.
