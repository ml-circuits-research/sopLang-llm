# 369 — Propagation of effects through a network

Problem world. In the teaching model, foods can provide energy and substances needed to build and operate the body. A balanced meal is not decided by a single food but by the combination and amount. For this problem, each option has explicitly stated properties; no real food values need to be memorized. We track three requirements at the same time: enough energy, a source of protein, and a source of fiber.

Case data. The network of dependencies has the arrows: mass with enough energy → energy available; protein in mass → material for increase and repair; fiber in mass → transit supported in the model; excess of sugar over limit → Option does not meet Rule. We change “mass with enough energy”.

Question. Mark every node the effect can reach, directly or indirectly. Do not include nodes for which no arrow path exists.
