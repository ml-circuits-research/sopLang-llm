# 252 — Is the evidence sufficient?

Problem world. For the imaginary bean species F in this problem, a seed begins germination only if it has water, air, and a suitable temperature. Light is not required for the first step of germination, but it becomes important after the green shoot appears. In our model, if any required condition is missing, germination stops at that point.

Case data. Two hypotheses remain possible. H1: the problem is a lack of air predicts [the rootlet does not appear, improves after ventilation, humid]. H2: the problem is a temperature that is too low predicts [the rootlet does not appear, improves after warming, humid]. Until now we know only “the rootlet does not appear”, while the problem explicitly states that this information is compatible with both hypotheses.

Question. Is this information sufficient to choose a hypothesis? If not, what evidence would genuinely discriminate between them?
