# 24 — Choice under multiple conditions

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. Option A: has passed through the egg stage: YES; has passed through the larval stage: YES; has passed through the pupal stage: NO; is adult: YES. Option B: has passed through the egg stage: YES; has passed through the larval stage: NO; has passed through the pupal stage: YES; is adult: NO. Option C: has passed through the egg stage: NO; has passed through the larval stage: YES; has passed through the pupal stage: YES; is adult: YES. Option D: has passed through the egg stage: YES; has passed through the larval stage: YES; has passed through the pupal stage: YES; is adult: YES.

Question. Choose the option that simultaneously meets all conditions: has passed through the larval stage, has passed through the pupal stage, has passed through the egg stage, is adult. If even one mandatory condition is NO, the option must be eliminated. Which one remains, and why can the “count the most YES values” method give a wrong answer?
