# 124 — Choice under multiple conditions

Problem world. A measurement is useful only if we know what quantity was measured and in which unit. For comparisons, values must be expressed in the same unit and obtained by the same method. Repeating a measurement can show whether an unusual result is stable or only an accidental error. No conversions need to be memorized in this problem; every required conversion is stated explicitly.

Case data. Option A: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: NO. Option B: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: NO; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES. Option C: the same unit is used: NO; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES. Option D: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES.

Question. Choose the option that simultaneously meets all conditions: the conditions are the same, the instrument is checked, the measurement is repeated, the same unit is used. If even one mandatory condition is NO, the option must be eliminated. Which one remains, and why can the “count the most YES values” method give a wrong answer?
