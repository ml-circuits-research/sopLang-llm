# 49 — Choice under multiple conditions

Problem world. Choosing a material depends on the properties required by the object, such as waterproofness, flexibility, transparency, or thermal insulation. No material is “the best” in every situation; it is suitable if it meets the requirements of its use at the same time. In this problem, the properties are stated explicitly and do not need to be guessed from everyday experience.

Case data. Option A: is waterproof: YES; is flexible: YES; is transparent: NO; provides thermal insulation: YES. Option B: is waterproof: YES; is flexible: NO; is transparent: YES; provides thermal insulation: YES. Option C: is waterproof: NO; is flexible: YES; is transparent: YES; provides thermal insulation: YES. Option D: is waterproof: YES; is flexible: YES; is transparent: YES; provides thermal insulation: YES.

Question. Choose the option that simultaneously meets all conditions: is flexible, is waterproof, is transparent, provides thermal insulation. If even one mandatory condition is NO, the option must be eliminated. Which one remains, and why can the “count the most YES values” method give a wrong answer?
