# 34 — Choice under multiple conditions

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. Option A: air reaches the lungs: YES; oxygen passes into the blood: YES; the blood circulates: YES; oxygen reaches the cells: NO. Option B: air reaches the lungs: YES; oxygen passes into the blood: NO; the blood circulates: YES; oxygen reaches the cells: YES. Option C: air reaches the lungs: NO; oxygen passes into the blood: YES; the blood circulates: YES; oxygen reaches the cells: YES. Option D: air reaches the lungs: YES; oxygen passes into the blood: YES; the blood circulates: YES; oxygen reaches the cells: YES.

Question. Choose the option that simultaneously meets all conditions: air reaches the lungs, oxygen reaches the cells, oxygen passes into the blood, the blood circulates. If even one mandatory condition is NO, the option must be eliminated. Which one remains, and why can the “count the most YES values” method give a wrong answer?
