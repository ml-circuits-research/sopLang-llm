# 607 — Quantifiers: all, some, none

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. The observations for the five cases are: the route A: is short, remains open during rain; the route B: has a small elevation gain; the route C: is short, has a small elevation gain; the route D: has a small elevation gain, remains open during rain; the route E: is short, has a small elevation gain, remains open during rain.

Question. Decide whether each statement is true or false in the table: all cases with the property “is short” also have the property “has a small elevation gain”. / some cases with the property “is short” also have the property “has a small elevation gain”. / no case with the property “has a small elevation gain” does not have the property “remains open during rain”. / some cases with the property “remains open during rain” do not have the property “is short”. For every false statement, give a counterexample; for a true existential statement, give a witness.
