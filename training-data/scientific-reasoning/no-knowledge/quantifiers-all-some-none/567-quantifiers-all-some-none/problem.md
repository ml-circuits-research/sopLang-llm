# 567 — Quantifiers: all, some, none

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. The observations for the five cases are: syringe A: has an opening; the balloon B: keeps air trapped, can change its volume; the container C: keeps air trapped, has an opening; the tube D: can change its volume, has an opening; the piston E: keeps air trapped, can change its volume, has an opening.

Question. Decide whether each statement is true or false in the table: all cases with the property “keeps air trapped” also have the property “can change its volume”. / some cases with the property “keeps air trapped” also have the property “can change its volume”. / no case with the property “can change its volume” does not have the property “has an opening”. / some cases with the property “has an opening” do not have the property “keeps air trapped”. For every false statement, give a counterexample; for a true existential statement, give a witness.
