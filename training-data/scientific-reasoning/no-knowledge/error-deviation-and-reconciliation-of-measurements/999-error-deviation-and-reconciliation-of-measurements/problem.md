# 999 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. In the kitchen model, heating transfers energy, dissolving forms a homogeneous mixture only for certain substances and quantities, and clean utensils reduce contamination. Experimental recipes can be compared only when quantities and times are measured in the same way. A dissolved substance has not disappeared; it is distributed through the solvent. Stirring can speed dissolving without changing the maximum amount allowed by the model.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “temperature in the model” (°C-model): 23, 24, 25, 24, 23, 25. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
