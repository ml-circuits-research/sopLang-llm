# 954 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “route length” (squares): 20, 21, 20, 19, 21, 30. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
