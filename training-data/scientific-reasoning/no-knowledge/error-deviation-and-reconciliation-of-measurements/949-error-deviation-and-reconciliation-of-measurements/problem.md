# 949 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “water remaining” (mL): 23, 24, 25, 24, 23, 25. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
