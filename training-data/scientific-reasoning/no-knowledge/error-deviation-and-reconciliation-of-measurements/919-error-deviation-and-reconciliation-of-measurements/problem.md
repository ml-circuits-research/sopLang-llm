# 919 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. Microorganisms can multiply on food when they find suitable conditions. In the model, cooling slows multiplication, drying reduces available water, and a clean container reduces initial contamination; none of these measures means that a food becomes sterile. Heat and moisture can speed some biological processes. “Cold” means slowing in the model, not absolute stopping.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “index of spoilage” (points): 23, 24, 23, 14, 22, 24. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
