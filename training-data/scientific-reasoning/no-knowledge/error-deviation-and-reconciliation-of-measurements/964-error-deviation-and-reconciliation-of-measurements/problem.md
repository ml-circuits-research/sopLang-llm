# 964 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. In the circuit model, the metals used here are conductors, while plastic and rubber are insulators. Current appears only if there is a continuous conducting path between the source terminals; insulation is used to prevent contact with conducting parts. A conductor allows current to pass in the model. An insulator breaks the electrical path.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “number of correct contacts” (contacts): 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
