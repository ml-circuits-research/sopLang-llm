# 889 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “remaining mass” (g): 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
