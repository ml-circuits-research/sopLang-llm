# 899 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “energy reserve” (energy units): 23, 24, 25, 24, 23, 25. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
