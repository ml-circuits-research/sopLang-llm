# 979 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. In the greenhouse model, plants need light and water, temperature must remain within a stated range, and fruit formation in the species used requires pollination. Windows can cool and ventilate, watering adds water, and insect access can increase pollination. Too much water can reduce the air in the soil. Ventilation can lower temperature and humidity.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “greenhouse state index” (points): 20, 21, 20, 19, 21, 30. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
