# 959 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “echo level” (units): 29, 21, 22, 21, 20, 22. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
