# 914 — Error, deviation, and reconciliation of measurements

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. Repeated measurements for “pulse in the model” (beats/min): 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27. The sheet states that, in a stable system, the repeated measurements tend to form a group with small differences; a series that changes steadily can indicate drift rather than a single bad point.

Question. Analyze the shape of the series: is there an isolated suspicious point, a gradual drift or none? What should you check before changing the data? Give a robust estimate of the center of the series when appropriate.
