# 632 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. Seeds can be dispersed by wind, water, or animals. In the model, a seed reaches a new place only if it leaves the parent plant and remains capable of germinating after transport. Light seeds or seeds with wings can be carried more easily by wind. Some fruits have hooks that attach to fur.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “seeds viable” (unit: seeds). The value starts from 4 seeds and reaches 16 seeds after 4 equal intervals. The model states that the rate is constant.

Question. Determine the net change per interval. Then find the value after one interval and after one additional interval beyond the final measurement.
