# 722 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. In the model, the Moon does not produce its own visible light; it reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “light packets in the model” (unit: packets). The value starts from 4 packets and reaches 24 packets after 4 equal intervals. The model states that the rate is constant.

Question. Determine the net change per interval. Then find the value after one interval and after one additional interval beyond the final measurement.
