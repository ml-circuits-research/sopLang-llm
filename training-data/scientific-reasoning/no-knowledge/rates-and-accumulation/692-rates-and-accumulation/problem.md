# 692 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “air volume” (unit: mL). The value starts from 4 mL and reaches 16 mL after 4 equal intervals. The model states that the rate is constant.

Question. Determine the net change per interval. Then find the value after one interval and after one additional interval beyond the final measurement.
