# 662 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “energy available” (unit: energy units). The value starts from 4 energy units and reaches 24 energy units after 4 equal intervals. The model states that the rate is constant.

Question. Determine the net change per interval. Then find the value after one interval and after one additional interval beyond the final measurement.
