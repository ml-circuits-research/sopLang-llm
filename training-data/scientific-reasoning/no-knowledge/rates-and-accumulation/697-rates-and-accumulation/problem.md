# 697 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “water” (unit: mL). We start with 5 mL; in each equal interval, the net change is +4 mL; we track 5 intervals.

Question. Write the sequence of values after each interval, then calculate the value after two more intervals if the rate remains constant.
