# 717 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “energy” (unit: J-model). We start with 3 J-model; in each equal interval, the net change is +4 J-model; we track 3 intervals.

Question. Write the sequence of values after each interval, then calculate the value after two more intervals if the rate remains constant.
