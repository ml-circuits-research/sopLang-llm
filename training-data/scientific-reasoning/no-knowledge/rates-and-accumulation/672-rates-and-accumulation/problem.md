# 672 — Rates and accumulation

Given knowledge. A lever has a fulcrum, a load, and a place where effort is applied. In the numerical model, for the same load, moving the effort farther from the fulcrum can reduce the force required. The fulcrum is the point around which the lever rotates. The effort arm is the distance between the applied effort and the fulcrum.

Problem data. The quantity tracked is “mechanical work in the model” (unit: work units). The value starts from 3 work units and reaches 12 work units after 3 equal intervals. The model states that the rate is constant.

Question. Determine the net change per interval. Then find the value after one interval and after one additional interval beyond the final measurement.
