# 844 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the battery A: produces light, produces motion, stores energy; the bulb B: produces motion, stores energy; the motor C: produces motion; the wire D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
