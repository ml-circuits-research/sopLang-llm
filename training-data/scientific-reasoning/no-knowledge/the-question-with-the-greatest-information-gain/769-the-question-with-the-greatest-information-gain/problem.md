# 769 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the sample A: contains humus, drains quickly, retains air-filled pores; the sample B: drains quickly, retains air-filled pores; the sample C: retains air-filled pores; the sample D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
