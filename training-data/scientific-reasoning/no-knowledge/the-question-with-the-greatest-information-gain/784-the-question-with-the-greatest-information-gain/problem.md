# 784 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the incisor A: can cut, can crush, removes deposits; the canine B: can cut, removes deposits; the molar C: removes deposits; the toothbrush D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
