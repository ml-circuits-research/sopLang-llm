# 824 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the cloth A: is in an air current, is stretched, starts with the same water; the cloth B: is in an air current, starts with the same water; the towel C: is in an air current; the paper D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
