# 839 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. In the circuit model, the metals used here are conductors, while plastic and rubber are insulators. Current appears only if there is a continuous conducting path between the source terminals; insulation is used to prevent contact with conducting parts. A conductor allows current to pass in the model. An insulator breaks the electrical path.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the wire A: is conductor, is insulated on the outside, can close the path; the clamp B: is insulated on the outside, can close the path; the seed coat C: is insulated on the outside; the switch D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
