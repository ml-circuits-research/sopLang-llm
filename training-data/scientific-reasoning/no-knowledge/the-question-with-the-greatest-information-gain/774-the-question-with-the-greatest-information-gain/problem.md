# 774 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the animal A: has a protected shelter, stores food, migrates; the animal B: has a protected shelter, stores food; the animal C: has a protected shelter; the animal D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
