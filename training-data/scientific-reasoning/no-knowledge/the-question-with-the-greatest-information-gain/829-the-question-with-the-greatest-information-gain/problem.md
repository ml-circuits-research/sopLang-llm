# 829 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: mirror A: is diagonally oriented, reflects toward right, is on the route; mirror B: reflects toward right, is on the route; the screen C: is on the route; the source D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
