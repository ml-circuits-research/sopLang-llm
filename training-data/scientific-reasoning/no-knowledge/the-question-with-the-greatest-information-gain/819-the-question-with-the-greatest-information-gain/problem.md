# 819 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: syringe A: has an opening, can change its volume, keeps air trapped; the balloon B: has an opening, can change its volume; the container C: has an opening; the tube D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
