# 849 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. In the model, the Moon does not produce its own visible light; it reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the position A: is opposite the Sun, is between Earth and Sun, shows the illuminated part; the position B: is opposite the Sun, is between Earth and Sun; the position C: is between Earth and Sun; the position D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
