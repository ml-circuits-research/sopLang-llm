# 864 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: room A: has good insulation, uses natural light, switches off unnecessary loads; room B: uses natural light, switches off unnecessary loads; room C: switches off unnecessary loads; room D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
