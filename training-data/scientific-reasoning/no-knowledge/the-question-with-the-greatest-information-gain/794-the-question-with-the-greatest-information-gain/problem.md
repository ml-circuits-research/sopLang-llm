# 794 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Microorganisms can multiply on food when they find suitable conditions. In the model, cooling slows multiplication, drying reduces available water, and a clean container reduces initial contamination; none of these measures means that a food becomes sterile. Heat and moisture can speed some biological processes. “Cold” means slowing in the model, not absolute stopping.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the sample A: is cooled, is dry, is in a clean container; the sample B: is dry, is in a clean container; the sample C: is dry; the sample D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
