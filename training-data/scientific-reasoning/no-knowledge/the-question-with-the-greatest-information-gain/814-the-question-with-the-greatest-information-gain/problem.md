# 814 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. For equal volumes, the material with smaller mass has lower density. In the model, an object floats in water if its average density is lower than the density of water; a hollow shape can contain air and reduce average density. A compact piece of metal can be denser than water. A metal ship can float because its total volume contains a great deal of air.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the object A: has an air cavity, has low mass for its volume, has a large volume; the object B: has an air cavity, has low mass for its volume; the object C: has low mass for its volume; the object D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
