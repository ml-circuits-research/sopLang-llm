# 799 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. A lever has a fulcrum, a load, and a place where effort is applied. In the numerical model, for the same load, moving the effort farther from the fulcrum can reduce the force required. The fulcrum is the point around which the lever rotates. The effort arm is the distance between the applied effort and the fulcrum.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: lever A: has a long effort arm, has a fixed support, changes the direction of the force; lever B: has a long effort arm, changes the direction of the force; lever C: changes the direction of the force; lever D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
