# 834 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the wall A: absorbs a lot in the model, is porous, is hard; the curtain B: absorbs a lot in the model, is porous; the panel C: absorbs a lot in the model; the floor D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
