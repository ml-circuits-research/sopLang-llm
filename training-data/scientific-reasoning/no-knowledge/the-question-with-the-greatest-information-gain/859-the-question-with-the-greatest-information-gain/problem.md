# 859 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the route A: has a small elevation gain, is short, remains open during rain; the route B: is short, remains open during rain; the route C: remains open during rain; the route D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
