# 804 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Two gears that touch directly rotate in opposite directions. In the model, if a small gear drives a larger gear, the larger gear makes fewer rotations in the same time; an odd or even number of contacts can change the final direction. The teeth prevent slipping in the ideal model. An intermediate gear can change direction without being the useful output.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the gear A: has 10 teeth, is the output gear, is in direct contact with A; the gear B: is the output gear, is in direct contact with A; the gear C: is the output gear; the gear D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
