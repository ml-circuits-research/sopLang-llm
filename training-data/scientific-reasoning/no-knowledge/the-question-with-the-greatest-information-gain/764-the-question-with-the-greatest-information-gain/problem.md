# 764 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the leaf A: is reached by decomposers, is broken into smaller pieces, is moist; the twig B: is reached by decomposers, is broken into smaller pieces; the shell C: is broken into smaller pieces; dry grass D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
