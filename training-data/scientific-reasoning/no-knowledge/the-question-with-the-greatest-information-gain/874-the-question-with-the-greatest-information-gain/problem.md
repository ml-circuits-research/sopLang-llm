# 874 — The question with the greatest information gain

Given knowledge. In the kitchen model, heating transfers energy, dissolving forms a homogeneous mixture only for certain substances and quantities, and clean utensils reduce contamination. Experimental recipes can be compared only when quantities and times are measured in the same way. A dissolved substance has not disappeared; it is distributed through the solvent. Stirring can speed dissolving without changing the maximum amount allowed by the model.

Problem data. An unknown case is one of: the container A: is clean, allows the measurement of the temperature, keeps heat; the container B: is clean, allows the measurement of the temperature; the spoon C: allows the measurement of the temperature; the lid D: none.

Question. You may ask one YES/NO question about one of the three properties. Choose the question that minimizes the maximum number of candidates remaining in the worst case.
