# 661 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each minute of recovery produces exactly 3 units of recovery in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 6 units of recovery.

Question. How many units of recovery do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 18 units of recovery? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
