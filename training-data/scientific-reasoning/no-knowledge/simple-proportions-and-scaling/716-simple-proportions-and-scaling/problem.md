# 716 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each unit of input energy produces exactly 2 useful model energy units in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 useful model energy units.

Question. How many useful model energy units do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 useful model energy units? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
