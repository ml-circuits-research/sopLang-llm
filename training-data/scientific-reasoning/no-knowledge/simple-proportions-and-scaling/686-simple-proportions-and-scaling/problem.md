# 686 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. For equal volumes, the material with smaller mass has lower density. In the model, an object floats in water if its average density is lower than the density of water; a hollow shape can contain air and reduce average density. A compact piece of metal can be denser than water. A metal ship can float because its total volume contains a great deal of air.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each unit of volume added produces exactly 2 model units of density reduction in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 model units of density reduction.

Question. How many model units of density reduction do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 model units of density reduction? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
