# 696 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each minute of drying produces exactly 4 mL of water evaporated in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 8 mL of water evaporated.

Question. How many mL of water evaporated do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 24 mL of water evaporated? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
