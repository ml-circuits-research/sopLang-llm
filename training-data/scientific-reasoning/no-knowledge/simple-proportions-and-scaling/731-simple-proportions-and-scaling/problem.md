# 731 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each route segment produces exactly 2 minutes of progress in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 minutes of progress.

Question. How many minutes of progress do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 minutes of progress? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
