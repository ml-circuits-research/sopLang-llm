# 726 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. In the greenhouse model, plants need light and water, temperature must remain within a stated range, and fruit formation in the species used requires pollination. Windows can cool and ventilate, watering adds water, and insect access can increase pollination. Too much water can reduce the air in the soil. Ventilation can lower temperature and humidity.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each control intervention produces exactly 4 state-improvement points in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 8 state-improvement points.

Question. How many state-improvement points do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 24 state-improvement points? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
