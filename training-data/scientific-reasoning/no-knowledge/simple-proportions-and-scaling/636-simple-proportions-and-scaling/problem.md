# 636 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each portion of compost produces exactly 4 units of material decomposed in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 8 units of material decomposed.

Question. How many units of material decomposed do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 24 units of material decomposed? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
