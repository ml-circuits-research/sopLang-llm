# 736 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each efficiency measure produces exactly 3 units saved in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 6 units saved.

Question. How many units saved do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 18 units saved? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
