# 701 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each correct reflection produces exactly 2 useful route squares in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 useful route squares.

Question. How many useful route squares do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 useful route squares? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
