# 666 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Microorganisms can multiply on food when they find suitable conditions. In the model, cooling slows multiplication, drying reduces available water, and a clean container reduces initial contamination; none of these measures means that a food becomes sterile. Heat and moisture can speed some biological processes. “Cold” means slowing in the model, not absolute stopping.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each preservation measure produces exactly 4 slowing points in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 8 slowing points.

Question. How many slowing points do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 24 slowing points? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
