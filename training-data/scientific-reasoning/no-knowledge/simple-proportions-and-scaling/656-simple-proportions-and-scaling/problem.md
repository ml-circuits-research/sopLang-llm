# 656 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each series of chews produces exactly 2 units broken into smaller pieces in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 units broken into smaller pieces.

Question. How many units broken into smaller pieces do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 units broken into smaller pieces? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
