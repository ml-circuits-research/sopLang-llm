# 646 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each day of preparation produces exactly 3 units of energy saved in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 6 units of energy saved.

Question. How many units of energy saved do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 18 units of energy saved? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
