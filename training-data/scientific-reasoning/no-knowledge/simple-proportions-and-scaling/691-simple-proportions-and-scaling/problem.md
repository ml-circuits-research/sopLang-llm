# 691 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each millimeter of piston movement produces exactly 3 units of compression in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 6 units of compression.

Question. How many units of compression do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 18 units of compression? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
