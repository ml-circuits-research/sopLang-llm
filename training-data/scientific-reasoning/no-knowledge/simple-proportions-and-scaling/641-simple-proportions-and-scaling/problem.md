# 641 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each portion of soil produces exactly 2 mL of water retained in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 4 mL of water retained.

Question. How many mL of water retained do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 12 mL of water retained? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
