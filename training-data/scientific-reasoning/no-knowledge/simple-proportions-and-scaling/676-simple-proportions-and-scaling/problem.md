# 676 — Simple proportions and scaling

Given knowledge. Two gears that touch directly rotate in opposite directions. In the model, if a small gear drives a larger gear, the larger gear makes fewer rotations in the same time; an odd or even number of contacts can change the final direction. The teeth prevent slipping in the ideal model. An intermediate gear can change direction without being the useful output.

Problem data. In the contest’s simplified numerical model, each input rotation produces exactly 3 rotations transmitted in the same interval, and the effects add linearly. With 2 active units, we obtain 6 rotations transmitted.

Question. How many rotations transmitted do we obtain with 5 active units? How many active units are needed for 18 rotations transmitted? Show the scaling factor, not only the product.
