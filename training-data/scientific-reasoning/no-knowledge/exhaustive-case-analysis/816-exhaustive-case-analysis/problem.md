# 816 — Exhaustive case analysis

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. A=air is trapped; B=the volume available decreases; C=the container has no leak. The target result is “pressure of the trapped air increases”. In this submodel, target appears exactly in cases in which exactly two conditions are YES. Each letter can be YES or NO.

Question. Systematically enumerate all 8 A/B/C combinations and identify exactly the favorable cases. Explain why the enumeration is complete.
