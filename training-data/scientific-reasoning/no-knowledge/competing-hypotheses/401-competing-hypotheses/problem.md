# 401 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the simplified model used here, food is broken into smaller pieces in the mouth, reaches the stomach, and then the small intestine, where much of the nutrients pass into the blood. In the large intestine, some water is recovered from the remaining material. The order of the organs matters: a substance cannot reach a later part of the intestine before passing through earlier parts of the route.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: problem is before the intestine → predictions [low absorption, food does not reach the intestine, the stomach receives food]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the problem is intestinal absorption → predictions [low absorption, food reaches the intestine, the preceding digestive path is normal]. In the experiment, we observe “food does not reach the intestine”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
