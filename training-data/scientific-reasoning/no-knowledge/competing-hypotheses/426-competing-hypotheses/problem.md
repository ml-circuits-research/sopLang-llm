# 426 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. Our model mixture contains iron filings, pebbles, sand, and salt dissolved in water. A magnet attracts the iron filings; a sieve separates larger particles from smaller ones; filtration can retain sand while water passes through; evaporating the water can leave the salt behind. A method must be chosen according to the property that differs between the components.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the the liquid is cloudy because of the sand → predictions [the filter clarifies it, the magnet does not change the turbidity, sand on filter]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the the liquid is cloudy because of the fine iron particles → predictions [the filter can retain them as well, the magnet reduces the particles, particles magnetic]. In the experiment, we observe “the filter clarifies it”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
