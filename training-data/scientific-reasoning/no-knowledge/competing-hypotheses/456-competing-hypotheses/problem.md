# 456 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the model, a magnet can attract certain magnetic materials without direct contact. Two magnet ends can attract or repel depending on orientation. We do not assume that all metals are magnetic; the result of the test is stated explicitly for every object. A thin nonmagnetic barrier does not necessarily stop the magnet’s effect in our model.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the object is not magnetic → predictions [near of magnet, the magnet attracts another object, does not moves]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the magnet is too far → predictions [does not move far, object known magnetic, moves then when we bring closer the magnet]. In the experiment, we observe “near of magnet”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
