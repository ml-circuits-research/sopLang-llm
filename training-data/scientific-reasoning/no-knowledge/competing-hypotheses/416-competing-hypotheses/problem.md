# 416 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: transmission is through hands → predictions [microbes on hands, washing reduces cases, surface is reached]. Hypothesis 2: H2: transmission is direct through food → predictions [covering of the food reduces cases, the food are exposed, the hands can be clean]. In the experiment, we observe “microbes on hands”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
