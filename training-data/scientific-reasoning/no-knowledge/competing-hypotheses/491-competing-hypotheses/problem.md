# 491 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the teaching model, foods can provide energy and substances needed to build and operate the body. A balanced meal is not decided by a single food but by the combination and amount. For this problem, each option has explicitly stated properties; no real food values need to be memorized. We track three requirements at the same time: enough energy, a source of protein, and a source of fiber.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the lack of satiety comes from a portion with too little energy → predictions [increasing the portion helps, energy below target, protein may be present]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the lack comes from unsuitable composition → predictions [energy at target, the required component is missing, changing the composition helps]. In the experiment, we observe “increasing the portion helps”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
