# 396 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: food of the larvae is insufficient → predictions [the larvae increase slowly, many larvae, few pupae]. Hypothesis 2: H2: few eggs are viable → predictions [few larvae, few eggs hatches, ratio normal larvae-pupae]. In the experiment, we observe “the larvae increase slowly”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
