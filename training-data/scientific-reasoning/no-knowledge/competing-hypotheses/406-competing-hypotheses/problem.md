# 406 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: air not reaches sufficient in lungs → predictions [little air, low blood oxygen, ventilation helps]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the transfer in blood is problem → predictions [normal air, low blood oxygen, ventilation alone does not solve]. In the experiment, we observe “little air”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
