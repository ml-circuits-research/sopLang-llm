# 381 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the soil is too dry → predictions [wilted leaves, dry soil, watering helps]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the stem does not transport water → predictions [wilted leaves, moist soil, watering alone does not help]. In the experiment, we observe “dry soil”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
