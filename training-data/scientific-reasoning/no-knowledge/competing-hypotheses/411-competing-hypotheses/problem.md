# 411 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In our model, a sensory receptor detects a stimulus, sends a nerve signal, the control center interprets the information, and muscles can produce a response. If the signal does not reach the control center, a response based on that signal cannot be chosen correctly. A fast response is not automatically a correct response; the information must first be detected and transmitted.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the receiver does not detect → predictions [without signal initial, reaction absent, stimulus stronger helps]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the path of signal is blocked → predictions [the center does not receive, signal initial there is, stimulus stronger does not help]. In the experiment, we observe “without signal initial”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
