# 466 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. In the model, air occupies space, water flows through open spaces, and soils differ in how quickly they allow water to pass. Soil with different particles and spaces may retain more or less water; this property is measured in the test rather than guessed. To compare two soils, we pour the same amount of water and wait the same amount of time.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the soil is compacted → predictions [loosening the soil helps, water at the surface, drainage slow]. Hypothesis 2: H2: too much water was poured → predictions [loosening the soil is not necessary, larger initial quantity, drainage apparently slow]. In the experiment, we observe “loosening the soil helps”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
