# 376 — Competing hypotheses

Problem world. For the imaginary bean species F in this problem, a seed begins germination only if it has water, air, and a suitable temperature. Light is not required for the first step of germination, but it becomes important after the green shoot appears. In our model, if any required condition is missing, germination stops at that point.

Case data. Hypothesis 1: H1: the problem is a lack of air → predictions [the rootlet does not appear, improves after ventilation, humid]. Hypothesis 2: H2: the problem is a temperature that is too low → predictions [the rootlet does not appear, improves after warming, humid]. In the experiment, we observe “improves after ventilation”.

Question. Which hypothesis is better supported, and which is eliminated by this observation in the model? State the conclusion without saying more than the data allow.
