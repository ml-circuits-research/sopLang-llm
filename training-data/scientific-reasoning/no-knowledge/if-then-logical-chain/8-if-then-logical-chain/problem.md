# 8 — If-then logical chain

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. The local rules form a single chain: In the model, if “water enters the root” has been completed, then “water moves up through the stem” can begin. In the model, if “water moves up through the stem” has been completed, then “water reaches the leaf” can begin. In the model, if “water reaches the leaf” has been completed, then “the leaf uses water and light” can begin. We know that “water enters the root” has been completed.

Question. What conclusions can we deduce successively? Show why each conclusion becomes a premise for the next rule.
