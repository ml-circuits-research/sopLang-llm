# 550 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. A lever has a fulcrum, a load, and a place where effort is applied. In the numerical model, for the same load, moving the effort farther from the fulcrum can reduce the force required. The fulcrum is the point around which the lever rotates. The effort arm is the distance between the applied effort and the fulcrum.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the fulcrum” is north of “the effort end”; “task” is east of “the fulcrum”; “the middle marker” is south of “task”; “the free end” is northeast of “the fulcrum”.

Question. Where is “the middle marker” relative to “the effort end”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
