# 545 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Microorganisms can multiply on food when they find suitable conditions. In the model, cooling slows multiplication, drying reduces available water, and a clean container reduces initial contamination; none of these measures means that a food becomes sterile. Heat and moisture can speed some biological processes. “Cold” means slowing in the model, not absolute stopping.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the dry shelf” is east of “the model refrigerator”; “the warm mass” is north of “the dry shelf”; “the clean container” is west of “the warm mass”; “the dirty container” is northeast of “the dry shelf”.

Question. Where is “the clean container” relative to “the model refrigerator”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
