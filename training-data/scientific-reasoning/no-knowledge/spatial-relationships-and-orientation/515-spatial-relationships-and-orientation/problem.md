# 515 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “moist zone” is west of “the pile of leaves”; “dry zone” is south of “moist zone”; “the layer of soil” is east of “dry zone”; “the box of compost” is northeast of “moist zone”.

Question. Where is “the layer of soil” relative to “the pile of leaves”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
