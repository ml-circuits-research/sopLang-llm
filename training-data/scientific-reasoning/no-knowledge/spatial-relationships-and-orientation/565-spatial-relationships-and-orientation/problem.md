# 565 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. For equal volumes, the material with smaller mass has lower density. In the model, an object floats in water if its average density is lower than the density of water; a hollow shape can contain air and reduce average density. A compact piece of metal can be denser than water. A metal ship can float because its total volume contains a great deal of air.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “zone of floating” is east of “surface of the water”; “the bottom of the container” is north of “zone of floating”; “the edge of the container” is west of “the bottom of the container”; “the platform of test” is northeast of “zone of floating”.

Question. Where is “the edge of the container” relative to “surface of the water”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
