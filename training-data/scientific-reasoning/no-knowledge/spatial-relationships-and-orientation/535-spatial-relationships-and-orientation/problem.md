# 535 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “canine zone” is west of “incisor zone”; “molar zone” is south of “canine zone”; “the tongue” is east of “molar zone”; “the swallowing zone” is northeast of “canine zone”.

Question. Where is “the tongue” relative to “incisor zone”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
