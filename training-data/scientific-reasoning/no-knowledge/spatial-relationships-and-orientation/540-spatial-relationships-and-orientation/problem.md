# 540 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the effort path” is south of “the starting point”; “the stopping point” is west of “the effort path”; “rest zone” is north of “the stopping point”; “measurement mass” is northeast of “the effort path”.

Question. Where is “rest zone” relative to “the starting point”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
