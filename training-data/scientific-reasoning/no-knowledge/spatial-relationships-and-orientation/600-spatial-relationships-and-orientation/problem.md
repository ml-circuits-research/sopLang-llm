# 600 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. In the model, the Moon does not produce its own visible light; it reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “Earth” is south of “the Sun”; “the eastern position” is west of “Earth”; “the position opposite the Sun” is north of “the eastern position”; “the western position” is northeast of “Earth”.

Question. Where is “the position opposite the Sun” relative to “the Sun”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
