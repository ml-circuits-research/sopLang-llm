# 595 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the cable” is west of “the source”; “the converter” is south of “the cable”; “useful output” is east of “the converter”; “the external environment” is northeast of “the cable”.

Question. Where is “useful output” relative to “the source”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
