# 525 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “feeding area” is east of “the shelter”; “the migration route” is north of “feeding area”; “cold zone” is west of “the migration route”; “the mild area” is northeast of “feeding area”.

Question. Where is “cold zone” relative to “the shelter”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
