# 585 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the nearby wall” is east of “the source”; “the far wall” is north of “the nearby wall”; “the absorbent panel” is west of “the far wall”; “the microphone” is northeast of “the nearby wall”.

Question. Where is “the absorbent panel” relative to “the source”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
