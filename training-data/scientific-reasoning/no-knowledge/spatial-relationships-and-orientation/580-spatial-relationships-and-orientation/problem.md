# 580 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “mirror 1” is south of “the source”; “mirror 2” is west of “mirror 1”; “the screen” is north of “mirror 2”; “the obstacle” is northeast of “mirror 1”.

Question. Where is “the screen” relative to “the source”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
