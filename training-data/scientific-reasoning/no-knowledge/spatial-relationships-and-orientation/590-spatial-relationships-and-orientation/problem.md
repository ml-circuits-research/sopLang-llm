# 590 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. In the circuit model, the metals used here are conductors, while plastic and rubber are insulators. Current appears only if there is a continuous conducting path between the source terminals; insulation is used to prevent contact with conducting parts. A conductor allows current to pass in the model. An insulator breaks the electrical path.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the switch” is north of “the positive terminal”; “the bulb” is east of “the switch”; “the negative terminal” is south of “the bulb”; “the insulated handle” is northeast of “the switch”.

Question. Where is “the negative terminal” relative to “the positive terminal”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
