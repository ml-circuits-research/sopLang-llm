# 570 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the piston” is north of “the air chamber”; “the opening” is east of “the piston”; “the side tube” is south of “the opening”; “the exterior” is northeast of “the piston”.

Question. Where is “the side tube” relative to “the air chamber”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
