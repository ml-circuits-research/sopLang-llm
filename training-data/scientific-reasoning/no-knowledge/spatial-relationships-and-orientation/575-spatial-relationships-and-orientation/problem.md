# 575 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the windy zone” is west of “the shelf shaded”; “the warm zone” is south of “the windy zone”; “humid chamber” is east of “the warm zone”; “the weighing mass” is northeast of “the windy zone”.

Question. Where is “humid chamber” relative to “the shelf shaded”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
