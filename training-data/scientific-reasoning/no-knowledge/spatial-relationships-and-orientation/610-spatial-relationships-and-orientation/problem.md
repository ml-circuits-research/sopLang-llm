# 610 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the bridge” is north of “cabin”; “the step” is east of “the bridge”; “the spring” is south of “the step”; “the point of arrival” is northeast of “the bridge”.

Question. Where is “the spring” relative to “cabin”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
