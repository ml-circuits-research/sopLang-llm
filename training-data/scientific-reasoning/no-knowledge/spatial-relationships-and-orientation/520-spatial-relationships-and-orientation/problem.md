# 520 — Spatial relationships and orientation

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. On a grid map, a step north/south/east/west has the same length. We know: “the layer with humus” is south of “the sandy layer”; “the clayey layer” is west of “the layer with humus”; “the container of test” is north of “the clayey layer”; “the root zone” is northeast of “the layer with humus”.

Question. Where is “the container of test” relative to “the sandy layer”? How many elementary moves are required along the described route between them? Represent the coordinates mentally or symbolically.
