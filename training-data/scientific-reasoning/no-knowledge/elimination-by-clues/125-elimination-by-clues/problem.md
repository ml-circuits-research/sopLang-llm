# 125 — Elimination by clues

Problem world. A measurement is useful only if we know what quantity was measured and in which unit. For comparisons, values must be expressed in the same unit and obtained by the same method. Repeating a measurement can show whether an unusual result is stable or only an accidental error. No conversions need to be memorized in this problem; every required conversion is stated explicitly.

Case data. Case A: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: NO. Case B: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: NO; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES. Case C: the same unit is used: NO; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES. Case D: the same unit is used: YES; the instrument is checked: YES; the measurement is repeated: YES; the conditions are the same: YES.

Question. An unknown case matches the clues: “the same unit is used” is YES; “the instrument is checked” is YES; “the conditions are the same” is YES. Which case is it? Show the elimination of the other possibilities, not just the final answer.
