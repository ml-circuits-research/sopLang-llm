# 40 — Elimination by clues

Problem world. In our model, a sensory receptor detects a stimulus, sends a nerve signal, the control center interprets the information, and muscles can produce a response. If the signal does not reach the control center, a response based on that signal cannot be chosen correctly. A fast response is not automatically a correct response; the information must first be detected and transmitted.

Case data. Case A: the stimulus is detected: YES; the signal is transmitted: YES; the information is interpreted: YES; the muscle can respond: NO. Case B: the stimulus is detected: YES; the signal is transmitted: NO; the information is interpreted: YES; the muscle can respond: YES. Case C: the stimulus is detected: NO; the signal is transmitted: YES; the information is interpreted: YES; the muscle can respond: YES. Case D: the stimulus is detected: YES; the signal is transmitted: YES; the information is interpreted: YES; the muscle can respond: YES.

Question. An unknown case matches the clues: “the stimulus is detected” is YES; “the signal is transmitted” is YES; “the muscle can respond” is YES. Which case is it? Show the elimination of the other possibilities, not just the final answer.
