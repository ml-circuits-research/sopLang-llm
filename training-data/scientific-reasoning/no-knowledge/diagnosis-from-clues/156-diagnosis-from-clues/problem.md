# 156 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: air insufficient in lungs: little oxygen in lungs, little oxygen in blood. Poor lung-to-blood transfer: air reaches the lungs, little oxygen in blood. Circulation weak: reaches the cells with difficulty, oxygen enters the blood. We observe: air reaches the lungs, little oxygen in blood.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
