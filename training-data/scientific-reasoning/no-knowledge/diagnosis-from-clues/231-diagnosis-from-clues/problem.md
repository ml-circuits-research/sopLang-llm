# 231 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In our model, rock fragments can be broken off by weathering, transported by water or wind, and then deposited when the transporting agent loses energy. Repeated deposits can form layers. Traces or remains of organisms can become trapped in sediments and, under certain conditions, become fossils. A fossil does not form instantly; the problem uses only the logical order of the stages, not their real duration.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: transport reduced: fragments there is, remain near of source. Deposition reduced: fragments have been transported, does not accumulates local. Lack of sediments fine: the tracks of the organisms has not covered, the chance of conservation decreases in the model. We observe: fragments have been transported, does not accumulates local.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
