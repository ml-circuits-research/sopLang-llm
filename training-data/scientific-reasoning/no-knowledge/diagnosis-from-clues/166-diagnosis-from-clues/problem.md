# 166 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: unwashed hands: microbes remain on hands, surface was reached. Surface very contaminated: more people have contact, the hands contaminates rapid. Food unprotected: microbes reach direct on food, the hands can be clean. We observe: microbes remain on hands, surface was reached.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
