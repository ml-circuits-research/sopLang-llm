# 131 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In this model, the root absorbs water from the soil, the stem transports it toward the leaves, and the leaves use water, carbon dioxide, and light to make nutrients. If the water pathway through the stem is interrupted, the leaves may receive too little water even when the soil is moist. We will treat a healthy leaf as needing both transported water and light.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: dry soil: the leaves wilt, the soil is dry. Blocked stem: the leaves wilt, the soil is moist. Lack of light: the leaves do not produce enough food, the stem still can transport water. We observe: the leaves wilt, the soil is moist.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
