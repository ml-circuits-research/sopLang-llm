# 221 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the water-cycle model, liquid water can evaporate, water vapor can cool and condense into droplets, and sufficiently large droplets can fall as precipitation. Fallen water can collect in rivers, lakes, or soil and later re-enter the cycle. Clouds are not explained as “water smoke,” but as very small droplets formed after cooling and condensation.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: evaporation reduced: little water vapor, the water surface is cold. Condensation reduced: the air does not cool enough, much water vapor. Collection blocked: water does not reach the tank, rains. We observe: the air does not cool enough, much water vapor.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
