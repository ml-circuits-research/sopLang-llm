# 171 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. Choosing a material depends on the properties required by the object, such as waterproofness, flexibility, transparency, or thermal insulation. No material is “the best” in every situation; it is suitable if it meets the requirements of its use at the same time. In this problem, the properties are stated explicitly and do not need to be guessed from everyday experience.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: material unsuitable for water: water passes, the test of waterproofing fails. Material too rigid: does not bend, the remainder of the properties can be good. Material opaque: light does not pass, can be waterproof. We observe: does not bend, the remainder of the properties can be good.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
