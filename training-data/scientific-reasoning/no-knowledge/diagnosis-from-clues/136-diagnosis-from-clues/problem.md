# 136 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the small meadow described here, grass is eaten by rabbits, and rabbits can be eaten by foxes. If the food available to one group decreases greatly, that group has fewer resources; the effect can then continue along the chain. We will not assume that animal numbers change instantly: we track only the likely direction of the effect after some time.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: lack of grass: grass a low, rabbits find hard food. Increased predation: grass is sufficient, the tracks of fox increase. Lack of water: the animals collects at the few sources of water, food there is. We observe: grass is sufficient, the tracks of fox increase.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
