# 206 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the model, a magnet can attract certain magnetic materials without direct contact. Two magnet ends can attract or repel depending on orientation. We do not assume that all metals are magnetic; the result of the test is stated explicitly for every object. A thin nonmagnetic barrier does not necessarily stop the magnet’s effect in our model.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: object nonmagnetic: the magnet works on another object, does not moves at proximity of the magnet. Distance too large: does not move until the magnet approaches, object magnetic. Orientation of rejection: two magnets moves away, changing the orientation changes the effect. We observe: does not move until the magnet approaches, object magnetic.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
