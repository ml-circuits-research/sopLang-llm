# 226 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In the model, Earth is approximated as a sphere rotating around its axis. The side facing the Sun is illuminated, while the opposite side is in night. As Earth rotates, a place can move from the illuminated region to the dark region and back again. These problems do not require astronomical distances or real durations; we use only the stated geometric model.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: the model does not rotate: the same point remains illuminated, no alternation appears in the test. The source of light is off: the model cannot represent day, no part is illuminated. Observer moved: the comparison no longer tracks the same place, the order of the observations changes. We observe: the same point remains illuminated, no alternation appears in the test.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
