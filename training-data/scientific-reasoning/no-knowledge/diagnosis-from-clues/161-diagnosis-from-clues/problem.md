# 161 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. In our model, a sensory receptor detects a stimulus, sends a nerve signal, the control center interprets the information, and muscles can produce a response. If the signal does not reach the control center, a response based on that signal cannot be chosen correctly. A fast response is not automatically a correct response; the information must first be detected and transmitted.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: stimulus too weak: does not start the signal, the receiver does not detect. Path of signal blocked: the center does not receive the signal, the receiver detects. Muscle blocked: no motion appears, the signal is interpreted. We observe: the center does not receive the signal, the receiver detects.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
