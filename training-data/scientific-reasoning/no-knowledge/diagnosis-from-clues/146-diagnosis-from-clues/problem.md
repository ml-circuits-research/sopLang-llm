# 146 — Diagnosis from clues

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. The possible causes and their signs are: insufficient food for the larva: the larva grows slowly, few pupae. Temperature too low: the order remains the same, all stages last longer. Few eggs viable: the proportion larva-pupa remains normal, few larvae from the beginning. We observe: the larva grows slowly, few pupae.

Question. Which cause is best supported by the observations? Also check why the other explanations do not fit all the data.
