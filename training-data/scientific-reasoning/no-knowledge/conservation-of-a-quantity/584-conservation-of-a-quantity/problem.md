# 584 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “sound energy”, and the total is 24 load units; initial state: “direct”=6, “reflected”=6, “absorbed”=12. Move 3 units from the compartment “direct” to the compartment “reflected”, then 2 units from “reflected” to “absorbed”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
