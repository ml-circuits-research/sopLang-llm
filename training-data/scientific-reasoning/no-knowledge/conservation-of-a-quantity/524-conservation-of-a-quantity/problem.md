# 524 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “energy”, and the total is 24 energy units; initial state: “in stored food”=6, “in body”=6, “consumed”=12. Move 3 units from the compartment “in stored food” to the compartment “in body”, then 2 units from “in body” to “consumed”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
