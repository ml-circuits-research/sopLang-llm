# 574 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “water”, and the total is 30 mL; initial state: “in material”=7, “at the surface”=4, “in air”=19. Move 4 units from the compartment “in material” to the compartment “at the surface”, then 2 units from “at the surface” to “in air”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
