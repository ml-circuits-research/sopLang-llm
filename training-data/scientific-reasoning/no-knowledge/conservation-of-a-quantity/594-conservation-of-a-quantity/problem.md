# 594 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “energy”, and the total is 30 J-model; initial state: “in source”=5, “useful output”=5, “dissipated heat”=20. Move 2 units from the compartment “in source” to the compartment “useful output”, then 2 units from “useful output” to “dissipated heat”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
