# 589 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. In the circuit model, the metals used here are conductors, while plastic and rubber are insulators. Current appears only if there is a continuous conducting path between the source terminals; insulation is used to prevent contact with conducting parts. A conductor allows current to pass in the model. An insulator breaks the electrical path.

Problem data. In a system closed, the quantity tracked is “load in the model”, while the total is 27 load units; after two transfers, “in source”=3 and “along the path”=7. Nothing entered or left the system.

Question. Determine the value lack from the compartment “returned to the source”. Then reconstructs a possible transfer of 4 units between the first two compartments which ar keep the same total.
