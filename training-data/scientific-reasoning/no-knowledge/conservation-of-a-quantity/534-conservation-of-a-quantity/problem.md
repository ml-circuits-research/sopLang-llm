# 534 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “food surface”, and the total is 30 load units; initial state: “large pieces”=5, “small pieces”=5, “food bolus”=20. Move 2 units from the compartment “large pieces” to the compartment “small pieces”, then 2 units from “small pieces” to “food bolus”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
