# 539 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. In a system closed, the quantity tracked is “energy available”, while the total is 33 energy units; after two transfers, “in reserve”=3 and “transferred for effort”=8. Nothing entered or left the system.

Question. Determine the value lack from the compartment “dissipated as heat”. Then reconstructs a possible transfer of 3 units between the first two compartments which ar keep the same total.
