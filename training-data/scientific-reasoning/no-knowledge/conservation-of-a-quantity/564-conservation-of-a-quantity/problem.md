# 564 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. For equal volumes, the material with smaller mass has lower density. In the model, an object floats in water if its average density is lower than the density of water; a hollow shape can contain air and reduce average density. A compact piece of metal can be denser than water. A metal ship can float because its total volume contains a great deal of air.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “trapped air”, and the total is 24 mmodel L; initial state: “in the cavity main”=5, “in the side cavity”=5, “in test chamber”=14. Move 2 units from the compartment “in the cavity main” to the compartment “in the side cavity”, then 2 units from “in the side cavity” to “in test chamber”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
