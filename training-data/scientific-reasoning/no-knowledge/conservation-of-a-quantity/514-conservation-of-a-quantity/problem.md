# 514 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “organic matter”, and the total is 30 mass units; initial state: “large remains”=7, “fragmented remains”=4, “substances in soil”=19. Move 4 units from the compartment “large remains” to the compartment “fragmented remains”, then 2 units from “fragmented remains” to “substances in soil”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
