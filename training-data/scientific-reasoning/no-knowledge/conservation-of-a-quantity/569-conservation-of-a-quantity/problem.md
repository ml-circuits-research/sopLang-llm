# 569 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. In a system closed, the quantity tracked is “air volume”, while the total is 27 mL; after two transfers, “in the syringe”=3 and “in the tube”=8. Nothing entered or left the system.

Question. Determine the value lack from the compartment “moved outside”. Then reconstructs a possible transfer of 3 units between the first two compartments which ar keep the same total.
