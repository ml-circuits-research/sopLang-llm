# 599 — Conservation of a quantity

Half of the Moon is illuminated by the Sun, and the phase seen from Earth depends on the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. Moon phases are not caused by Earth’s shadow; Earth’s shadow matters during a lunar eclipse. The Moon travels in orbit around Earth.

Problem data. In a system closed, the quantity tracked is “light packets in the model”, while the total is 33 packets; after two transfers, “emitted by the model source”=3 and “reached the Moon”=8. Nothing entered or left the system.

Question. Determine the value lack from the compartment “reflected toward the observer”. Then reconstructs a possible transfer of 3 units between the first two compartments which ar keep the same total.
