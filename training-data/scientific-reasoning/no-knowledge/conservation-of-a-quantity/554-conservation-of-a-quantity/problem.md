# 554 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. Two gears that touch directly rotate in opposite directions. In the model, if a small gear drives a larger gear, the larger gear makes fewer rotations in the same time; an odd or even number of contacts can change the final direction. The teeth prevent slipping in the ideal model. An intermediate gear can change direction without being the useful output.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “rotations”, and the total is 30 rotations; initial state: “at input”=6, “in the intermediate gear”=6, “at output”=18. Move 3 units from the compartment “at input” to the compartment “in the intermediate gear”, then 2 units from “in the intermediate gear” to “at output”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
