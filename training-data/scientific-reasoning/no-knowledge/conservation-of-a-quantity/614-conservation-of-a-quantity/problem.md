# 614 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. The system is closed. The tracked quantity is “energy”, and the total is 30 kJ-model; initial state: “in heating”=6, “as useful light”=6, “losses toward outside”=18. Move 3 units from the compartment “in heating” to the compartment “as useful light”, then 2 units from “as useful light” to “losses toward outside”.

Question. Calculate the final state of the three compartments and check by summing that the total has been conserved.
