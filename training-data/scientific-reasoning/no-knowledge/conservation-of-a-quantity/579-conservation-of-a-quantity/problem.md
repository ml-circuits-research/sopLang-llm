# 579 — Conservation of a quantity

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. In a system closed, the quantity tracked is “segments of the light path”, while the total is 33 segments; after two transfers, “before the mirror”=3 and “after first reflection”=6. Nothing entered or left the system.

Question. Determine the value lack from the compartment “after the second reflection”. Then reconstructs a possible transfer of 2 units between the first two compartments which ar keep the same total.
