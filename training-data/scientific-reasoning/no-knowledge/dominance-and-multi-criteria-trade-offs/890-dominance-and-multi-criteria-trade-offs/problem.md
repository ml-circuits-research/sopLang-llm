# 890 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “speed of decomposition”, C2=performance for “moisture retention”, C3=performance for “maintenance effort”. A=(5,2,5); B=(4,4,4); C=(3,3,2); D=(2,5,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
