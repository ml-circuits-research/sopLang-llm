# 910 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “breaking into smaller pieces”, C2=performance for “time necessary”, C3=performance for “protecting tooth enamel”. A=(4,4,4); B=(5,2,4); C=(3,3,3); D=(2,5,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
