# 970 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “useful energy”, C2=performance for “unwanted heat”, C3=performance for “operating time”. A=(3,5,4); B=(5,3,3); C=(2,2,2); D=(4,4,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
