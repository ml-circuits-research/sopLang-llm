# 960 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “reducing of the echo”, C2=performance for “the cost of the material”, C3=performance for “covered space”. A=(4,4,4); B=(5,2,4); C=(3,3,3); D=(2,5,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
