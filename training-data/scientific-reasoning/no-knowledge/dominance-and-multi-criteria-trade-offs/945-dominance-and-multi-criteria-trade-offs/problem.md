# 945 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Air occupies space and can be compressed. In a model of a closed syringe, pushing the plunger decreases the volume of the air and increases pressure; if the air has a path out, pressure does not increase in the same way. Air is matter even though we cannot see it. A balloon can stretch when the pressure inside increases.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “the resulting pressure”, C2=performance for “the effort necessary”, C3=performance for “safety”. A=(3,5,4); B=(5,3,3); C=(2,2,2); D=(4,4,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
