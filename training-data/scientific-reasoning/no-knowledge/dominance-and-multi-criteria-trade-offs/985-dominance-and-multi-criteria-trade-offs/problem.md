# 985 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. In the hiking model, the map shows distances and elevation changes, weather can change speed, and the team has a limited reserve of water and energy. Layered clothing reduces heat loss under the stated cold conditions. Map scale allows map distance to be converted into real distance. Climbing uses more energy in the model than walking on level ground.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “time”, C2=performance for “water consumption”, C3=performance for “weather safety”. A=(4,4,4); B=(5,2,4); C=(3,3,3); D=(2,5,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
