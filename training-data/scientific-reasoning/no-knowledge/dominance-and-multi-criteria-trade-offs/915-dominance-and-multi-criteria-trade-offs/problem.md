# 915 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. During exercise, muscles need more oxygen and energy, and pulse and breathing may increase. after exercise stops, the values tend to return gradually toward the resting level. In the model, recovery is tracked by measurements taken at the same time intervals. Sweating can contribute to water loss. A single measurement does not describe the whole recovery process.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “speed of recovery”, C2=performance for “regularity of the data”, C3=performance for “the effort total”. A=(5,2,5); B=(4,4,4); C=(3,3,2); D=(2,5,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
