# 895 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “water retention”, C2=performance for “ventilation”, C3=performance for “ease of work”. A=(3,5,4); B=(5,3,3); C=(2,2,2); D=(4,4,5).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
