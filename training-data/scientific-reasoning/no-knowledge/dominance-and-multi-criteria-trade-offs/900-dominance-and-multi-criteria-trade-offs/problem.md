# 900 — Dominance and multi-criteria trade-offs

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. Four options receive scores 1–5, where 5 means better performance on the corresponding criterion: C1=performance for “safety”, C2=performance for “energy retained”, C3=performance for “distance traveled”. A=(5,5,3); B=(4,4,2); C=(3,5,5); D=(5,2,4).

Question. An option is dominated if another option is at least as good on every criterion and strictly better on at least one. Eliminate the dominated options and state whether the remaining frontier has a unique winner without additional priorities.
