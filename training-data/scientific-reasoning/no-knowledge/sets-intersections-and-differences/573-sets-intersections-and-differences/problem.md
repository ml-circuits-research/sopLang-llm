# 573 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid. In the model, a larger surface area, moving air, and a higher temperature can speed drying, while very humid air can slow it. Drying does not require water to boil. Spreading out a cloth increases its exposed surface area.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The cloth A: is stretched, is in an air current, starts with the same water; the cloth B: is stretched, starts with the same water; the towel C: is in an air current; the paper D: is stretched; the sponge E: is in an air current, starts with the same water.

Question. Construct A=“is stretched”, B=“is in an air current”, C=“starts with the same water”. Calculate A∩B∩C, B\C and A∪B. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
