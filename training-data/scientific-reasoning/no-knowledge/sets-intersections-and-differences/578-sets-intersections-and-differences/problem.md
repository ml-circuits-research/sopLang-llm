# 578 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. A mirror does not produce light; it changes the direction of light that reaches its surface. In the grid-ray model, the outgoing angle is symmetric with the incoming angle relative to the normal to the mirror. Light travels in straight lines in the uniform medium of the model. A matte surface scatters light in many directions.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. Mirror A: reflects toward right, is diagonally oriented; mirror B: reflects toward right; the screen C: is diagonally oriented, is on the route; the source D: reflects toward right, is diagonally oriented, is on the route; the obstacle E: is on the route.

Question. Construct A=“reflects toward right”, B=“is diagonally oriented”, C=“is on the route”. Calculate A∩B, A∩C and C\A. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
