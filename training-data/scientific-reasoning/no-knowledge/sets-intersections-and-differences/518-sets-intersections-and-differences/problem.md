# 518 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Soil is a mixture that may contain mineral particles, humus, water, and air. In the model, a good layer for roots must retain some water while also leaving air spaces; too much water without air, or drainage that is too fast, can create problems. Sand generally has larger particles than clay. Humus comes from transformed organic remains.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The sample A: retains air-filled pores; the sample B: drains quickly, contains humus; the sample C: drains quickly, retains air-filled pores; the sample D: contains humus, retains air-filled pores; the sample E: drains quickly, contains humus, retains air-filled pores.

Question. Construct A=“drains quickly”, B=“contains humus”, C=“retains air-filled pores”. Calculate A∩B, A∩C and C\A. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
