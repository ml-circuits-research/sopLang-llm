# 523 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Animals can respond to seasons through migration, hibernation, storing food, or changing their coat. In the model, a strategy succeeds if the animal secures energy, protection, and access to resources under the season’s conditions. Migration means seasonal movement between regions. Hibernation greatly reduces activity during certain periods.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The animal A: migrates, stores food, has a protected shelter; the animal B: migrates, has a protected shelter; the animal C: stores food; the animal D: migrates; the animal E: stores food, has a protected shelter.

Question. Construct A=“migrates”, B=“stores food”, C=“has a protected shelter”. Calculate B∩C, A\B and A∪C. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
