# 588 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. In the circuit model, the metals used here are conductors, while plastic and rubber are insulators. Current appears only if there is a continuous conducting path between the source terminals; insulation is used to prevent contact with conducting parts. A conductor allows current to pass in the model. An insulator breaks the electrical path.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The wire A: is conductor; the clamp B: is conductor, is insulated on the outside; the seed coat C: is insulated on the outside; the switch D: is insulated on the outside, can close the path; the bulb E: is conductor, can close the path.

Question. Construct A=“is conductor”, B=“is insulated on the outside”, C=“can close the path”. Calculate A∩B∩C, B\C and A∪B. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
