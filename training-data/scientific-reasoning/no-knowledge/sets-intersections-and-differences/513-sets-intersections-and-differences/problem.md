# 513 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Fungi and bacteria can decompose organic remains. In the model, active decomposition needs decomposers, enough water, and a suitable temperature; the matter in the remains is transformed and some substances return to the soil. Dead leaves do not disappear instantly; decomposition has stages. Strong drying can slow the activity of many decomposers.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The leaf A: is moist; the twig B: is moist, is broken into smaller pieces; the shell C: is broken into smaller pieces; dry grass D: is broken into smaller pieces, is reached by decomposers; the remainder of fruit E: is moist, is reached by decomposers.

Question. Construct A=“is moist”, B=“is broken into smaller pieces”, C=“is reached by decomposers”. Calculate A∩B∩C, B\C and A∪B. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
