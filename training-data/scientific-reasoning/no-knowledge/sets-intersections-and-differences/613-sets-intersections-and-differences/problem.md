# 613 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. Room A: has good insulation; room B: has good insulation, uses natural light; room C: uses natural light; room D: uses natural light, switches off unnecessary loads; room E: has good insulation, switches off unnecessary loads.

Question. Construct A=“has good insulation”, B=“uses natural light”, C=“switches off unnecessary loads”. Calculate B∩C, A\B and A∪C. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
