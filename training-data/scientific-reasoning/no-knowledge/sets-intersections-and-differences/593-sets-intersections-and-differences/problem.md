# 593 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The battery A: produces motion; the bulb B: stores energy, produces light; the motor C: stores energy, produces motion; the wire D: produces light, produces motion; the resistor E: stores energy, produces light, produces motion.

Question. Construct A=“stores energy”, B=“produces light”, C=“produces motion”. Calculate A∩B, A∩C and C\A. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
