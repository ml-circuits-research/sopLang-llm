# 533 — Sets, intersections, and differences

Given knowledge. Incisors cut, canines can tear, and molars crush and grind. Enamel protects the outside of the tooth. In the model, preparing a piece of food well requires cutting or tearing and then grinding before swallowing. Teeth have different shapes for different mechanical roles. Chewing increases the surface area of pieces of food.

Problem data. We use the properties as set labels. The incisor A: can cut, removes deposits; the canine B: can crush; the molar C: can cut, can crush; the toothbrush D: can crush, removes deposits; the piece E: can cut, can crush, removes deposits.

Question. Construct A=“can cut”, B=“can crush”, C=“removes deposits”. Calculate A∩B, A∩C and C\A. Explain for each operation what you keep and what you eliminate.
