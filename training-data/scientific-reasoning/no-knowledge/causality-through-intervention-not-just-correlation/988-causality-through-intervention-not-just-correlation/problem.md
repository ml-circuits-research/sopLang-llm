# 988 — Causality through intervention, not just correlation

Given knowledge. In the home model, insulation slows heat transfer, natural light can reduce the use of electric lights, and electrical devices transform energy and also produce heat. The goal is comfort while using resources as efficiently as possible. Windows can provide light but can also be areas of heat transfer. A thick curtain can reduce some heat loss.

Problem data. In a subexperiment, the factors “has good insulation” and “uses natural light” occur together with an increase in the quantity “energy consumption”. Test A changes only “has good insulation” from present to absent, with all other conditions held constant; the increase disappears. Test B changes only “uses natural light” from present to absent; the increase remains.

Question. Which factor is supported as causal in this submodel? Which factor remains merely associated? Explain why the intervention is more informative than the initial observation.
