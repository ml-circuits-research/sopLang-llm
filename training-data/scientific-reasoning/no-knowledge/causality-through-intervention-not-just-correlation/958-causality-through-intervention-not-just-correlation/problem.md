# 958 — Causality through intervention, not just correlation

Given knowledge. An echo occurs when sound is reflected and returns after a delay. Hard, smooth surfaces can reflect more sound, while soft or porous materials can absorb a larger share. Sound propagates through vibrations in a medium. A greater distance to the wall increases the echo’s return time.

Problem data. In a subexperiment, the factors “is hard” and “is porous” occur together with an increase in the quantity “echo level”. Test A changes only “is hard” from present to absent, with all other conditions held constant; the increase disappears. Test B changes only “is porous” from present to absent; the increase remains.

Question. Which factor is supported as causal in this submodel? Which factor remains merely associated? Explain why the intervention is more informative than the initial observation.
