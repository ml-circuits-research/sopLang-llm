# 968 — Causality through intervention, not just correlation

Given knowledge. Energy can be transferred and transformed. In the model, a battery stores chemical energy, a circuit transfers it electrically, a bulb produces light and heat, and a motor produces motion and heat. A device does not create energy from nothing. Useful energy depends on the purpose of the device.

Problem data. In a subexperiment, the factors “stores energy” and “produces light” occur together with an increase in the quantity “useful energy”. Test A changes only “stores energy” from present to absent, with all other conditions held constant; the increase disappears. Test B changes only “produces light” from present to absent; the increase remains.

Question. Which factor is supported as causal in this submodel? Which factor remains merely associated? Explain why the intervention is more informative than the initial observation.
