# 464 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In a simple-circuit model, a battery, wires, and a bulb must form a closed path for the bulb to light. A break anywhere along the path opens the circuit. Some materials conduct and can bridge a gap; others are insulators. We do not use details about voltage or current; every conclusion follows only from the idea of a continuous path.

Case data. after the observation “bulb off”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: the wire is broken and H2: the switch is open. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
