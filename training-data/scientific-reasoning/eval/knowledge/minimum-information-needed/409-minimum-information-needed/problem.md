# 409 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In the simplified model, inhaled air brings oxygen into the lungs; oxygen passes into the blood, and the blood carries it to the cells. Cells produce carbon dioxide, which is carried by the blood back to the lungs and then exhaled. If one transport link is blocked, stages after that link receive less of the transported substance.

Case data. after the observation “low blood oxygen”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: air not reaches sufficient in lungs and H2: the transfer in blood is problem. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
