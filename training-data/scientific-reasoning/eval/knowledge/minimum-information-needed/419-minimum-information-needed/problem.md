# 419 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In the model, microbes can transfer from a surface to the hands and then from the hands to the mouth if the hands are not cleaned. Correct washing with soap and water greatly reduces the number of microbes on the hands, without meaning that every microbe in the world disappears. To reduce risk, we can break the transmission chain at several points.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: transmission is through hands and H2: transmission is direct through food. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
