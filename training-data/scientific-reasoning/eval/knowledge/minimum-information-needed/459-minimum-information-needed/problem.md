# 459 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In the model, a magnet can attract certain magnetic materials without direct contact. Two magnet ends can attract or repel depending on orientation. We do not assume that all metals are magnetic; the result of the test is stated explicitly for every object. A thin nonmagnetic barrier does not necessarily stop the magnet’s effect in our model.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: the object is not magnetic and H2: the magnet is too far. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
