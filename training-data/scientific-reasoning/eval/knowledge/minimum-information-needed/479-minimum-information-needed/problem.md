# 479 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In the model, Earth is approximated as a sphere rotating around its axis. The side facing the Sun is illuminated, while the opposite side is in night. As Earth rotates, a place can move from the illuminated region to the dark region and back again. These problems do not require astronomical distances or real durations; we use only the stated geometric model.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: alternation comes from rotation of the sphere and H2: alternation comes from turning off the source. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
