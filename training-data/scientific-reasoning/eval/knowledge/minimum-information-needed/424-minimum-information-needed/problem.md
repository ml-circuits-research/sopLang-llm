# 424 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. Choosing a material depends on the properties required by the object, such as waterproofness, flexibility, transparency, or thermal insulation. No material is “the best” in every situation; it is suitable if it meets the requirements of its use at the same time. In this problem, the properties are stated explicitly and do not need to be guessed from everyday experience.

Case data. after the observation “temperature decreases quickly”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: the loss of heat is through material and H2: the loss is through opening. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
