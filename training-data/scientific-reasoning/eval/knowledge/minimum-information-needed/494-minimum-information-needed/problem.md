# 494 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. In the teaching model, foods can provide energy and substances needed to build and operate the body. A balanced meal is not decided by a single food but by the combination and amount. For this problem, each option has explicitly stated properties; no real food values need to be memorized. We track three requirements at the same time: enough energy, a source of protein, and a source of fiber.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: the lack of satiety comes from a portion with too little energy and H2: the lack comes from unsuitable composition. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
