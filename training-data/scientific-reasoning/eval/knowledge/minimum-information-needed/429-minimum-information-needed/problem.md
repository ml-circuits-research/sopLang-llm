# 429 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. Our model mixture contains iron filings, pebbles, sand, and salt dissolved in water. A magnet attracts the iron filings; a sieve separates larger particles from smaller ones; filtration can retain sand while water passes through; evaporating the water can leave the salt behind. A method must be chosen according to the property that differs between the components.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: the the liquid is cloudy because of the sand and H2: the the liquid is cloudy because of the fine iron particles. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
