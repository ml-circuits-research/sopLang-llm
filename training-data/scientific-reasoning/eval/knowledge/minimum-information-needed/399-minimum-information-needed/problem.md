# 399 — Minimum information needed

Problem world. For insect L in this problem, the life cycle has four stages in order: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Only the adult lays eggs, and a larva cannot become an adult without passing through the pupal stage. A temperature in the suitable range may change the rate of development, but it does not change the order of the stages.

Case data. after the observation “the observed problem exists, but its cause has not yet been located”, both hypotheses remain possible: H1: food of the larvae is insufficient and H2: few eggs are viable. We can make one additional observation.

Question. What is the minimum information that would separate the hypotheses? Explain why a shared observation does not help, while a discriminating one does.
