# 4.4.3 — Normalization and Capacity

Scenario. In planning an optics demonstration, the worksheet lists a raw requirement of 102 optical elements in a local counting unit. One local unit corresponds to 0.75 standard units. Operational losses are estimated at 8%, so enough input must be available to deliver the final requirement after loss. Each container or service slot can supply 25 standard units, and at most 8 are available. Another note gives the color coding of the containers, which is irrelevant to capacity.

Main question. How many containers or slots are required, and is the plan feasible? Best decomposition (4 subproblems).
