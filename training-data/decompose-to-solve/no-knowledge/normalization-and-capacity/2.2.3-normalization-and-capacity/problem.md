# 2.2.3 — Normalization and Capacity

Scenario. In sequencing a small renovation project, the worksheet lists a raw requirement of 98 construction tasks in a local counting unit. One local unit corresponds to 2 standard units. Operational losses are estimated at 12%, so enough input must be available to deliver the final requirement after loss. Each container or service slot can supply 28 standard units, and at most 7 are available. Another note gives the color coding of the containers, which is irrelevant to capacity.

Main question. How many containers or slots are required, and is the plan feasible? Best decomposition (4 subproblems).
