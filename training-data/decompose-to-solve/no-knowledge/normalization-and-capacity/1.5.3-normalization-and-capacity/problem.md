# 1.5.3 — Normalization and Capacity

Scenario. In choosing between service plans with different constraints, the worksheet lists a raw requirement of 113 service units in a local counting unit. One local unit corresponds to 0.75 standard units. Operational losses are estimated at 15%, so enough input must be available to deliver the final requirement after loss. Each container or service slot can supply 18 standard units, and at most 10 are available. Another note gives the color coding of the containers, which is irrelevant to capacity.

Main question. How many containers or slots are required, and is the plan feasible? Best decomposition (4 subproblems).
