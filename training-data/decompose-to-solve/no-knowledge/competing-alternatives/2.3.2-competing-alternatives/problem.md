# 2.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning seasonal farm work, 72 field tasks must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 13 field tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 21.00 units fixed plus 1.46 per item. Option B has a 12-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 16.00 units fixed plus 2.60 per item. The operation must finish within 58 minutes and cost at most 212.85 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
