# 9.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

92 supply units must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 13 supply units per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 1.87 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 60.00 units fixed plus 1.45 per item. The operation must finish within 67 minutes and cost at most 214.29 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
