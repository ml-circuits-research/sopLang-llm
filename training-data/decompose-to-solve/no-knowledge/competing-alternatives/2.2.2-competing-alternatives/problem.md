# 2.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For sequencing a small renovation project, 64 construction tasks must be handled. Option A has a 17-minute setup, processes 10 construction tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 33.00 units fixed plus 2.07 per item. Option B has a 12-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 44.00 units fixed plus 2.29 per item. The operation must finish within 63 minutes and cost at most 200.42 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
