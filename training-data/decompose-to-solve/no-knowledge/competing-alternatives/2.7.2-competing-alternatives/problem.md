# 2.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

84 dated records must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 10 dated records per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 2.01 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 29.00 units fixed plus 2.42 per item. The operation must finish within 67 minutes and cost at most 242.67 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
