# 3.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning water use and storage, 72 litres of water must be handled. Option A has a 15-minute setup, processes 11 litres of water per six-minute cycle, costs 44.00 units fixed plus 2.28 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 24.00 units fixed plus 2.30 per item. The operation must finish within 62 minutes and cost at most 218.09 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
