# 2.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For coordinating procurement and delivery, 39 shipments must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 16 shipments per six-minute cycle, costs 24.00 units fixed plus 1.93 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 41.00 units fixed plus 1.62 per item. The operation must finish within 35 minutes and cost at most 114.30 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
