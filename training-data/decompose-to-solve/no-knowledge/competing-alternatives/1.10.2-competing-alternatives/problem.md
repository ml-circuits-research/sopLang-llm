# 1.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing practical purchase options, 46 requirements must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 12 requirements per six-minute cycle, costs 41.00 units fixed plus 1.36 per item. Option B has a 11-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 18.00 units fixed plus 2.43 per item. The operation must finish within 46 minutes and cost at most 139.56 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
