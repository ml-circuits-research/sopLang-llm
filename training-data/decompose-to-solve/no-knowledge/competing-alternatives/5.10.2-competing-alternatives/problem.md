# 5.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a low-impact visit or conservation activity, 41 site zones must be handled. Option A has a 17-minute setup, processes 9 site zones per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 1.17 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 16.00 units fixed plus 2.56 per item. The operation must finish within 51 minutes and cost at most 130.86 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
