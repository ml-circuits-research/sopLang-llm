# 5.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning an elevation-sensitive route, 100 route segments must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 9 route segments per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 1.48 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 46.00 units fixed plus 2.04 per item. The operation must finish within 94 minutes and cost at most 260.12 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
