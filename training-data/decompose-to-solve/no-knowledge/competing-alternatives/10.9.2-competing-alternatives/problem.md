# 10.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a community renewable-energy project, 92 project components must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 14 project components per six-minute cycle, costs 47.00 units fixed plus 1.17 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 57.00 units fixed plus 1.20 per item. The operation must finish within 57 minutes and cost at most 177.24 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
