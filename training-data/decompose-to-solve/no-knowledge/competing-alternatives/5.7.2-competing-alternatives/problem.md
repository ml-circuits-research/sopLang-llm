# 5.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For allocating land among competing uses, 44 land parcels must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 12 land parcels per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 2.14 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 50.00 units fixed plus 2.01 per item. The operation must finish within 46 minutes and cost at most 157.05 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
