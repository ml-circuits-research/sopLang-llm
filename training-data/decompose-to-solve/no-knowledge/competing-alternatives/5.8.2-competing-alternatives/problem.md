# 5.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing urban travel modes, 31 urban trips must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 urban trips per six-minute cycle, costs 50.00 units fixed plus 2.00 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 2.49 per item. The operation must finish within 35 minutes and cost at most 125.28 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
