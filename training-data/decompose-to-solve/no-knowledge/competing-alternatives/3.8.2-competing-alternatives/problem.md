# 3.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a print run, 55 printed copies must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 13 printed copies per six-minute cycle, costs 52.00 units fixed plus 2.04 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 1.12 per item. The operation must finish within 45 minutes and cost at most 174.14 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
