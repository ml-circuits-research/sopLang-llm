# 6.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For constructing a historical narrative from dated evidence, 93 events must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 14 events per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 1.42 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 30.00 units fixed plus 1.43 per item. The operation must finish within 59 minutes and cost at most 181.86 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
