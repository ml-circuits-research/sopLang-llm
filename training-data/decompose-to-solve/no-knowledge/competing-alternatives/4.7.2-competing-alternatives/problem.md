# 4.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting geological observations, 50 field observations must be handled. Option A has a 16-minute setup, processes 10 field observations per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 2.39 per item. Option B has a 11-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 30.00 units fixed plus 1.66 per item. The operation must finish within 50 minutes and cost at most 171.39 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
