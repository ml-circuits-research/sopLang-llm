# 6.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For mapping responsibilities in an international cooperation case, 92 institutional commitments must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 9 institutional commitments per six-minute cycle, costs 52.00 units fixed plus 1.66 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 1.54 per item. The operation must finish within 82 minutes and cost at most 214.93 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
