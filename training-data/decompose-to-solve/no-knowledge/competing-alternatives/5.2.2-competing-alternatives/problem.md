# 5.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For reasoning about a river catchment, 60 catchment observations must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 13 catchment observations per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 2.34 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 23.00 units fixed plus 1.90 per item. The operation must finish within 46 minutes and cost at most 185.62 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
