# 3.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

48 energy-consuming devices must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 9 energy-consuming devices per six-minute cycle, costs 23.00 units fixed plus 1.52 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 55.00 units fixed plus 1.93 per item. The operation must finish within 48 minutes and cost at most 157.45 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
