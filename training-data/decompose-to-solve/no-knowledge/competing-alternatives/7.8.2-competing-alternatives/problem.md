# 7.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing primary and secondary historical sources, 33 source statements must be handled. Option A has a 14-minute setup, processes 9 source statements per six-minute cycle, costs 46.00 units fixed plus 1.20 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 1.89 per item. The operation must finish within 42 minutes and cost at most 95.44 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
