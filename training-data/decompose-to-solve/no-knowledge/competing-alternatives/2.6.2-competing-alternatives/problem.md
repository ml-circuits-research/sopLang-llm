# 2.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For preparing a software release, 80 release steps must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 12 release steps per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 2.01 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 26.00 units fixed plus 2.41 per item. The operation must finish within 56 minutes and cost at most 228.91 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
