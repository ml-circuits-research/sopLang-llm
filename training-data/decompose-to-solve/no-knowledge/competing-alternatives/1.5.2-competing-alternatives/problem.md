# 1.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For choosing between service plans with different constraints, 48 service units must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 12 service units per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 1.16 per item. Option B has a 9-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 23.00 units fixed plus 2.59 per item. The operation must finish within 36 minutes and cost at most 157.49 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
