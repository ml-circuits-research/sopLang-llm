# 2.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For running a laboratory workflow, 97 samples must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 9 samples per six-minute cycle, costs 25.00 units fixed plus 2.33 per item. Option B has a 11-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 1.26 per item. The operation must finish within 82 minutes and cost at most 260.58 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
