# 5.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning communication across time zones, 93 time-zone events must be handled. Option A has a 16-minute setup, processes 12 time-zone events per six-minute cycle, costs 43.00 units fixed plus 2.33 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 1.59 per item. The operation must finish within 68 minutes and cost at most 270.14 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
