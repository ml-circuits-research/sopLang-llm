# 9.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing suppliers and delivery options, 94 orders must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 11 orders per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 2.17 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 17.00 units fixed plus 1.61 per item. The operation must finish within 68 minutes and cost at most 251.92 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
