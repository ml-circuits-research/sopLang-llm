# 1.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

42 observations must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 9 observations per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 1.94 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 57.00 units fixed plus 1.84 per item. The operation must finish within 42 minutes and cost at most 144.23 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
