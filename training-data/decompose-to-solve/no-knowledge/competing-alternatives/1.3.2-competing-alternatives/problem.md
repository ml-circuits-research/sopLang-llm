# 1.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For preparing food for a group, 95 portions must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 12 portions per six-minute cycle, costs 33.00 units fixed plus 1.69 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 48.00 units fixed plus 2.53 per item. The operation must finish within 63 minutes and cost at most 298.39 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
