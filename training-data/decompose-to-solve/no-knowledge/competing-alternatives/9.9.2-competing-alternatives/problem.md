# 9.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

70 stakeholder effects must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 9 stakeholder effects per six-minute cycle, costs 46.00 units fixed plus 1.54 per item. Option B has a 11-minute setup, processes 17 per six-minute cycle, costs 19.00 units fixed plus 1.37 per item. The operation must finish within 64 minutes and cost at most 163.73 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
