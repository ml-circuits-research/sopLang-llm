# 9.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a learning sequence, 80 learning units must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 15 learning units per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 1.70 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 49.00 units fixed plus 1.26 per item. The operation must finish within 52 minutes and cost at most 179.97 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
