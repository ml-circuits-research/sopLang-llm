# 7.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For combining results from several experiments, 75 experimental results must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 experimental results per six-minute cycle, costs 46.00 units fixed plus 1.74 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 33.00 units fixed plus 1.25 per item. The operation must finish within 59 minutes and cost at most 186.75 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
