# 6.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For analyzing exchange along a historical trade network, 99 trade links must be handled. Option A has a 17-minute setup, processes 16 trade links per six-minute cycle, costs 29.00 units fixed plus 2.03 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 47.00 units fixed plus 2.00 per item. The operation must finish within 66 minutes and cost at most 254.55 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
