# 4.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For checking a simple material or structural design, 94 structural elements must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 9 structural elements per six-minute cycle, costs 49.00 units fixed plus 1.88 per item. Option B has a 9-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 2.08 per item. The operation must finish within 83 minutes and cost at most 245.63 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
