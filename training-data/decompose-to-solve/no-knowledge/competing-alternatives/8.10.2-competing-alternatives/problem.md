# 8.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For diagnosing a service incident, 73 service components must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 9 service components per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 1.21 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 1.53 per item. The operation must finish within 69 minutes and cost at most 166.72 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
