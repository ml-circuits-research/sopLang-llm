# 3.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 2.20 per item. The operation must finish within 47 minutes and cost at most 169.81 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
