# 3.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a non-medical sports training schedule, 58 training intervals must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 16 training intervals per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 1.37 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 2.20 per item. The operation must finish within 47 minutes and cost at most 169.81 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
