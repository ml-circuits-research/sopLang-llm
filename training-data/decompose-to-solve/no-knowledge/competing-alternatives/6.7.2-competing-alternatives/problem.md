# 6.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For curating objects from different artistic periods, 51 artworks must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 14 artworks per six-minute cycle, costs 52.00 units fixed plus 1.96 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 21.00 units fixed plus 2.42 per item. The operation must finish within 38 minutes and cost at most 162.18 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
