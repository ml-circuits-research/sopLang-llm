# 10.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a heritage-building restoration, 89 restoration tasks must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 12 restoration tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 2.25 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 1.82 per item. The operation must finish within 63 minutes and cost at most 237.14 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
