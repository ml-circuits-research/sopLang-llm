# 3.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For estimating material and recycling flows, 33 waste units must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 14 waste units per six-minute cycle, costs 33.00 units fixed plus 1.45 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 39.00 units fixed plus 1.02 per item. The operation must finish within 36 minutes and cost at most 90.78 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
