# 3.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing a constrained household budget, 62 budget items must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 10 budget items per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 2.23 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 1.91 per item. The operation must finish within 56 minutes and cost at most 182.02 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
