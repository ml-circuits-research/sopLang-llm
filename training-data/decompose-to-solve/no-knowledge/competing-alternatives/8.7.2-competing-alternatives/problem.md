# 8.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For assigning permissions in a small organization, 58 permissions must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 16 permissions per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 1.46 per item. Option B has a 9-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 44.00 units fixed plus 1.42 per item. The operation must finish within 46 minutes and cost at most 151.07 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
