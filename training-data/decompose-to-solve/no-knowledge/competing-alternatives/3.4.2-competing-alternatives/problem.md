# 3.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For scaling food portions and label quantities, 30 servings must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 13 servings per six-minute cycle, costs 37.00 units fixed plus 1.94 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 1.55 per item. The operation must finish within 33 minutes and cost at most 105.17 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
