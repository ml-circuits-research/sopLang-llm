# 6.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing institutional responsibilities in a generic constitutional system, 56 institutional actions must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 13 institutional actions per six-minute cycle, costs 39.00 units fixed plus 1.60 per item. Option B has a 11-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 48.00 units fixed plus 1.61 per item. The operation must finish within 46 minutes and cost at most 152.11 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
