# 7.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For evaluating a causal claim from observational data, 75 observations must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 9 observations per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 2.06 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 2.52 per item. The operation must finish within 68 minutes and cost at most 226.83 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
