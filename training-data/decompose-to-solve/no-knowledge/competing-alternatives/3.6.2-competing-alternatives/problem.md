# 3.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For managing a small inventory, 72 stock units must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 14 stock units per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 2.22 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 1.63 per item. The operation must finish within 49 minutes and cost at most 211.55 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
