# 10.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a multidisciplinary field expedition, 43 field activities must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 10 field activities per six-minute cycle, costs 36.00 units fixed plus 1.25 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 1.80 per item. The operation must finish within 48 minutes and cost at most 111.23 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
