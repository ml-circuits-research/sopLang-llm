# 1.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For a local public-transport journey, 93 journey legs must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 14 journey legs per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 2.04 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 48.00 units fixed plus 1.94 per item. The operation must finish within 67 minutes and cost at most 248.16 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
