# 7.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For reading and checking a quantitative chart, 66 data points must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 16 data points per six-minute cycle, costs 47.00 units fixed plus 1.58 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 31.00 units fixed plus 1.20 per item. The operation must finish within 53 minutes and cost at most 161.55 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
