# 5.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting a basic hazard scenario, 70 hazard observations must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 13 hazard observations per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 1.88 per item. Option B has a 12-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 22.00 units fixed plus 2.33 per item. The operation must finish within 55 minutes and cost at most 195.28 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
