# 5.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For using a map to plan a route or field survey, 49 map segments must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 9 map segments per six-minute cycle, costs 41.00 units fixed plus 1.16 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 17 per six-minute cycle, costs 48.00 units fixed plus 2.58 per item. The operation must finish within 49 minutes and cost at most 184.44 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
