# 4.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a low-voltage electrical load, 85 electrical loads must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 11 electrical loads per six-minute cycle, costs 37.00 units fixed plus 1.51 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 1.36 per item. The operation must finish within 65 minutes and cost at most 174.94 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
