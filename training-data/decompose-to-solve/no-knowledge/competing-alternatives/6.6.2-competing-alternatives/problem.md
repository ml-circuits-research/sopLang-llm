# 6.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting phases of a historic building, 75 building features must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 15 building features per six-minute cycle, costs 30.00 units fixed plus 2.14 per item. Option B has a 10-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 18.00 units fixed plus 1.56 per item. The operation must finish within 56 minutes and cost at most 200.35 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
