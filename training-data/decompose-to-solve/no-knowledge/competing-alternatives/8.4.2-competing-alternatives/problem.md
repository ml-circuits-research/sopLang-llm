# 8.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For resolving software dependency constraints, 31 packages must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 15 packages per six-minute cycle, costs 22.00 units fixed plus 2.15 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 52.00 units fixed plus 1.93 per item. The operation must finish within 40 minutes and cost at most 121.84 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
