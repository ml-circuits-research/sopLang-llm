# 1.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For packing for a trip under weight and volume limits, 46 packed items must be handled. Option A has a 16-minute setup, processes 12 packed items per six-minute cycle, costs 43.00 units fixed plus 1.64 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 59.00 units fixed plus 1.77 per item. The operation must finish within 44 minutes and cost at most 150.63 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
