# 6.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For analyzing a simplified representation system, 100 votes or seats must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 16 votes or seats per six-minute cycle, costs 54.00 units fixed plus 2.14 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 1.11 per item. The operation must finish within 68 minutes and cost at most 277.86 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
