# 9.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For preparing a structured negotiation, 99 issues must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 14 issues per six-minute cycle, costs 43.00 units fixed plus 1.51 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 15.00 units fixed plus 1.54 per item. The operation must finish within 61 minutes and cost at most 202.69 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
