# 1.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For organizing a small community event, 90 event tasks must be handled. Option A has a 15-minute setup, processes 15 event tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 21.00 units fixed plus 1.27 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 1.03 per item. The operation must finish within 55 minutes and cost at most 155.21 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
