# 4.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For analyzing a simple motion or load case, 49 measurements must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 10 measurements per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 1.91 per item. Option B has a 10-minute setup, processes 11 per six-minute cycle, costs 56.00 units fixed plus 1.66 per item. The operation must finish within 47 minutes and cost at most 154.96 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
