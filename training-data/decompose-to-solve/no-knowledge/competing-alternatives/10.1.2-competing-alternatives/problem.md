# 10.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a city festival with transport, energy, waste, and scheduling constraints, 89 festival operations must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 14 festival operations per six-minute cycle, costs 51.00 units fixed plus 1.76 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 58.00 units fixed plus 2.37 per item. The operation must finish within 56 minutes and cost at most 279.04 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
