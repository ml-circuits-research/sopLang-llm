# 6.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For reconstructing the provenance of an artifact, 52 records must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 14 records per six-minute cycle, costs 21.00 units fixed plus 1.90 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 17 per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 2.50 per item. The operation must finish within 41 minutes and cost at most 168.26 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
