# 2.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a multi-leg rail or air journey, 53 connections must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 16 connections per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 2.20 per item. Option B has a 9-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 51.00 units fixed plus 2.40 per item. The operation must finish within 39 minutes and cost at most 188.20 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
