# 7.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For reasoning about a probabilistic decision, 57 cases must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 12 cases per six-minute cycle, costs 55.00 units fixed plus 2.06 per item. Option B has a 12-minute setup, processes 17 per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 2.39 per item. The operation must finish within 43 minutes and cost at most 196.32 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
