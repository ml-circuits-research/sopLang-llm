# 9.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a small project, 77 project tasks must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 14 project tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 2.11 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 1.60 per item. The operation must finish within 54 minutes and cost at most 207.17 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
