# 9.1.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For allocating work across a small team, 91 work packages must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 work packages per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 1.61 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 58.00 units fixed plus 1.70 per item. The operation must finish within 59 minutes and cost at most 222.49 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
