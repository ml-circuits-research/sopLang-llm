# 9.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For checking a batch or service process, 82 samples must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 12 samples per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 1.18 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 25.00 units fixed plus 2.27 per item. The operation must finish within 57 minutes and cost at most 221.37 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
