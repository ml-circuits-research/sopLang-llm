# 10.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

72 relief deliveries must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 9 relief deliveries per six-minute cycle, costs 35.00 units fixed plus 1.30 per item. Option B has a 10-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 1.01 per item. The operation must finish within 60 minutes and cost at most 138.45 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
