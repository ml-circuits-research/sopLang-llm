# 10.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning an educational international trip, 42 travel activities must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 16 travel activities per six-minute cycle, costs 50.00 units fixed plus 1.11 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 1.17 per item. The operation must finish within 34 minutes and cost at most 106.68 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
