# 7.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For evaluating an environmental claim from mixed evidence, 78 measurements must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 11 measurements per six-minute cycle, costs 55.00 units fixed plus 1.98 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 13 per six-minute cycle, costs 36.00 units fixed plus 1.65 per item. The operation must finish within 65 minutes and cost at most 219.60 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
