# 6.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a multilingual translation workflow, 62 text segments must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 14 text segments per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 1.31 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 22.00 units fixed plus 1.10 per item. The operation must finish within 47 minutes and cost at most 144.09 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
