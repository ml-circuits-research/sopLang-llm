# 8.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For evaluating a small AI-assisted workflow, 76 test cases must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 11 test cases per six-minute cycle, costs 54.00 units fixed plus 1.49 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 2.30 per item. The operation must finish within 64 minutes and cost at most 224.62 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
