# 6.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For following a generic hierarchy of rules and decisions, 39 legal instruments must be handled. Option A has a 18-minute setup, processes 15 legal instruments per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 1.90 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 1.96 per item. The operation must finish within 40 minutes and cost at most 127.58 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
