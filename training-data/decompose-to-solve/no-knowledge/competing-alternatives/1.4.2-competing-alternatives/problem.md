# 1.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, 94 scheduled tasks must be handled. Option A has a 15-minute setup, processes 12 scheduled tasks per six-minute cycle, costs 37.00 units fixed plus 1.92 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 37.00 units fixed plus 2.19 per item. The operation must finish within 67 minutes and cost at most 252.44 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
