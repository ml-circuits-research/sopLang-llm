# 3.7.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting population and area data, 98 population groups must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 15 population groups per six-minute cycle, costs 40.00 units fixed plus 2.28 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 19.00 units fixed plus 1.88 per item. The operation must finish within 55 minutes and cost at most 273.22 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
