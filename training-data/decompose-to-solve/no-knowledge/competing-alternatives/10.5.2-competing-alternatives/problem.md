# 10.5.2 — Competing Alternatives

38 production batches must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 10 production batches per six-minute cycle, costs 20.00 units fixed plus 2.16 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 56.00 units fixed plus 1.04 per item. The operation must finish within 41 minutes and cost at most 112.05 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred?
