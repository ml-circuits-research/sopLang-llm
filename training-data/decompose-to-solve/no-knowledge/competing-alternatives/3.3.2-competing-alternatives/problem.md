# 3.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For estimating travel fuel or energy needs, 47 journey kilometres must be handled. Option A has a 14-minute setup, processes 16 journey kilometres per six-minute cycle, costs 27.00 units fixed plus 1.94 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 18.00 units fixed plus 1.33 per item. The operation must finish within 38 minutes and cost at most 128.17 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
