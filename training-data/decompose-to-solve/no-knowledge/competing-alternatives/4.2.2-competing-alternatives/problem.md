# 4.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For comparing heat-loss or insulation choices, 30 thermal observations must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 11 thermal observations per six-minute cycle, costs 31.00 units fixed plus 2.34 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 16.00 units fixed plus 1.61 per item. The operation must finish within 32 minutes and cost at most 111.11 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
