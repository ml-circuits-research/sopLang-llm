# 4.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For analyzing an ecosystem interaction, 79 organism groups must be handled. Option A has a 9-minute setup, processes 11 organism groups per six-minute cycle, costs 49.00 units fixed plus 1.83 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 12 per six-minute cycle, costs 23.00 units fixed plus 2.40 per item. The operation must finish within 63 minutes and cost at most 222.66 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
