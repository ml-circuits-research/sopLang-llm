# 4.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For combining repeated measurements, 51 measurements must be handled. Option A has a 14-minute setup, processes 10 measurements per six-minute cycle, costs 34.00 units fixed plus 2.20 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 2.20 per item. The operation must finish within 54 minutes and cost at most 167.33 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
