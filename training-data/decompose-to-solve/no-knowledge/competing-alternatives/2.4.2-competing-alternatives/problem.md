# 2.4.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For installing a museum exhibition, 44 exhibition components must be handled. Option A has a 15-minute setup, processes 14 exhibition components per six-minute cycle, costs 46.00 units fixed plus 1.97 per item. Option B has a 13-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 1.22 per item. The operation must finish within 43 minutes and cost at most 142.49 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
