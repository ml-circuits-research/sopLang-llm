# 2.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For scheduling sessions and shared rooms, 46 sessions must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 11 sessions per six-minute cycle, costs 52.00 units fixed plus 2.05 per item. Option B has a 6-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 16.00 units fixed plus 2.05 per item. The operation must finish within 44 minutes and cost at most 156.38 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
