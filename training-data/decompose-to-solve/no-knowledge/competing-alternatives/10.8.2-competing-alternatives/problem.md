# 10.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning a civic open-data release, 72 data sets must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 11 data sets per six-minute cycle, costs 44.00 units fixed plus 2.03 per item. Option B has a 16-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 19.00 units fixed plus 2.00 per item. The operation must finish within 58 minutes and cost at most 200.33 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
