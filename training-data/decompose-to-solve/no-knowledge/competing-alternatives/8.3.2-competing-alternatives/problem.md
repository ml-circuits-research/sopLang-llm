# 8.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning backup and recovery, 45 data sets must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 12 data sets per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 1.78 per item. Option B has a 10-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 2.01 per item. The operation must finish within 40 minutes and cost at most 148.82 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
