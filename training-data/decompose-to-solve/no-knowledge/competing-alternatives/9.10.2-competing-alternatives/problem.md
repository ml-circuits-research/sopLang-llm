# 9.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning routine safety controls, 92 hazards must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 10 hazards per six-minute cycle, costs 29.00 units fixed plus 1.14 per item. Option B has a 12-minute setup, processes 14 per six-minute cycle, costs 54.00 units fixed plus 2.07 per item. The operation must finish within 75 minutes and cost at most 254.43 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
