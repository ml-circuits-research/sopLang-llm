# 5.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting seasonal climate information, 30 climate observations must be handled. Option A has a 13-minute setup, processes 11 climate observations per six-minute cycle, costs 55.00 units fixed plus 2.18 per item. Option B has a 14-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 53.00 units fixed plus 1.58 per item. The operation must finish within 35 minutes and cost at most 130.32 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
