# 4.8.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For planning an introductory astronomy observation, 53 observations must be handled. Option A has a 11-minute setup, processes 15 observations per six-minute cycle, costs 28.00 units fixed plus 2.29 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 1.56 per item. The operation must finish within 39 minutes and cost at most 159.30 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
