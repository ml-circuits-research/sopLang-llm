# 7.3.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For interpreting a public survey, 66 responses must be handled. Option A has a 15-minute setup, processes 15 responses per six-minute cycle, costs 47.00 units fixed plus 1.53 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 20 per six-minute cycle, costs 37.00 units fixed plus 1.73 per item. The operation must finish within 49 minutes and cost at most 170.52 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
