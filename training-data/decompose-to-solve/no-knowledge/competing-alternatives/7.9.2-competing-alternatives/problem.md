# 7.9.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For reconstructing a non-political event from reports, 98 reports must be handled. Option A has a 14-minute setup, processes 15 reports per six-minute cycle, costs 32.00 units fixed plus 1.56 per item. Option B has a 15-minute setup, processes 18 per six-minute cycle, costs 36.00 units fixed plus 1.68 per item. The operation must finish within 60 minutes and cost at most 210.99 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
