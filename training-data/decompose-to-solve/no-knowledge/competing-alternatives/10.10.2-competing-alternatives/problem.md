# 10.10.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For coordinating a conference and publication workflow, 33 submissions must be handled. Option A has a 8-minute setup, processes 11 submissions per six-minute cycle, costs 51.00 units fixed plus 1.88 per item. Option B has a 9-minute setup, processes 16 per six-minute cycle, costs 24.00 units fixed plus 1.40 per item. The operation must finish within 31 minutes and cost at most 122.92 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
