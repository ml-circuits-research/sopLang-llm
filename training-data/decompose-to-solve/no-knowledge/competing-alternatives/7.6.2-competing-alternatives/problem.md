# 7.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For evaluating a general, non-personal health claim, 82 study findings must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 10 study findings per six-minute cycle, costs 23.00 units fixed plus 1.69 per item. Option B has a 8-minute setup, processes 15 per six-minute cycle, costs 42.00 units fixed plus 1.91 per item. The operation must finish within 68 minutes and cost at most 208.31 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
