# 8.6.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For designing a data-collection workflow, 47 data fields must be handled. Option A has a 12-minute setup, processes 15 data fields per six-minute cycle, costs 48.00 units fixed plus 1.63 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 19 per six-minute cycle, costs 45.00 units fixed plus 1.55 per item. The operation must finish within 40 minutes and cost at most 141.50 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
