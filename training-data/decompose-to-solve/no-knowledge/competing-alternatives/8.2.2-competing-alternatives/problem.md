# 8.2.2 — Competing Alternatives

Scenario. For designing a basic authentication flow, 78 authentication steps must be handled. Option A has a 10-minute setup, processes 13 authentication steps per six-minute cycle, costs 54.00 units fixed plus 1.67 per item. Option B has a 7-minute setup, processes 17 per six-minute cycle, costs 38.00 units fixed plus 2.32 per item. The operation must finish within 50 minutes and cost at most 228.75 units. A brochure also gives background information about the domain, but it does not alter these operational numbers.

Main question. Which option should be selected if feasibility is mandatory and, among feasible options, lower cost is preferred? Best decomposition (3 subproblems).
