# 6.7.6 — Evidence Tree and Elimination

Scenario. In curating objects from different artistic periods, a failure occurred while handling 60 artworks. Three hypotheses are proposed: H1—insufficient capacity; H2—an ordering or dependency mistake; H3—a measurement or recording error. The capacity log shows at least 72 units were available throughout. A time-stamped record shows a dependent action occurred before its prerequisite had been completed. An independent record reproduces the measured quantity within the stated tolerance. A separate comment says the team was busy that day; this may be true but does not discriminate among the three hypotheses.

Main question. Which hypothesis is best supported, and how should the evidence be decomposed so each observation tests the right claim? Best decomposition (6 subproblems).
