# 1.4.6 — Evidence Tree and Elimination

In coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, a failure occurred while handling 64 scheduled tasks. Three hypotheses are proposed: H1—insufficient capacity; H2—an ordering or dependency mistake; H3—a measurement or recording error. The capacity log shows at least 76 units were available throughout. A time-stamped record shows a dependent action occurred before its prerequisite had been completed. An independent record reproduces the measured quantity within the stated tolerance.

Question. Which hypothesis is best supported, and how should the evidence be decomposed so each observation tests the right claim?
