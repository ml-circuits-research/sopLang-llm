# 9.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 158 local units of hazards. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 28 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 5 minutes. Setup takes 20 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 76 fixed plus 1.34 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 43 minutes and the budget is 256.49 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
