# 7.8.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In comparing primary and secondary historical sources, a plan starts from 79 local units of source statements. Convert by 1.5 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 20 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 8 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 50 fixed plus 0.85 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 52 minutes and the budget is 225.06 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
