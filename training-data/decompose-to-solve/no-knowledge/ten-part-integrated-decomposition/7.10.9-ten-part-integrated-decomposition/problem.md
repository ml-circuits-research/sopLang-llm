# 7.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In combining results from several experiments, a plan starts from 93 local units of experimental results. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 19 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 11 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 7 batch slots. Cost is 72 fixed plus 1.32 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 45 minutes and the budget is 174.92 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
