# 2.4.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 120 local units of exhibition components. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 25 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 12 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 10 batch slots. Cost is 57 fixed plus 1.85 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 58 minutes and the budget is 320.60 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
