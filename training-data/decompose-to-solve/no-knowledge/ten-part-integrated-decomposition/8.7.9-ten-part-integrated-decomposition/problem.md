# 8.7.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 87 local units of permissions. Convert by 1.5 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 25 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 19 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 10 batch slots. Cost is 67 fixed plus 1.92 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 63 minutes and the budget is 359.66 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
