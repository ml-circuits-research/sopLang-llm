# 4.9.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In checking a simple material or structural design, a plan starts from 91 local units of structural elements. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 27 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 9 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 8 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 72 fixed plus 2.02 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 49 minutes and the budget is 291.64 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
