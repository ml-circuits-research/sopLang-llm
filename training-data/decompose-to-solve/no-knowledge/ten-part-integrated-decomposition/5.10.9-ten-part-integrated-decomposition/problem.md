# 5.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning a low-impact visit or conservation activity, a plan starts from 157 local units of site zones. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 10% process loss. Each batch handles 17 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 14 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 5 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 47 fixed plus 1.89 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 65 minutes and the budget is 186.70 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
