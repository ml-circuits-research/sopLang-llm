# 2.9.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In coordinating procurement and delivery, a plan starts from 128 local units of shipments. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 18 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 4 minutes. Setup takes 11 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 6 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 73 fixed plus 1.77 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 49 minutes and the budget is 428.42 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
