# 6.6.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In interpreting phases of a historic building, a plan starts from 77 local units of building features. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 29 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 5 minutes. Setup takes 12 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 10 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 59 fixed plus 2.08 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 37 minutes and the budget is 182.45 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
