# 9.1.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In allocating work across a small team, a plan starts from 75 local units of work packages. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 14 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 13 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 9 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 76 fixed plus 1.40 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 35 minutes and the budget is 152.91 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
