# 6.1.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In constructing a historical narrative from dated evidence, a plan starts from 95 local units of events. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 27 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 12 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 8 batch slots. Cost is 77 fixed plus 2.16 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 45 minutes and the budget is 344.06 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
