# 1.4.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, a plan starts from 85 local units of scheduled tasks. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 21 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 9 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 6 minutes. There are at most 6 batch slots. Cost is 40 fixed plus 1.70 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 68 minutes and the budget is 267.64 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
