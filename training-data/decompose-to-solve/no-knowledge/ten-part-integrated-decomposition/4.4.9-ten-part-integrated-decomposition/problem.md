# 4.4.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning an optics demonstration, a plan starts from 139 local units of optical elements. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 26 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 7 minutes. Setup takes 15 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 69 fixed plus 2.03 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 57 minutes and the budget is 279.37 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
