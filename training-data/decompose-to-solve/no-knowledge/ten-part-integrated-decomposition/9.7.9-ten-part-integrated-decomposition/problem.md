# 9.7.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning non-clinical public-health logistics, a plan starts from 117 local units of supply units. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 29 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 11 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 6 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 80 fixed plus 1.36 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 49 minutes and the budget is 206.36 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
