# 10.2.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 153 local units of museum work packages. Convert by 1.5 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 27 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 16 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 8 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 78 fixed plus 1.16 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 79 minutes and the budget is 400.52 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
