# 6.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In mapping responsibilities in an international cooperation case, a plan starts from 108 local units of institutional commitments. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 26 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 9 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 7 minutes. There are at most 7 batch slots. Cost is 85 fixed plus 1.33 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 33 minutes and the budget is 255.81 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
