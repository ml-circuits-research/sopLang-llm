# 10.9.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 102 local units of project components. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 24 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 7 minutes. Setup takes 8 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 10 minutes. There are at most 6 batch slots. Cost is 85 fixed plus 1.93 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 37 minutes and the budget is 220.20 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
