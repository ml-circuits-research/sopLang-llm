# 6.7.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In curating objects from different artistic periods, a plan starts from 70 local units of artworks. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 17 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 15 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 51 fixed plus 0.90 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 47 minutes and the budget is 120.37 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
