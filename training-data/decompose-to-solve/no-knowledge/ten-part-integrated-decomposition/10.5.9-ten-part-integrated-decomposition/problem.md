# 10.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In launching a small manufacturing line, a plan starts from 138 local units of production batches. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 22 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 5 minutes. Setup takes 20 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 7 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 87 fixed plus 1.91 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 63 minutes and the budget is 290.10 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
