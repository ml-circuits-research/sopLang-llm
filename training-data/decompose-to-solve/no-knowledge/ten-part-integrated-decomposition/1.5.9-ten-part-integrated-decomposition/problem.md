# 1.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In choosing between service plans with different constraints, a plan starts from 97 local units of service units. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 20 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 11 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 83 fixed plus 1.30 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 102 minutes and the budget is 290.95 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
