# 9.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 110 local units of samples. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 21 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 8 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 10 minutes. There are at most 8 batch slots. Cost is 81 fixed plus 0.98 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 28 minutes and the budget is 202.04 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
