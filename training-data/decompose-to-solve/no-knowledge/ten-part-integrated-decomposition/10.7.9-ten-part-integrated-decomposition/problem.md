# 10.7.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning a heritage-building restoration, a plan starts from 73 local units of restoration tasks. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 10% process loss. Each batch handles 29 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 18 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 69 fixed plus 1.81 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 46 minutes and the budget is 235.76 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
