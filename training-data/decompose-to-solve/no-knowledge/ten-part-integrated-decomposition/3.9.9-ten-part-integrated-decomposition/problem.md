# 3.9.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning a non-medical sports training schedule, a plan starts from 105 local units of training intervals. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 4% process loss. Each batch handles 18 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 6 minutes. Setup takes 10 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 78 fixed plus 2.19 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 62 minutes and the budget is 352.83 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
