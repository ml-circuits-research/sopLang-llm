# 2.2.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In sequencing a small renovation project, a plan starts from 75 local units of construction tasks. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 25 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 4 minutes. Setup takes 18 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 5 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 66 fixed plus 1.43 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 47 minutes and the budget is 207.98 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
