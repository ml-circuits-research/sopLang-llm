# 1.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In comparing practical purchase options, a plan starts from 72 local units of requirements. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 26 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 4 minutes. Setup takes 19 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 10 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 69 fixed plus 0.90 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 49 minutes and the budget is 164.13 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
