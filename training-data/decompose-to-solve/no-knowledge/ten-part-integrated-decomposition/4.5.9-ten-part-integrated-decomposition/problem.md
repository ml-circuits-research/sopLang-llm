# 4.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In preparing and checking a laboratory solution, a plan starts from 158 local units of solution portions. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 26 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 5 minutes. Setup takes 20 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 88 fixed plus 1.07 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 82 minutes and the budget is 327.80 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
