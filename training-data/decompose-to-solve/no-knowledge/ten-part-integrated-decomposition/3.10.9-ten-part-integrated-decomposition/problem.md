# 3.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 109 local units of waste units. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 21 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 13 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 43 fixed plus 1.58 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 43 minutes and the budget is 266.24 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
