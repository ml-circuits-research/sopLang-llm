# 10.10.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In coordinating a conference and publication workflow, a plan starts from 101 local units of submissions. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 10% process loss. Each batch handles 18 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 17 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 11 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 61 fixed plus 1.78 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 62 minutes and the budget is 135.88 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
