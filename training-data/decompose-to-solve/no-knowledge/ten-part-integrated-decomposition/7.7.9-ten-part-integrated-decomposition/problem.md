# 7.7.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In evaluating an environmental claim from mixed evidence, a plan starts from 122 local units of measurements. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 19 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 18 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 11 batch slots. Cost is 41 fixed plus 1.36 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 80 minutes and the budget is 196.08 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
