# 1.6.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In organizing a small community event, a plan starts from 117 local units of event tasks. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 10% process loss. Each batch handles 15 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 7 minutes. Setup takes 10 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 5 minutes. There are at most 10 batch slots. Cost is 54 fixed plus 1.89 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 52 minutes and the budget is 257.98 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
