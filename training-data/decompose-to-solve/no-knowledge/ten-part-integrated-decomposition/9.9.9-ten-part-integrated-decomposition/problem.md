# 9.9.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In mapping consequences for several stakeholders, a plan starts from 93 local units of stakeholder effects. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 24 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 12 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 56 fixed plus 1.56 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 32 minutes and the budget is 113.29 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
