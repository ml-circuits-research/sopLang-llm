# 2.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 117 local units of samples. Convert by 1 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 22 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 16 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 46 fixed plus 1.10 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 89 minutes and the budget is 251.88 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
