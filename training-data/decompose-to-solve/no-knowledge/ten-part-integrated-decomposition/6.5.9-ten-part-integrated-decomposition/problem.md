# 6.5.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In following a generic hierarchy of rules and decisions, a plan starts from 126 local units of legal instruments. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 14 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 11 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 10 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 42 fixed plus 1.62 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 37 minutes and the budget is 210.90 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
