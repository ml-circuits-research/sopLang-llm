# 9.4.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

A plan starts from 157 local units of orders. Convert by 1.5 to standard units, then allow 10% process loss. Each batch handles 19 standard units. Up to 3 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 14 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 5 minutes. There are at most 12 batch slots. Cost is 51 fixed plus 1.15 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 80 minutes and the budget is 371.86 units.

Question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts?
