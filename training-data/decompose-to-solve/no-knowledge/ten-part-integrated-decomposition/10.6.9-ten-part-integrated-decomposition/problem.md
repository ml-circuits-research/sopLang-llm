# 10.6.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning an educational international trip, a plan starts from 104 local units of travel activities. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 6% process loss. Each batch handles 21 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 9 minutes. Setup takes 8 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 68 fixed plus 0.85 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 61 minutes and the budget is 225.63 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
