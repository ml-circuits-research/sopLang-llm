# 7.6.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In evaluating a general, non-personal health claim, a plan starts from 76 local units of study findings. Convert by 0.75 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 22 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 10 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 12 minutes. There are at most 8 batch slots. Cost is 76 fixed plus 1.39 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 62 minutes and the budget is 226.16 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
