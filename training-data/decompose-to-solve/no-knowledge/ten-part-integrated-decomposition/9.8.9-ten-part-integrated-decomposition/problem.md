# 9.8.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In preparing a structured negotiation, a plan starts from 150 local units of issues. Convert by 1.25 to standard units, then allow 12% process loss. Each batch handles 28 standard units. Up to 1 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 4 minutes. Setup takes 13 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 4 minutes. There are at most 6 batch slots. Cost is 43 fixed plus 1.68 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 41 minutes and the budget is 441.79 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
