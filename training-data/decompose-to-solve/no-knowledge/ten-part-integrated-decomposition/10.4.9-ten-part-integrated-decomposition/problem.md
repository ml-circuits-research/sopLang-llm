# 10.4.9 — Ten-Part Integrated Decomposition

Scenario. In planning a fictional training exercise in relief distribution, a plan starts from 96 local units of relief deliveries. Convert by 0.5 to standard units, then allow 8% process loss. Each batch handles 27 standard units. Up to 2 batches can run in parallel, each wave taking 8 minutes. Setup takes 12 minutes and a mandatory buffer adds 9 minutes. There are at most 9 batch slots. Cost is 80 fixed plus 1.85 per pre-loss standard unit. The deadline is 34 minutes and the budget is 196.46 units. The data are deliberately mixed rather than grouped by calculation.

Main question. Is the plan feasible, and what intermediate outputs should be computed so that each subproblem uses the fewest necessary facts? Best decomposition (10 subproblems).
