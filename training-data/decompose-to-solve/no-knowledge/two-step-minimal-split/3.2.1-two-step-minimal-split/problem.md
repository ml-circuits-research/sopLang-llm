# 3.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning water use and storage, a team must handle 117 litres of water. The stated completion limit is 96 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 litres of water, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
