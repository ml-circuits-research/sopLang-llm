# 9.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In checking a batch or service process, a team must handle 99 samples. The stated completion limit is 19 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 samples, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
