# 6.7.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In curating objects from different artistic periods, a team must handle 114 artworks. The stated completion limit is 96 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 artworks, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 6 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
