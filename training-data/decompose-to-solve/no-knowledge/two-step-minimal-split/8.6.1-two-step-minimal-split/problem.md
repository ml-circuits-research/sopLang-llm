# 8.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In designing a data-collection workflow, a team must handle 83 data fields. The stated completion limit is 66 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 8 data fields, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
