# 8.8.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In reasoning about encryption, signatures, and key handling, a team must handle 85 messages. The stated completion limit is 50 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 messages, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
