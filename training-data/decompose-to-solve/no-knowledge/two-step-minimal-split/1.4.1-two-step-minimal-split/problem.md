# 1.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, a team must handle 124 scheduled tasks. The stated completion limit is 50 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 13 scheduled tasks, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
