# 1.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In a local public-transport journey, a team must handle 127 journey legs. The stated completion limit is 41 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 17 journey legs, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
