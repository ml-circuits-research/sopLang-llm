# 5.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In reasoning about a river catchment, a team must handle 91 catchment observations. The stated completion limit is 12 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 catchment observations, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 6 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
