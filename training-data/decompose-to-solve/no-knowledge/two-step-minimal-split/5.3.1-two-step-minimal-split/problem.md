# 5.3.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In interpreting seasonal climate information, a team must handle 98 climate observations. The stated completion limit is 85 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 climate observations, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
