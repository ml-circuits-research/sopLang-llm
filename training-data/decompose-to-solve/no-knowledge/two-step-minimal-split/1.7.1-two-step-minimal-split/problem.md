# 1.7.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In following a multi-step administrative procedure, a team must handle 65 documents. The stated completion limit is 49 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 documents, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 6 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
