# 4.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In checking a simple material or structural design, a team must handle 98 structural elements. The stated completion limit is 99 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 structural elements, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
