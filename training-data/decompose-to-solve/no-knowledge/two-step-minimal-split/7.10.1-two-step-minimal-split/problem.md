# 7.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In combining results from several experiments, a team must handle 122 experimental results. The stated completion limit is 82 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 experimental results, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
