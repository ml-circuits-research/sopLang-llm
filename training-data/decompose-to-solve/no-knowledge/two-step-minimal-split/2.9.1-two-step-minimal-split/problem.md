# 2.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In coordinating procurement and delivery, a team must handle 79 shipments. The stated completion limit is 52 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 9 shipments, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
