# 5.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a low-impact visit or conservation activity, a team must handle 128 site zones. The stated completion limit is 26 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 site zones, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
