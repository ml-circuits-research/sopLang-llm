# 2.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a staged evacuation exercise, a team must handle 81 evacuation groups. The stated completion limit is 37 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 evacuation groups, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
