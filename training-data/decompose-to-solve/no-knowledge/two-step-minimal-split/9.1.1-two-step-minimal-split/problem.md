# 9.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In allocating work across a small team, a team must handle 96 work packages. The stated completion limit is 55 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 work packages, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 7 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
