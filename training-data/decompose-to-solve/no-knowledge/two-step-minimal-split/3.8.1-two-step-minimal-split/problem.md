# 3.8.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a print run, a team must handle 110 printed copies. The stated completion limit is 51 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 printed copies, and each block takes 4 minutes. A one-time setup takes 10 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
