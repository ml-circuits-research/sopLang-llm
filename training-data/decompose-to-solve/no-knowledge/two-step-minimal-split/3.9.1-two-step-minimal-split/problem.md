# 3.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a non-medical sports training schedule, a team must handle 79 training intervals. The stated completion limit is 23 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 16 training intervals, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 6 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
