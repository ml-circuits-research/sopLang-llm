# 9.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In mapping consequences for several stakeholders, a team must handle 74 stakeholder effects. The stated completion limit is 43 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 stakeholder effects, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 15 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
