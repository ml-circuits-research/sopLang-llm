# 10.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a fictional training exercise in relief distribution, a team must handle 46 relief deliveries. The stated completion limit is 28 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 relief deliveries, and each block takes 7 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
