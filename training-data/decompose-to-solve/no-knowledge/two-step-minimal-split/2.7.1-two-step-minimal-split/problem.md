# 2.7.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In building a defensible historical timeline, a team must handle 89 dated records. The stated completion limit is 75 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 11 dated records, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 13 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
