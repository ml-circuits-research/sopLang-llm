# 10.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a community renewable-energy project, a team must handle 91 project components. The stated completion limit is 60 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 project components, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
