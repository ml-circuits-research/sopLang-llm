# 2.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a multi-leg rail or air journey, a team must handle 98 connections. The stated completion limit is 65 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 connections, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 5 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
