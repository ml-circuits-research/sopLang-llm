# 2.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In sequencing a small renovation project, a team must handle 121 construction tasks. The stated completion limit is 79 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 construction tasks, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 9 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
