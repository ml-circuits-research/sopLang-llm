# 4.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In analyzing an ecosystem interaction, a team must handle 56 organism groups. The stated completion limit is 16 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 organism groups, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
