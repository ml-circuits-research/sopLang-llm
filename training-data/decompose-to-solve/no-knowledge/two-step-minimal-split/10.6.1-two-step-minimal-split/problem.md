# 10.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning an educational international trip, a team must handle 66 travel activities. The stated completion limit is 15 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 16 travel activities, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
