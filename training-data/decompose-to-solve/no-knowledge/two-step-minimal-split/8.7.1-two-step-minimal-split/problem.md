# 8.7.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In assigning permissions in a small organization, a team must handle 85 permissions. The stated completion limit is 68 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 9 permissions, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
