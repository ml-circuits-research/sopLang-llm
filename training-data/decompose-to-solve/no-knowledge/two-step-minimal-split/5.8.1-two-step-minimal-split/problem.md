# 5.8.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In comparing urban travel modes, a team must handle 127 urban trips. The stated completion limit is 113 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 urban trips, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 5 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
