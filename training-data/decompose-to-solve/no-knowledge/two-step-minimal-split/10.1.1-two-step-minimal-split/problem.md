# 10.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a city festival with transport, energy, waste, and scheduling constraints, a team must handle 89 festival operations. The stated completion limit is 55 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 festival operations, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
