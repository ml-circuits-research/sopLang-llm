# 5.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In using a map to plan a route or field survey, a team must handle 70 map segments. The stated completion limit is 65 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 map segments, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 13 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
