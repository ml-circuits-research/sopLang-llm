# 7.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In reasoning about a probabilistic decision, a team must handle 103 cases. The stated completion limit is 67 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 13 cases, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 7 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
