# 6.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In analyzing exchange along a historical trade network, a team must handle 64 trade links. The stated completion limit is 92 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 9 trade links, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
