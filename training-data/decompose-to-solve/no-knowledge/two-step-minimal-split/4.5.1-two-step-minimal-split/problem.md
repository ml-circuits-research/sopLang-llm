# 4.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In preparing and checking a laboratory solution, a team must handle 77 solution portions. The stated completion limit is 72 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 8 solution portions, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 15 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
