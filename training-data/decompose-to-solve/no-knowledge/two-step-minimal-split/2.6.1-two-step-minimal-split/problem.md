# 2.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In preparing a software release, a team must handle 66 release steps. The stated completion limit is 51 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 release steps, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 10 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
