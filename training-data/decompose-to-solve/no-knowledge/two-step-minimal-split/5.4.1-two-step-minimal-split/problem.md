# 5.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning communication across time zones, a team must handle 81 time-zone events. The stated completion limit is 29 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 time-zone events, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 6 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
