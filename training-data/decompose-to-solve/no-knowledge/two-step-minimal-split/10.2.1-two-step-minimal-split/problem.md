# 10.2.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a museum expansion and collection move, a team must handle 68 museum work packages. The stated completion limit is 79 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 9 museum work packages, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
