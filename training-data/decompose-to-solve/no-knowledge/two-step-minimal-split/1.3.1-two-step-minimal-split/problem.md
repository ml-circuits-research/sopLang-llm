# 1.3.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In preparing food for a group, a team must handle 121 portions. The stated completion limit is 54 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 17 portions, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 10 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
