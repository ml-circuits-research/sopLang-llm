# 1.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In comparing practical purchase options, a team must handle 59 requirements. The stated completion limit is 32 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 13 requirements, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 7 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
