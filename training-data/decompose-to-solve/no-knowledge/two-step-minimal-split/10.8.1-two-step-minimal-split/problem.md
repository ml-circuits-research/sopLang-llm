# 10.8.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a civic open-data release, a team must handle 46 data sets. The stated completion limit is 39 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 16 data sets, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 7 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
