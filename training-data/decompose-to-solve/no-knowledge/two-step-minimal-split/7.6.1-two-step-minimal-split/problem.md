# 7.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In evaluating a general, non-personal health claim, a team must handle 105 study findings. The stated completion limit is 61 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 study findings, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 15 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
