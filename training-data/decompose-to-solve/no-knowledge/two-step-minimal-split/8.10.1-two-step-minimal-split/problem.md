# 8.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In diagnosing a service incident, a team must handle 122 service components. The stated completion limit is 64 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 service components, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
