# 5.7.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In allocating land among competing uses, a team must handle 99 land parcels. The stated completion limit is 86 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 11 land parcels, and each block takes 7 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
