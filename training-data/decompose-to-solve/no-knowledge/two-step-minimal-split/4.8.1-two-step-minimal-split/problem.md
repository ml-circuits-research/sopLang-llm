# 4.8.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning an introductory astronomy observation, a team must handle 49 observations. The stated completion limit is 49 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 observations, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 9 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
