# 7.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In reconstructing a non-political event from reports, a team must handle 106 reports. The stated completion limit is 37 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 reports, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 7 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
