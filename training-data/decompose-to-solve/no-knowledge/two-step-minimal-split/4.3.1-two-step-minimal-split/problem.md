# 4.3.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning a low-voltage electrical load, a team must handle 96 electrical loads. The stated completion limit is 52 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 16 electrical loads, and each block takes 4 minutes. A one-time setup takes 16 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 5 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
