# 3.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In estimating household electricity use, a team must handle 74 energy-consuming devices. The stated completion limit is 26 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 energy-consuming devices, and each block takes 4 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
