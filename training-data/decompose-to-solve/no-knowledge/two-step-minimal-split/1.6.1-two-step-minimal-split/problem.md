# 1.6.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In organizing a small community event, a team must handle 79 event tasks. The stated completion limit is 62 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 12 event tasks, and each block takes 6 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
