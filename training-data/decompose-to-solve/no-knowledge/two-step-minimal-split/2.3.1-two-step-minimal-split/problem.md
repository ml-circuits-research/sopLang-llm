# 2.3.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In planning seasonal farm work, a team must handle 49 field tasks. The stated completion limit is 46 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 15 field tasks, and each block takes 4 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 9 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
