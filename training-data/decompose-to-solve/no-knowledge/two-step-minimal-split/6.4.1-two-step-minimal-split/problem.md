# 6.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In analyzing a simplified representation system, a team must handle 122 votes or seats. The stated completion limit is 132 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 8 votes or seats, and each block takes 7 minutes. A one-time setup takes 8 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
