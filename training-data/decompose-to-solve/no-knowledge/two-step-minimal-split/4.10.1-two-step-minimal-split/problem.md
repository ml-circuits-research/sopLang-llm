# 4.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In combining repeated measurements, a team must handle 56 measurements. The stated completion limit is 47 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 17 measurements, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 11 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
