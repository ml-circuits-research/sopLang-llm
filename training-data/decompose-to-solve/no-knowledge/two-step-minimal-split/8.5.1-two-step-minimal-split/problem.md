# 8.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In moving data through several file formats, a team must handle 94 records. The stated completion limit is 40 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 records, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 7 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 4 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
