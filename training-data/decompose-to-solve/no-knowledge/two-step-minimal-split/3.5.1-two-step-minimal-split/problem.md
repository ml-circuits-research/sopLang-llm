# 3.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In comparing a constrained household budget, a team must handle 73 budget items. The stated completion limit is 52 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 10 budget items, and each block takes 4 minutes. A one-time setup takes 15 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
