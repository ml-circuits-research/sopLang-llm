# 10.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In launching a small manufacturing line, a team must handle 60 production batches. The stated completion limit is 68 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 11 production batches, and each block takes 8 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
