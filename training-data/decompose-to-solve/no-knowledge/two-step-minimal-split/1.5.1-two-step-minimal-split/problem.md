# 1.5.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In choosing between service plans with different constraints, a team must handle 104 service units. The stated completion limit is 86 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 9 service units, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 6 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
