# 9.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In comparing suppliers and delivery options, a team must handle 54 orders. The stated completion limit is 49 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 13 orders, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 10 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
