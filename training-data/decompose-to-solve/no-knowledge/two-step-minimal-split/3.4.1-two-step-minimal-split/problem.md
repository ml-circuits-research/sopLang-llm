# 3.4.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In scaling food portions and label quantities, a team must handle 105 servings. The stated completion limit is 73 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 17 servings, and each block takes 7 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 8 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
