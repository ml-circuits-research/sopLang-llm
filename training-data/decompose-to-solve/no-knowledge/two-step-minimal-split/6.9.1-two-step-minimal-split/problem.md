# 6.9.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In reconstructing the provenance of an artifact, a team must handle 111 records. The stated completion limit is 53 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 11 records, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 12 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
