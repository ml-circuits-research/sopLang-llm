# 8.1.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In diagnosing or planning a small computer network, a team must handle 78 network links. The stated completion limit is 38 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 14 network links, and each block takes 3 minutes. A one-time setup takes 14 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 2 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
