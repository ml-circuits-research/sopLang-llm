# 3.10.1 — Two-Step Minimal Split

Scenario. In estimating material and recycling flows, a team must handle 107 waste units. The stated completion limit is 65 minutes. Each processing block can handle at most 8 waste units, and each block takes 5 minutes. A one-time setup takes 5 minutes. A separate briefing mentions 3 staff roles, but staffing does not change the stated block rate. The facts are intentionally out of solving order.

Main question. Can the stated workload be completed within the limit under the given operating rule? Best decomposition (2 subproblems).
