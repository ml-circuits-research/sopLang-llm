# 9.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a small project, two configurations are being compared for the same project tasks. A costs 122 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 108 units, takes 37 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
