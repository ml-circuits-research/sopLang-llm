# 2.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 134 units, takes 42 minutes, and scores 73/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
