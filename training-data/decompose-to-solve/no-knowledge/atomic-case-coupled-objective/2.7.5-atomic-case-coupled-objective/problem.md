# 2.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In building a defensible historical timeline, two configurations are being compared for the same dated records. A costs 82 units, takes 39 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 89/100. B costs 102 units, takes 62 minutes, and scores 74/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
