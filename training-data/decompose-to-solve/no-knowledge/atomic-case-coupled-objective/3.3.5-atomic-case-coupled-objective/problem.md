# 3.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In estimating travel fuel or energy needs, two configurations are being compared for the same journey kilometres. A costs 76 units, takes 72 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 84/100. B costs 73 units, takes 75 minutes, and scores 81/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
