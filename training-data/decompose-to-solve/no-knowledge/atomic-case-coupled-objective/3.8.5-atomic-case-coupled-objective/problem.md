# 3.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a print run, two configurations are being compared for the same printed copies. A costs 94 units, takes 61 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 70/100. B costs 104 units, takes 48 minutes, and scores 92/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
