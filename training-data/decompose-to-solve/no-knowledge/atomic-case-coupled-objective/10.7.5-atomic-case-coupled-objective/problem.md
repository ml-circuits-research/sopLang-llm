# 10.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a heritage-building restoration, two configurations are being compared for the same restoration tasks. A costs 124 units, takes 35 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 75/100. B costs 86 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 72/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
