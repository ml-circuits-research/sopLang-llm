# 3.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In estimating household electricity use, two configurations are being compared for the same energy-consuming devices. A costs 86 units, takes 62 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 84/100. B costs 106 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 73/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
