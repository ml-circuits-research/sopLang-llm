# 3.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In estimating household electricity use, two configurations are being compared for the same energy-consuming devices. A costs 86 units, takes 62 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 84/100. B costs 106 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 73/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
