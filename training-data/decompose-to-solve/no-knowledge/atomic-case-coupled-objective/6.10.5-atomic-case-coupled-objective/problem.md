# 6.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In mapping responsibilities in an international cooperation case, two configurations are being compared for the same institutional commitments. A costs 127 units, takes 46 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 85/100. B costs 112 units, takes 54 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
