# 8.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In moving data through several file formats, two configurations are being compared for the same records. A costs 131 units, takes 57 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 89/100. B costs 72 units, takes 73 minutes, and scores 80/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
