# 1.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In household faults and maintenance observations, two configurations are being compared for the same observations. A costs 101 units, takes 74 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 71 units, takes 47 minutes, and scores 76/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
