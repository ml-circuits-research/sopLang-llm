# 5.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In comparing urban travel modes, two configurations are being compared for the same urban trips. A costs 83 units, takes 69 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 92/100. B costs 116 units, takes 55 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
