# 5.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In comparing urban travel modes, two configurations are being compared for the same urban trips. A costs 83 units, takes 69 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 92/100. B costs 116 units, takes 55 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
