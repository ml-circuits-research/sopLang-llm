# 7.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

A costs 73 units, takes 69 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 83/100. B costs 115 units, takes 35 minutes, and scores 88/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
