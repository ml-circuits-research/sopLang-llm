# 4.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In comparing heat-loss or insulation choices, two configurations are being compared for the same thermal observations. A costs 95 units, takes 70 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 73/100. B costs 87 units, takes 75 minutes, and scores 79/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
