# 2.4.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In installing a museum exhibition, two configurations are being compared for the same exhibition components. A costs 106 units, takes 50 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 85/100. B costs 85 units, takes 37 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
