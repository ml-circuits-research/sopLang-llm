# 7.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In evaluating an environmental claim from mixed evidence, two configurations are being compared for the same measurements. A costs 130 units, takes 57 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 90/100. B costs 140 units, takes 71 minutes, and scores 91/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
