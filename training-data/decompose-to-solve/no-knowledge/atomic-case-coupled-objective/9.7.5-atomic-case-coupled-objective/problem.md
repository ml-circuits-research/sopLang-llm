# 9.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 137 units, takes 40 minutes, and scores 91/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
