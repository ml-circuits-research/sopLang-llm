# 4.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In checking a simple material or structural design, two configurations are being compared for the same structural elements. A costs 78 units, takes 55 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 86 units, takes 49 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
