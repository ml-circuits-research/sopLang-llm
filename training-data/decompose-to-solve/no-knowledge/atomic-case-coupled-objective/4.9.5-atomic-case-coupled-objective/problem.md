# 4.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In checking a simple material or structural design, two configurations are being compared for the same structural elements. A costs 78 units, takes 55 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 86 units, takes 49 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
