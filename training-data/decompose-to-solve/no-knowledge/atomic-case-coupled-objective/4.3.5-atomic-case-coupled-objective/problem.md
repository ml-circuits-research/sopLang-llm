# 4.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a low-voltage electrical load, two configurations are being compared for the same electrical loads. A costs 147 units, takes 65 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 96/100. B costs 71 units, takes 38 minutes, and scores 82/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
