# 3.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 121 units, takes 74 minutes, and scores 90/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
