# 3.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In estimating material and recycling flows, two configurations are being compared for the same waste units. A costs 78 units, takes 37 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 84/100. B costs 121 units, takes 74 minutes, and scores 90/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
