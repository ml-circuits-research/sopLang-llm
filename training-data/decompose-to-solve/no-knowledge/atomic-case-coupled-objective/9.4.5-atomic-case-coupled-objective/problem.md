# 9.4.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 104 units, takes 41 minutes, and scores 81/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
