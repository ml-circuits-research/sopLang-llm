# 7.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In reconstructing a non-political event from reports, two configurations are being compared for the same reports. A costs 126 units, takes 65 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 87/100. B costs 129 units, takes 40 minutes, and scores 95/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
