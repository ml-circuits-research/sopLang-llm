# 9.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In planning a learning sequence, two configurations are being compared for the same learning units. A costs 104 units, takes 72 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 88/100. B costs 109 units, takes 47 minutes, and scores 84/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
