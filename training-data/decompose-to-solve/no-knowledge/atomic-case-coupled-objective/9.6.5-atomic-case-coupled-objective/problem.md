# 9.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a learning sequence, two configurations are being compared for the same learning units. A costs 104 units, takes 72 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 88/100. B costs 109 units, takes 47 minutes, and scores 84/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
