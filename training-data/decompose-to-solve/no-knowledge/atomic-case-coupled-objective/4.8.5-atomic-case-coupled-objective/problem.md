# 4.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning an introductory astronomy observation, two configurations are being compared for the same observations. A costs 143 units, takes 62 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 89/100. B costs 117 units, takes 40 minutes, and scores 90/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
