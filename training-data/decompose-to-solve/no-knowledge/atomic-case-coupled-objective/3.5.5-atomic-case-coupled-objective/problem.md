# 3.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In comparing a constrained household budget, two configurations are being compared for the same budget items. A costs 144 units, takes 52 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 80/100. B costs 71 units, takes 44 minutes, and scores 80/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
