# 7.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In evaluating several sources about the same claim, two configurations are being compared for the same sources. A costs 102 units, takes 49 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 76/100. B costs 71 units, takes 38 minutes, and scores 86/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
