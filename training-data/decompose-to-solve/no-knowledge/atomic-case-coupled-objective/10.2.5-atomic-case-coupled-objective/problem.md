# 10.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In planning a museum expansion and collection move, two configurations are being compared for the same museum work packages. A costs 134 units, takes 46 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 95/100. B costs 104 units, takes 59 minutes, and scores 86/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
