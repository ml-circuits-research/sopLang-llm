# 10.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a museum expansion and collection move, two configurations are being compared for the same museum work packages. A costs 134 units, takes 46 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 95/100. B costs 104 units, takes 59 minutes, and scores 86/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
