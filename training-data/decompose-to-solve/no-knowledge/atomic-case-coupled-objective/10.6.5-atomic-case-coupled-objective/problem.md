# 10.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In planning an educational international trip, two configurations are being compared for the same travel activities. A costs 127 units, takes 40 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 77/100. B costs 74 units, takes 37 minutes, and scores 84/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
