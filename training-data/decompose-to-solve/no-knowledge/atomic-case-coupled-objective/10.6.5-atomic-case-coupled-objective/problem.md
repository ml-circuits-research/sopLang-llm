# 10.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning an educational international trip, two configurations are being compared for the same travel activities. A costs 127 units, takes 40 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 77/100. B costs 74 units, takes 37 minutes, and scores 84/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
