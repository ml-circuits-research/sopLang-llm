# 6.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In analyzing exchange along a historical trade network, two configurations are being compared for the same trade links. A costs 99 units, takes 47 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 91/100. B costs 93 units, takes 62 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
