# 10.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In coordinating a conference and publication workflow, two configurations are being compared for the same submissions. A costs 113 units, takes 48 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 98 units, takes 47 minutes, and scores 87/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
