# 10.4.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a fictional training exercise in relief distribution, two configurations are being compared for the same relief deliveries. A costs 145 units, takes 38 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 95/100. B costs 91 units, takes 42 minutes, and scores 80/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
