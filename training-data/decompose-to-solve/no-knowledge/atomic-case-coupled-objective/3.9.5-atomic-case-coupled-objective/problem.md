# 3.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a non-medical sports training schedule, two configurations are being compared for the same training intervals. A costs 85 units, takes 39 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 72/100. B costs 100 units, takes 53 minutes, and scores 87/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
