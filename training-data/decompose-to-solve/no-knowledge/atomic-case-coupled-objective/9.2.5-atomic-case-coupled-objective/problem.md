# 9.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In designing a communication chain, two configurations are being compared for the same messages. A costs 73 units, takes 69 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 78/100. B costs 89 units, takes 36 minutes, and scores 86/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
