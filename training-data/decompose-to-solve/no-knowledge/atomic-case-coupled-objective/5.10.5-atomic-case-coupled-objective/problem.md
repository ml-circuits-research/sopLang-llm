# 5.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a low-impact visit or conservation activity, two configurations are being compared for the same site zones. A costs 134 units, takes 73 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 70/100. B costs 145 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 95/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
