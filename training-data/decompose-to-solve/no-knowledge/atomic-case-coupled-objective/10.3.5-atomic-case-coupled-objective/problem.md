# 10.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a multidisciplinary field expedition, two configurations are being compared for the same field activities. A costs 121 units, takes 51 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 78 units, takes 61 minutes, and scores 80/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
