# 5.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 140 units, takes 54 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
