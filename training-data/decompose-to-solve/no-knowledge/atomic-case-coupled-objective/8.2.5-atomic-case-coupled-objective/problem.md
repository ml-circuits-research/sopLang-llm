# 8.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In designing a basic authentication flow, two configurations are being compared for the same authentication steps. A costs 83 units, takes 41 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 77/100. B costs 77 units, takes 43 minutes, and scores 88/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
