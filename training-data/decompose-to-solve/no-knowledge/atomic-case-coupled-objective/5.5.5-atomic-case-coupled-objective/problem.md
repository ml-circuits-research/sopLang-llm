# 5.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning an elevation-sensitive route, two configurations are being compared for the same route segments. A costs 128 units, takes 70 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 83/100. B costs 123 units, takes 35 minutes, and scores 89/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
