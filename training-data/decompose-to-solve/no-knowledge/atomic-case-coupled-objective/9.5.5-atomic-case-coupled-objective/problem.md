# 9.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In checking a batch or service process, two configurations are being compared for the same samples. A costs 86 units, takes 65 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 70/100. B costs 77 units, takes 35 minutes, and scores 90/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
