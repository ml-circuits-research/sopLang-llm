# 1.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In managing a queue with limited service capacity, two configurations are being compared for the same customers. A costs 88 units, takes 42 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 77/100. B costs 118 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
