# 7.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In evaluating a general, non-personal health claim, two configurations are being compared for the same study findings. A costs 75 units, takes 60 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 88/100. B costs 84 units, takes 53 minutes, and scores 95/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
