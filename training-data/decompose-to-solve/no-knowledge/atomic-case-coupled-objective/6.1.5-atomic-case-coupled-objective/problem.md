# 6.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In constructing a historical narrative from dated evidence, two configurations are being compared for the same events. A costs 112 units, takes 49 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 81/100. B costs 81 units, takes 52 minutes, and scores 75/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
