# 7.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In evaluating a causal claim from observational data, two configurations are being compared for the same observations. A costs 121 units, takes 65 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 75/100. B costs 92 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 86/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
