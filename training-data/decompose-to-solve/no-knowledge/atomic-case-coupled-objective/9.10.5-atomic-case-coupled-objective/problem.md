# 9.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning routine safety controls, two configurations are being compared for the same hazards. A costs 130 units, takes 75 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 94/100. B costs 76 units, takes 44 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
