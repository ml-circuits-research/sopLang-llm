# 5.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In reasoning about a river catchment, two configurations are being compared for the same catchment observations. A costs 107 units, takes 60 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 75/100. B costs 89 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 75/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
