# 2.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In sequencing a small renovation project, two configurations are being compared for the same construction tasks. A costs 72 units, takes 45 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 78/100. B costs 140 units, takes 75 minutes, and scores 79/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
