# 8.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In diagnosing or planning a small computer network, two configurations are being compared for the same network links. A costs 109 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 88/100. B costs 97 units, takes 71 minutes, and scores 79/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
