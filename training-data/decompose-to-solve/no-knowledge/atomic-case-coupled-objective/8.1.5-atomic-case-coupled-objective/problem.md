# 8.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In diagnosing or planning a small computer network, two configurations are being compared for the same network links. A costs 109 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 88/100. B costs 97 units, takes 71 minutes, and scores 79/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
