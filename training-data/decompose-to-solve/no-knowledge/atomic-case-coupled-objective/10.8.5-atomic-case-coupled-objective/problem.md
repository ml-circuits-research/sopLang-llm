# 10.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a civic open-data release, two configurations are being compared for the same data sets. A costs 81 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 91/100. B costs 76 units, takes 73 minutes, and scores 84/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
