# 10.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a community renewable-energy project, two configurations are being compared for the same project components. A costs 122 units, takes 44 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 72/100. B costs 120 units, takes 43 minutes, and scores 76/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
