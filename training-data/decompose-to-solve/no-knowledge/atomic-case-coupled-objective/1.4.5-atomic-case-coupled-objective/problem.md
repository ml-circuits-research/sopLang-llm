# 1.4.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, two configurations are being compared for the same scheduled tasks. A costs 135 units, takes 41 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 82/100. B costs 114 units, takes 70 minutes, and scores 75/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
