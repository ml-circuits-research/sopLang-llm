# 9.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In mapping consequences for several stakeholders, two configurations are being compared for the same stakeholder effects. A costs 75 units, takes 39 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 81/100. B costs 86 units, takes 75 minutes, and scores 94/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
