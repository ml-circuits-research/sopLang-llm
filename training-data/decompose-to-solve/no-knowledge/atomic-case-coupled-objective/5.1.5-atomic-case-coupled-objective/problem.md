# 5.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In using a map to plan a route or field survey, two configurations are being compared for the same map segments. A costs 90 units, takes 57 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 127 units, takes 67 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
