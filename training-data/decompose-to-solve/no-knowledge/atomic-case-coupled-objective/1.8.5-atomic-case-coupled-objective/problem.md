# 1.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In packing for a trip under weight and volume limits, two configurations are being compared for the same packed items. A costs 109 units, takes 54 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 79/100. B costs 88 units, takes 69 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
