# 1.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In packing for a trip under weight and volume limits, two configurations are being compared for the same packed items. A costs 109 units, takes 54 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 79/100. B costs 88 units, takes 69 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
