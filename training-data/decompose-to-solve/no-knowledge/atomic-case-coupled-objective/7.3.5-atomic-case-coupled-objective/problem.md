# 7.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In interpreting a public survey, two configurations are being compared for the same responses. A costs 105 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 102 units, takes 58 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
