# 7.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In interpreting a public survey, two configurations are being compared for the same responses. A costs 105 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 102 units, takes 58 minutes, and scores 78/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
