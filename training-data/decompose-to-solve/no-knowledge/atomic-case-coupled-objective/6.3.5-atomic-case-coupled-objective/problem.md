# 6.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In comparing institutional responsibilities in a generic constitutional system, two configurations are being compared for the same institutional actions. A costs 96 units, takes 63 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 86/100. B costs 150 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 83/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
