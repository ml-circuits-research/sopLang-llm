# 6.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In interpreting phases of a historic building, two configurations are being compared for the same building features. A costs 73 units, takes 71 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 95/100. B costs 107 units, takes 67 minutes, and scores 95/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
