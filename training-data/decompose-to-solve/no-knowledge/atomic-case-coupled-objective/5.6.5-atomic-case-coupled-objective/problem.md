# 5.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning an activity near a coast, two configurations are being compared for the same coastal observations. A costs 110 units, takes 42 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 86/100. B costs 142 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 71/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
