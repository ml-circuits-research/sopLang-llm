# 3.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In managing a small inventory, two configurations are being compared for the same stock units. A costs 89 units, takes 51 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 96/100. B costs 106 units, takes 43 minutes, and scores 72/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
