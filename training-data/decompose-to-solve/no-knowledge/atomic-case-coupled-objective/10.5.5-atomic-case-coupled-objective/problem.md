# 10.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In launching a small manufacturing line, two configurations are being compared for the same production batches. A costs 78 units, takes 40 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 87/100. B costs 76 units, takes 53 minutes, and scores 81/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
