# 8.8.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In reasoning about encryption, signatures, and key handling, two configurations are being compared for the same messages. A costs 71 units, takes 61 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 89/100. B costs 114 units, takes 70 minutes, and scores 91/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
