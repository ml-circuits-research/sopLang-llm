# 2.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 123 units, takes 67 minutes, and scores 80/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
