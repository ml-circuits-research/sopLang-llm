# 2.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a multi-leg rail or air journey, two configurations are being compared for the same connections. A costs 108 units, takes 54 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 85/100. B costs 111 units, takes 40 minutes, and scores 82/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
