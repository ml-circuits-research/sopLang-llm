# 1.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In organizing a small community event, two configurations are being compared for the same event tasks. A costs 113 units, takes 53 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 90/100. B costs 124 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 71/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
