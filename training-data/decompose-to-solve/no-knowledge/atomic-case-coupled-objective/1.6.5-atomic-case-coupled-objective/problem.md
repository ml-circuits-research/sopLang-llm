# 1.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In organizing a small community event, two configurations are being compared for the same event tasks. A costs 113 units, takes 53 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 90/100. B costs 124 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 71/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
