# 1.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In a local public-transport journey, two configurations are being compared for the same journey legs. A costs 94 units, takes 54 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 110 units, takes 67 minutes, and scores 94/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
