# 4.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In analyzing a simple motion or load case, two configurations are being compared for the same measurements. A costs 77 units, takes 39 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 81/100. B costs 96 units, takes 64 minutes, and scores 74/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
