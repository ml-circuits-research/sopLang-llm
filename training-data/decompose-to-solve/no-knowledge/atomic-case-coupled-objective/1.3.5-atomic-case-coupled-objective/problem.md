# 1.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In preparing food for a group, two configurations are being compared for the same portions. A costs 136 units, takes 70 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 146 units, takes 58 minutes, and scores 94/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
