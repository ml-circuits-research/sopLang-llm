# 1.3.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In preparing food for a group, two configurations are being compared for the same portions. A costs 136 units, takes 70 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 93/100. B costs 146 units, takes 58 minutes, and scores 94/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
