# 1.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In choosing between service plans with different constraints, two configurations are being compared for the same service units. A costs 123 units, takes 41 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 74/100. B costs 96 units, takes 42 minutes, and scores 76/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
