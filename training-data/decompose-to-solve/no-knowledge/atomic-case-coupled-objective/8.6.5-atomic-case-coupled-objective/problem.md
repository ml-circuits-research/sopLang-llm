# 8.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

B costs 137 units, takes 66 minutes, and scores 82/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
