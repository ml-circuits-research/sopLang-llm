# 7.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In combining results from several experiments, two configurations are being compared for the same experimental results. A costs 79 units, takes 61 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 81/100. B costs 100 units, takes 68 minutes, and scores 77/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
