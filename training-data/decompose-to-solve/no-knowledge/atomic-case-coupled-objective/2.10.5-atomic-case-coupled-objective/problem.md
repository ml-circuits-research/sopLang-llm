# 2.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a staged evacuation exercise, two configurations are being compared for the same evacuation groups. A costs 93 units, takes 65 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 71/100. B costs 92 units, takes 59 minutes, and scores 93/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
