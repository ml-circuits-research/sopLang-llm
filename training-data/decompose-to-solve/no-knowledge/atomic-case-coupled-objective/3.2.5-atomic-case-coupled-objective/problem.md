# 3.2.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning water use and storage, two configurations are being compared for the same litres of water. A costs 70 units, takes 66 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 77/100. B costs 100 units, takes 65 minutes, and scores 93/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
