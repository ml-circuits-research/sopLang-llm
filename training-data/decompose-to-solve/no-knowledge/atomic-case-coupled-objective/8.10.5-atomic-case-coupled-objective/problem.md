# 8.10.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In diagnosing a service incident, two configurations are being compared for the same service components. A costs 133 units, takes 62 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 87/100. B costs 75 units, takes 42 minutes, and scores 92/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
