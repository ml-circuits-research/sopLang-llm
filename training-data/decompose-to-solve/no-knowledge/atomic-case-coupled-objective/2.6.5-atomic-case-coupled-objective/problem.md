# 2.6.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In preparing a software release, two configurations are being compared for the same release steps. A costs 140 units, takes 74 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 70/100. B costs 70 units, takes 37 minutes, and scores 81/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
