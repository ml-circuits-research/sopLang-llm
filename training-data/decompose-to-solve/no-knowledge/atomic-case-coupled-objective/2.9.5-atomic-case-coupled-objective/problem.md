# 2.9.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In coordinating procurement and delivery, two configurations are being compared for the same shipments. A costs 100 units, takes 44 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 96/100. B costs 134 units, takes 47 minutes, and scores 81/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
