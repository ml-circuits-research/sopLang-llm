# 7.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In reasoning about a probabilistic decision, two configurations are being compared for the same cases. A costs 104 units, takes 49 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 95/100. B costs 83 units, takes 39 minutes, and scores 74/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 2×time − 2×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
