# 10.1.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In planning a city festival with transport, energy, waste, and scheduling constraints, two configurations are being compared for the same festival operations. A costs 108 units, takes 73 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 73/100. B costs 107 units, takes 70 minutes, and scores 93/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
