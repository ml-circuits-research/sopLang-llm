# 4.5.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

Scenario. In preparing and checking a laboratory solution, two configurations are being compared for the same solution portions. A costs 149 units, takes 70 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 74/100. B costs 84 units, takes 36 minutes, and scores 96/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1.5×time − 3×quality. Colleagues propose solving “the cost problem,” “the time problem,” and “the quality problem” separately and then voting among their winners.

Main question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule? Best formulation (1 indivisible core). The apparent strands are clauses of one coupled model; splitting them would produce partial statements that must immediately be recombined before they mean anything.
