# 1.7.5 — Atomic Case: Coupled Objective

In following a multi-step administrative procedure, two configurations are being compared for the same documents. A costs 116 units, takes 35 minutes, and has an assessed quality score of 86/100. B costs 75 units, takes 44 minutes, and scores 88/100. The decision rule was fixed in advance: minimize S = cost + 1×time − 3×quality.

Question. What is the correct decomposition, and which configuration wins under the stated rule?
