# 1.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In choosing between service plans with different constraints, expected demand is 97 service units, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 128 for cost 127; Option B provides capacity 148 for cost 155. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
