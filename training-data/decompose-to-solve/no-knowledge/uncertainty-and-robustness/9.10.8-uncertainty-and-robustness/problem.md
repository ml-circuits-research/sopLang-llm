# 9.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning routine safety controls, expected demand is 87 hazards, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 87 for cost 91; Option B provides capacity 116 for cost 140. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
