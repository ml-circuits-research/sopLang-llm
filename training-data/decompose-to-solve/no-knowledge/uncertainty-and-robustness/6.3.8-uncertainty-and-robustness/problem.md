# 6.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing institutional responsibilities in a generic constitutional system, expected demand is 125 institutional actions, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 160 for cost 102; Option B provides capacity 190 for cost 131. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
