# 9.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In allocating work across a small team, expected demand is 136 work packages, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 152 for cost 81; Option B provides capacity 182 for cost 123. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
