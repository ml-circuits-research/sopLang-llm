# 1.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In packing for a trip under weight and volume limits, expected demand is 119 packed items, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 156 for cost 90; Option B provides capacity 187 for cost 123. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
