# 2.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In scheduling sessions and shared rooms, expected demand is 91 sessions, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 92 for cost 131; Option B provides capacity 134 for cost 162. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
