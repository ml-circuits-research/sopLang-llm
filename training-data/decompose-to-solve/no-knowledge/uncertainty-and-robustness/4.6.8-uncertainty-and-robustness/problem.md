# 4.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In analyzing an ecosystem interaction, expected demand is 67 organism groups, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 71 for cost 112; Option B provides capacity 92 for cost 124. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
