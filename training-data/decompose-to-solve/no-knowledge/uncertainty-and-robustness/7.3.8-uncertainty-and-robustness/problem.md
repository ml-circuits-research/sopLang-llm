# 7.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In interpreting a public survey, expected demand is 61 responses, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 67 for cost 117; Option B provides capacity 93 for cost 138. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
