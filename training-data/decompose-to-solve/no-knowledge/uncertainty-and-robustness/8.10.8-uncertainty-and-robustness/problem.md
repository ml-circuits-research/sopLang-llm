# 8.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In diagnosing a service incident, expected demand is 90 service components, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 115 for cost 98; Option B provides capacity 113 for cost 122. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
