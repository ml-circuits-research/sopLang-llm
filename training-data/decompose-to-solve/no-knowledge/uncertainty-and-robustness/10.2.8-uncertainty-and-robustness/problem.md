# 10.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a museum expansion and collection move, expected demand is 103 museum work packages, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 109 for cost 80; Option B provides capacity 163 for cost 109. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
