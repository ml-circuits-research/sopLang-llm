# 7.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In evaluating a causal claim from observational data, expected demand is 112 observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 132 for cost 104; Option B provides capacity 176 for cost 122. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
