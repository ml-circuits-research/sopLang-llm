# 7.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In evaluating a general, non-personal health claim, expected demand is 90 study findings, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 106 for cost 126; Option B provides capacity 137 for cost 147. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
