# 7.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reasoning about a probabilistic decision, expected demand is 111 cases, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 142 for cost 102; Option B provides capacity 173 for cost 125. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
