# 6.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In curating objects from different artistic periods, expected demand is 64 artworks, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 66 for cost 106; Option B provides capacity 101 for cost 120. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
