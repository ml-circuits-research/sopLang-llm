# 5.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In interpreting seasonal climate information, expected demand is 121 climate observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 151 for cost 82; Option B provides capacity 177 for cost 102. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
