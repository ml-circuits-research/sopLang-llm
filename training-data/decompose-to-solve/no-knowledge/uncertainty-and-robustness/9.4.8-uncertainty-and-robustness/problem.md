# 9.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing suppliers and delivery options, expected demand is 83 orders, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 108 for cost 140; Option B provides capacity 120 for cost 166. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
