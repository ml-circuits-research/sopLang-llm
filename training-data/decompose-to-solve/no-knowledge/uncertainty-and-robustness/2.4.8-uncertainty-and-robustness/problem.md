# 2.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In installing a museum exhibition, expected demand is 83 exhibition components, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 86 for cost 124; Option B provides capacity 129 for cost 156. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
