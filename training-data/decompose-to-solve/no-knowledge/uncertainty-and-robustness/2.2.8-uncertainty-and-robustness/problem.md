# 2.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In sequencing a small renovation project, expected demand is 111 construction tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 116 for cost 126; Option B provides capacity 159 for cost 168. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
