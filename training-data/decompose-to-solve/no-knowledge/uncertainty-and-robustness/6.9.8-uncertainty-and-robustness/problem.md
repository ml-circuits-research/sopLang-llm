# 6.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reconstructing the provenance of an artifact, expected demand is 63 records, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 77 for cost 106; Option B provides capacity 83 for cost 149. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
