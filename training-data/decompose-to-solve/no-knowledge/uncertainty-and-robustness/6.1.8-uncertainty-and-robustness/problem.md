# 6.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In constructing a historical narrative from dated evidence, expected demand is 97 events, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 110 for cost 92; Option B provides capacity 142 for cost 138. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
