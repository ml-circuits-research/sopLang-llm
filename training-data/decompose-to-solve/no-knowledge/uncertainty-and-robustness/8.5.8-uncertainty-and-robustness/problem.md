# 8.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In moving data through several file formats, expected demand is 124 records, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 148 for cost 114; Option B provides capacity 173 for cost 157. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
