# 3.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In estimating household electricity use, expected demand is 113 energy-consuming devices, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 127 for cost 100; Option B provides capacity 167 for cost 148. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
