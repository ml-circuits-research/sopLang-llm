# 1.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In managing a queue with limited service capacity, expected demand is 81 customers, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 83 for cost 97; Option B provides capacity 117 for cost 126. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
