# 4.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a low-voltage electrical load, expected demand is 103 electrical loads, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 135 for cost 96; Option B provides capacity 138 for cost 136. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
