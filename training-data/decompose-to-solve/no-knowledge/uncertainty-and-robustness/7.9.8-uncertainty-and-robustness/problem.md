# 7.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reconstructing a non-political event from reports, expected demand is 118 reports, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 122 for cost 101; Option B provides capacity 174 for cost 150. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
