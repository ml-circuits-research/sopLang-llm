# 9.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a small project, expected demand is 102 project tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 125 for cost 126; Option B provides capacity 129 for cost 174. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
