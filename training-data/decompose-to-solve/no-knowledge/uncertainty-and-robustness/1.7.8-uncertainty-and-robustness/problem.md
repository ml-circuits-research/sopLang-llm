# 1.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In following a multi-step administrative procedure, expected demand is 95 documents, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 119 for cost 134; Option B provides capacity 129 for cost 172. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
