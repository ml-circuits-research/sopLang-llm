# 2.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a staged evacuation exercise, expected demand is 115 evacuation groups, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 132 for cost 136; Option B provides capacity 172 for cost 157. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
