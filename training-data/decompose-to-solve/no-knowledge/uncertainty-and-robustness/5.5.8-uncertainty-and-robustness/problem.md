# 5.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning an elevation-sensitive route, expected demand is 68 route segments, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 89 for cost 125; Option B provides capacity 104 for cost 167. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
