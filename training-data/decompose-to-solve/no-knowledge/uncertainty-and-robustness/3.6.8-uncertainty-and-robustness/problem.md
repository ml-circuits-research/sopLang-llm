# 3.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In managing a small inventory, expected demand is 74 stock units, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 89 for cost 103; Option B provides capacity 111 for cost 134. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
