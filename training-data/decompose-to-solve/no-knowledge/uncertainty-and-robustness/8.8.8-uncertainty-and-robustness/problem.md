# 8.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reasoning about encryption, signatures, and key handling, expected demand is 112 messages, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 143 for cost 133; Option B provides capacity 176 for cost 165. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
