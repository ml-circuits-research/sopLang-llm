# 10.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning an educational international trip, expected demand is 93 travel activities, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 121 for cost 103; Option B provides capacity 130 for cost 130. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
