# 5.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In allocating land among competing uses, expected demand is 98 land parcels, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 124 for cost 111; Option B provides capacity 140 for cost 129. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
