# 10.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a fictional training exercise in relief distribution, expected demand is 135 relief deliveries, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 149 for cost 114; Option B provides capacity 210 for cost 138. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
