# 8.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In evaluating a small AI-assisted workflow, expected demand is 92 test cases, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 97 for cost 87; Option B provides capacity 132 for cost 105. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
