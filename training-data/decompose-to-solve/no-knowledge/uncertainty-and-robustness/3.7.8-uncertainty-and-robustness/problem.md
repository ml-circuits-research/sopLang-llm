# 3.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In interpreting population and area data, expected demand is 62 population groups, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 71 for cost 123; Option B provides capacity 81 for cost 163. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
