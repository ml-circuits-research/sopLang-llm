# 7.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In evaluating an environmental claim from mixed evidence, expected demand is 74 measurements, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 96 for cost 138; Option B provides capacity 105 for cost 171. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
