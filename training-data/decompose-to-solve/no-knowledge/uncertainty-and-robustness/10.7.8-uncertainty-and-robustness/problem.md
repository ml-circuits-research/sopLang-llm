# 10.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a heritage-building restoration, expected demand is 72 restoration tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 89 for cost 100; Option B provides capacity 108 for cost 132. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
