# 4.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In analyzing a simple motion or load case, expected demand is 124 measurements, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 139 for cost 95; Option B provides capacity 183 for cost 141. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
