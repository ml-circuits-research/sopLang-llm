# 4.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In interpreting geological observations, expected demand is 101 field observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 133 for cost 101; Option B provides capacity 126 for cost 147. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
