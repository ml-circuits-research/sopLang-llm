# 10.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In launching a small manufacturing line, expected demand is 120 production batches, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 130 for cost 108; Option B provides capacity 159 for cost 124. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
