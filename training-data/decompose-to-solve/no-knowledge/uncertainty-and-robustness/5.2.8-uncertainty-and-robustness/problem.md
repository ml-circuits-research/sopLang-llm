# 5.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reasoning about a river catchment, expected demand is 93 catchment observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 106 for cost 125; Option B provides capacity 130 for cost 160. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
