# 3.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In estimating material and recycling flows, expected demand is 115 waste units, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 154 for cost 89; Option B provides capacity 143 for cost 105. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
