# 8.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In diagnosing or planning a small computer network, expected demand is 135 network links, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 173 for cost 124; Option B provides capacity 214 for cost 164. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
