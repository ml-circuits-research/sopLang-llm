# 5.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning an activity near a coast, expected demand is 108 coastal observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 144 for cost 86; Option B provides capacity 153 for cost 108. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
