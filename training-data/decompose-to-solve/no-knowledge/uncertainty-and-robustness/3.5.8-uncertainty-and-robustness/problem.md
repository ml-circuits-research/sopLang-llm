# 3.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing a constrained household budget, expected demand is 109 budget items, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 114 for cost 87; Option B provides capacity 170 for cost 111. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
