# 4.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In checking a simple material or structural design, expected demand is 140 structural elements, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 147 for cost 112; Option B provides capacity 185 for cost 128. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
