# 10.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a multidisciplinary field expedition, expected demand is 138 field activities, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 184 for cost 128; Option B provides capacity 178 for cost 150. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
