# 3.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a non-medical sports training schedule, expected demand is 93 training intervals, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 103 for cost 127; Option B provides capacity 133 for cost 142. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
