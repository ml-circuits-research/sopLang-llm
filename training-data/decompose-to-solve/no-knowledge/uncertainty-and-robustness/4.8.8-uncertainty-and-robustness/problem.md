# 4.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning an introductory astronomy observation, expected demand is 75 observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 87 for cost 127; Option B provides capacity 111 for cost 169. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
