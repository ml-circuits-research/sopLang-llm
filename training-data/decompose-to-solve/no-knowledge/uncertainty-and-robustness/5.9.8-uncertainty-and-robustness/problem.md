# 5.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In interpreting a basic hazard scenario, expected demand is 83 hazard observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 106 for cost 113; Option B provides capacity 109 for cost 143. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
