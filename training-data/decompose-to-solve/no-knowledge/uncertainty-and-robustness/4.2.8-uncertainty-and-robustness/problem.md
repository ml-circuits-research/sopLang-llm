# 4.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing heat-loss or insulation choices, expected demand is 69 thermal observations, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 75 for cost 117; Option B provides capacity 92 for cost 140. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
