# 7.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In evaluating several sources about the same claim, expected demand is 116 sources, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 147 for cost 117; Option B provides capacity 175 for cost 136. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
