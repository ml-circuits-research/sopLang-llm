# 9.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In checking a batch or service process, expected demand is 103 samples, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 128 for cost 128; Option B provides capacity 143 for cost 171. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
