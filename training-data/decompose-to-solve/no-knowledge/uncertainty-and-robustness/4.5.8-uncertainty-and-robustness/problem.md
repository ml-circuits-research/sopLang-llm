# 4.5.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In preparing and checking a laboratory solution, expected demand is 131 solution portions, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 171 for cost 107; Option B provides capacity 183 for cost 138. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
