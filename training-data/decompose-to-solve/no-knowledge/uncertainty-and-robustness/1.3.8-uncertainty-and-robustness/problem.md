# 1.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In preparing food for a group, expected demand is 72 portions, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 88 for cost 110; Option B provides capacity 99 for cost 156. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
