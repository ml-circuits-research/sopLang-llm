# 6.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a multilingual translation workflow, expected demand is 97 text segments, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 121 for cost 86; Option B provides capacity 146 for cost 131. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
