# 1.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In organizing a small community event, expected demand is 100 event tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 102 for cost 135; Option B provides capacity 131 for cost 166. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
