# 5.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a low-impact visit or conservation activity, expected demand is 79 site zones, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 90 for cost 129; Option B provides capacity 123 for cost 175. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
