# 10.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a community renewable-energy project, expected demand is 127 project components, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 153 for cost 85; Option B provides capacity 200 for cost 117. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
