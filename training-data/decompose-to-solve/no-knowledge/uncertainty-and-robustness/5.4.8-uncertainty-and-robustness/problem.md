# 5.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning communication across time zones, expected demand is 106 time-zone events, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 139 for cost 135; Option B provides capacity 167 for cost 147. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
