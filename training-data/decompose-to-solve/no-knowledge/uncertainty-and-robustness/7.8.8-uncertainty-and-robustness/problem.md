# 7.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing primary and secondary historical sources, expected demand is 79 source statements, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 105 for cost 134; Option B provides capacity 121 for cost 171. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
