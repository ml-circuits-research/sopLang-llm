# 2.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a multi-leg rail or air journey, expected demand is 88 connections, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 113 for cost 107; Option B provides capacity 128 for cost 137. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
