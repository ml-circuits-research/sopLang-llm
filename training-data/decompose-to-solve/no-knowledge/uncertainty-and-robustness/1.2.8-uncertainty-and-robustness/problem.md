# 1.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In a local public-transport journey, expected demand is 77 journey legs, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 87 for cost 124; Option B provides capacity 94 for cost 138. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
