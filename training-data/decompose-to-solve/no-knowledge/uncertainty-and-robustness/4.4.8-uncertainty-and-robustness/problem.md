# 4.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning an optics demonstration, expected demand is 113 optical elements, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 117 for cost 94; Option B provides capacity 159 for cost 125. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
