# 1.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In coordinating a day of appointments and tasks, expected demand is 112 scheduled tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 140 for cost 119; Option B provides capacity 148 for cost 158. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
