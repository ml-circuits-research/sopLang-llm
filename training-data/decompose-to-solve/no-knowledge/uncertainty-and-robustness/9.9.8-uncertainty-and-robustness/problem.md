# 9.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In mapping consequences for several stakeholders, expected demand is 98 stakeholder effects, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 113 for cost 140; Option B provides capacity 148 for cost 154. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
