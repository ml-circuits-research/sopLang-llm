# 10.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a civic open-data release, expected demand is 116 data sets, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 129 for cost 99; Option B provides capacity 155 for cost 137. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
