# 8.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In resolving software dependency constraints, expected demand is 104 packages, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 107 for cost 96; Option B provides capacity 147 for cost 135. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
