# 5.8.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing urban travel modes, expected demand is 139 urban trips, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 155 for cost 116; Option B provides capacity 186 for cost 128. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
