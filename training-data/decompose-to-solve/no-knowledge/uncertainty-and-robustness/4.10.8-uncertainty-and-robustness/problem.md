# 4.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In combining repeated measurements, expected demand is 104 measurements, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 135 for cost 115; Option B provides capacity 144 for cost 150. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
