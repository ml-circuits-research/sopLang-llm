# 3.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In scaling food portions and label quantities, expected demand is 85 servings, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 111 for cost 135; Option B provides capacity 113 for cost 159. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
