# 1.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In comparing practical purchase options, expected demand is 95 requirements, but forecast error is estimated at ±12%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 122 for cost 96; Option B provides capacity 123 for cost 130. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
