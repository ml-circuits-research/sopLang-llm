# 9.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning non-clinical public-health logistics, expected demand is 88 supply units, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 90 for cost 107; Option B provides capacity 130 for cost 119. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
