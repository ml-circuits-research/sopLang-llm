# 2.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning seasonal farm work, expected demand is 78 field tasks, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 79 for cost 120; Option B provides capacity 113 for cost 166. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
