# 2.9.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In coordinating procurement and delivery, expected demand is 60 shipments, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 62 for cost 130; Option B provides capacity 84 for cost 140. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
