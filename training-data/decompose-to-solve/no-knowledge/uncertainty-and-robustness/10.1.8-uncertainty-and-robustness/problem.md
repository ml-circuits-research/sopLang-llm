# 10.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning a city festival with transport, energy, waste, and scheduling constraints, expected demand is 92 festival operations, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 106 for cost 83; Option B provides capacity 113 for cost 104. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
