# 8.7.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In assigning permissions in a small organization, expected demand is 83 permissions, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 99 for cost 120; Option B provides capacity 112 for cost 167. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
