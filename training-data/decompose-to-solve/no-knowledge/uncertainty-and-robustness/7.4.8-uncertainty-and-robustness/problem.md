# 7.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In reading and checking a quantitative chart, expected demand is 87 data points, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 92 for cost 129; Option B provides capacity 119 for cost 164. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
