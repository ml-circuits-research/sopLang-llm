# 3.3.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In estimating travel fuel or energy needs, expected demand is 76 journey kilometres, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 92 for cost 117; Option B provides capacity 114 for cost 132. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
