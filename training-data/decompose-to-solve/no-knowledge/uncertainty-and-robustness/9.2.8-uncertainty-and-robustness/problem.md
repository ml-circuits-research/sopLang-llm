# 9.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In designing a communication chain, expected demand is 89 messages, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 102 for cost 123; Option B provides capacity 131 for cost 139. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
