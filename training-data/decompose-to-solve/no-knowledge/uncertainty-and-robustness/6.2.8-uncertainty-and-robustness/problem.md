# 6.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In analyzing exchange along a historical trade network, expected demand is 128 trade links, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 137 for cost 139; Option B provides capacity 167 for cost 162. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
