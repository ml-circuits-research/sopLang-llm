# 3.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In planning water use and storage, expected demand is 61 litres of water, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 8% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 79 for cost 100; Option B provides capacity 90 for cost 134. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
