# 2.6.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In preparing a software release, expected demand is 87 release steps, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 109 for cost 109; Option B provides capacity 123 for cost 136. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
