# 8.2.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In designing a basic authentication flow, expected demand is 95 authentication steps, but forecast error is estimated at ±20%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 125 for cost 110; Option B provides capacity 145 for cost 141. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
