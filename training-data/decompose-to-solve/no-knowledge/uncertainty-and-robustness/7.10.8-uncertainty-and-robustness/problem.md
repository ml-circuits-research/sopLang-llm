# 7.10.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In combining results from several experiments, expected demand is 106 experimental results, but forecast error is estimated at ±8%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 122 for cost 84; Option B provides capacity 142 for cost 134. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
