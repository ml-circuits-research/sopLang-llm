# 5.1.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In using a map to plan a route or field survey, expected demand is 119 map segments, but forecast error is estimated at ±10%. The policy adds a further 10% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 140 for cost 91; Option B provides capacity 180 for cost 106. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
