# 6.4.8 — Uncertainty and Robustness

Scenario. In analyzing a simplified representation system, expected demand is 76 votes or seats, but forecast error is estimated at ±15%. The policy adds a further 5% safety margin to the high forecast before selecting capacity. Option A provides capacity 87 for cost 123; Option B provides capacity 113 for cost 147. The average forecast is useful for planning, but the stated policy explicitly asks for a robust capacity decision.

Main question. Which option satisfies the robustness rule at the lowest cost? Best decomposition (8 subproblems).
