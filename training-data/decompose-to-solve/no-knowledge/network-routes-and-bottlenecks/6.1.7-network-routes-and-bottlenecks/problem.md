# 6.1.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For constructing a historical narrative from dated evidence, three routes can move the same events. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [16, 18, 8] min, capacities [89, 86, 80]; B: times [13, 6, 9] min, capacities [96, 71, 80]; C: times [17, 17, 6] min, capacities [94, 46, 97]. The required flow is 44 units and total route time must not exceed 35 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
