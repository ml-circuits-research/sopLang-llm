# 9.7.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning non-clinical public-health logistics, three routes can move the same supply units. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [16, 14, 15] min, capacities [46, 49, 48]; B: times [15, 17, 8] min, capacities [45, 91, 97]; C: times [18, 7, 6] min, capacities [68, 85, 97]. The required flow is 58 units and total route time must not exceed 43 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
