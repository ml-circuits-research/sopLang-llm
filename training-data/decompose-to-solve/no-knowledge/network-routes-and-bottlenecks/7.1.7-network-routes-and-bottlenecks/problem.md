# 7.1.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Three routes can move the same sources. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 18, 14] min, capacities [65, 76, 100]; B: times [15, 9, 10] min, capacities [91, 38, 83]; C: times [12, 6, 8] min, capacities [70, 83, 90]. The required flow is 67 units and total route time must not exceed 45 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Question. Which route is feasible and fastest?
