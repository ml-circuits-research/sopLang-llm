# 7.8.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For comparing primary and secondary historical sources, three routes can move the same source statements. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [16, 16, 11] min, capacities [36, 37, 57]; B: times [6, 10, 18] min, capacities [41, 85, 45]; C: times [17, 18, 13] min, capacities [60, 39, 92]. The required flow is 41 units and total route time must not exceed 39 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
