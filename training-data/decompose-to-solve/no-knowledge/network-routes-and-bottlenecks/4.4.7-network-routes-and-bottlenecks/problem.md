# 4.4.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning an optics demonstration, three routes can move the same optical elements. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [11, 9, 13] min, capacities [80, 69, 57]; B: times [8, 9, 7] min, capacities [93, 52, 96]; C: times [11, 15, 7] min, capacities [64, 79, 100]. The required flow is 52 units and total route time must not exceed 55 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
