# 10.7.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning a heritage-building restoration, three routes can move the same restoration tasks. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 9, 11] min, capacities [41, 38, 64]; B: times [10, 17, 15] min, capacities [46, 86, 69]; C: times [10, 14, 15] min, capacities [81, 85, 71]. The required flow is 38 units and total route time must not exceed 35 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
