# 9.9.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For mapping consequences for several stakeholders, three routes can move the same stakeholder effects. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [11, 9, 12] min, capacities [74, 82, 89]; B: times [11, 10, 10] min, capacities [94, 87, 43]; C: times [15, 13, 12] min, capacities [60, 55, 87]. The required flow is 73 units and total route time must not exceed 39 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
