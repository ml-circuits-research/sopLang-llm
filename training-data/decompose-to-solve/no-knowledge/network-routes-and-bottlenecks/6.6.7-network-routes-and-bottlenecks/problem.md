# 6.6.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For interpreting phases of a historic building, three routes can move the same building features. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [8, 10, 16] min, capacities [71, 77, 65]; B: times [10, 8, 6] min, capacities [45, 61, 36]; C: times [17, 10, 16] min, capacities [49, 63, 51]. The required flow is 41 units and total route time must not exceed 39 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
