# 4.5.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For preparing and checking a laboratory solution, three routes can move the same solution portions. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 12, 7] min, capacities [61, 77, 35]; B: times [6, 14, 7] min, capacities [87, 57, 59]; C: times [8, 7, 17] min, capacities [65, 47, 42]. The required flow is 57 units and total route time must not exceed 40 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
