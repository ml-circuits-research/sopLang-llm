# 10.9.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning a community renewable-energy project, three routes can move the same project components. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 11, 18] min, capacities [74, 59, 70]; B: times [9, 10, 10] min, capacities [54, 76, 54]; C: times [13, 16, 14] min, capacities [75, 39, 43]. The required flow is 58 units and total route time must not exceed 54 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
