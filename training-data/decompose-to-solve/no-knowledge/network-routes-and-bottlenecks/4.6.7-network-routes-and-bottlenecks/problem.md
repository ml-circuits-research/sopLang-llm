# 4.6.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For analyzing an ecosystem interaction, three routes can move the same organism groups. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 12, 11] min, capacities [47, 95, 96]; B: times [10, 18, 15] min, capacities [75, 42, 43]; C: times [7, 9, 17] min, capacities [59, 35, 87]. The required flow is 35 units and total route time must not exceed 39 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
