# 9.8.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For preparing a structured negotiation, three routes can move the same issues. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [13, 13, 6] min, capacities [96, 100, 78]; B: times [6, 16, 15] min, capacities [68, 72, 87]; C: times [12, 16, 11] min, capacities [86, 87, 36]. The required flow is 50 units and total route time must not exceed 49 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
