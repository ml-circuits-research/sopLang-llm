# 6.5.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For following a generic hierarchy of rules and decisions, three routes can move the same legal instruments. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [11, 16, 17] min, capacities [55, 88, 59]; B: times [14, 6, 7] min, capacities [96, 59, 41]; C: times [14, 18, 12] min, capacities [83, 56, 58]. The required flow is 41 units and total route time must not exceed 40 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
