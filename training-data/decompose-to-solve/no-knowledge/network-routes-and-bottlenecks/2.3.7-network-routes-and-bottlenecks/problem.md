# 2.3.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning seasonal farm work, three routes can move the same field tasks. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [11, 9, 9] min, capacities [98, 73, 58]; B: times [12, 12, 14] min, capacities [51, 96, 55]; C: times [16, 10, 18] min, capacities [81, 61, 41]. The required flow is 50 units and total route time must not exceed 41 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
