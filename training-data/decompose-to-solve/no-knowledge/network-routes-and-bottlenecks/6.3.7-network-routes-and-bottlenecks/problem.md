# 6.3.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For comparing institutional responsibilities in a generic constitutional system, three routes can move the same institutional actions. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [8, 7, 12] min, capacities [65, 100, 81]; B: times [12, 14, 15] min, capacities [57, 58, 81]; C: times [7, 13, 10] min, capacities [38, 63, 75]. The required flow is 65 units and total route time must not exceed 47 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
