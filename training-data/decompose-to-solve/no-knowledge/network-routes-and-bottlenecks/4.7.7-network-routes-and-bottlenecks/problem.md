# 4.7.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For interpreting geological observations, three routes can move the same field observations. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [9, 13, 14] min, capacities [50, 81, 61]; B: times [8, 10, 15] min, capacities [72, 49, 74]; C: times [11, 18, 18] min, capacities [36, 56, 49]. The required flow is 45 units and total route time must not exceed 53 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
