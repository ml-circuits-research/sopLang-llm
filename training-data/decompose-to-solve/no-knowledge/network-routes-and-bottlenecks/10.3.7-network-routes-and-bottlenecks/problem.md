# 10.3.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning a multidisciplinary field expedition, three routes can move the same field activities. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [14, 12, 9] min, capacities [49, 62, 84]; B: times [9, 18, 14] min, capacities [93, 81, 52]; C: times [17, 9, 9] min, capacities [51, 75, 54]. The required flow is 49 units and total route time must not exceed 47 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
