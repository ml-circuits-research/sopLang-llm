# 1.10.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For comparing practical purchase options, three routes can move the same requirements. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [15, 9, 13] min, capacities [68, 39, 38]; B: times [17, 18, 14] min, capacities [66, 79, 86]; C: times [11, 7, 12] min, capacities [45, 85, 94]. The required flow is 45 units and total route time must not exceed 49 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
