# 10.6.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning an educational international trip, three routes can move the same travel activities. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [18, 16, 8] min, capacities [47, 51, 79]; B: times [7, 15, 16] min, capacities [58, 73, 48]; C: times [12, 15, 6] min, capacities [55, 84, 69]. The required flow is 55 units and total route time must not exceed 52 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
