# 7.3.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For interpreting a public survey, three routes can move the same responses. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [8, 18, 16] min, capacities [79, 61, 91]; B: times [15, 16, 18] min, capacities [87, 35, 36]; C: times [11, 8, 10] min, capacities [69, 84, 35]. The required flow is 46 units and total route time must not exceed 45 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
