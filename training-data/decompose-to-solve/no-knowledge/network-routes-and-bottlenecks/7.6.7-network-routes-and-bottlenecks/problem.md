# 7.6.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Three routes can move the same study findings. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [7, 15, 11] min, capacities [43, 63, 62]; B: times [9, 18, 13] min, capacities [81, 62, 45]; C: times [11, 17, 13] min, capacities [62, 56, 91]. The required flow is 43 units and total route time must not exceed 50 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.
