# 4.2.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For comparing heat-loss or insulation choices, three routes can move the same thermal observations. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [9, 11, 17] min, capacities [77, 83, 36]; B: times [11, 18, 14] min, capacities [92, 80, 56]; C: times [14, 14, 11] min, capacities [80, 75, 76]. The required flow is 56 units and total route time must not exceed 55 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
