# 6.10.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For mapping responsibilities in an international cooperation case, three routes can move the same institutional commitments. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [8, 17, 6] min, capacities [43, 81, 41]; B: times [14, 12, 10] min, capacities [53, 54, 77]; C: times [9, 9, 10] min, capacities [65, 92, 89]. The required flow is 41 units and total route time must not exceed 42 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
