# 7.7.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For evaluating an environmental claim from mixed evidence, three routes can move the same measurements. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [6, 17, 7] min, capacities [41, 73, 65]; B: times [8, 15, 7] min, capacities [38, 41, 43]; C: times [15, 13, 9] min, capacities [93, 60, 74]. The required flow is 45 units and total route time must not exceed 45 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
