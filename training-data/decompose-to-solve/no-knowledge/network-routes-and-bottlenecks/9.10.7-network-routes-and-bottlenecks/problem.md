# 9.10.7 — Network Routes and Bottlenecks

Scenario. For planning routine safety controls, three routes can move the same hazards. Each route has three sequential links. A: times [11, 15, 10] min, capacities [78, 75, 37]; B: times [10, 9, 8] min, capacities [90, 96, 64]; C: times [8, 17, 12] min, capacities [50, 96, 39]. The required flow is 46 units and total route time must not exceed 41 minutes. A route's flow capacity is limited by its narrowest link, not by the sum of link capacities.

Main question. Which route is feasible and fastest? Best decomposition (7 subproblems).
