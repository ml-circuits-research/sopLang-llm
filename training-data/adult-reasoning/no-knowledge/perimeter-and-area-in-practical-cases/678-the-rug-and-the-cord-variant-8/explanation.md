# Explanation 678 — The rug and the cord — variant 8

## Explanation

1. Tess's room measures 13 m by 4 m, and the sheet makes the rug the area, so the rug is 13 × 4 = 52 m².
2. The cord runs along the outline, which the sheet calls the perimeter, so the cord is 2(13 + 4) = 34 m.
3. The two requests use different formulas on the same rectangle: multiplying the sides gives the surface, and adding the two sides before doubling gives the length around it.

Reference material as printed in the source:

The name of the quantity decides the unit. Do not buy area in linear metres.
