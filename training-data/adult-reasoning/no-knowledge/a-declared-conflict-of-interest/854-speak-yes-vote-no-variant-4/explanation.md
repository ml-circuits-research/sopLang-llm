# Explanation 854 — Speak yes, vote no — variant 4

## Explanation

1. The rule of the Harbour Town committee removes the vote from whoever holds a direct money interest in the topic, and it grants the right to speak in the same sentence.
2. Tess sells paint, and the committee votes which paint firm to use, so the sale is a direct money interest in the topic under decision.
3. The first clause therefore applies as written: Tess does not vote.
4. The second clause is untouched by the interest, so Tess may speak, and the two answers are “Does not vote.” and “May speak.”.

Reference material as printed in the source:

The test is the direct interest. The procedure splits speech from vote. It is not an insult; it is a rule.
