# Explanation 2 — The warehouse notice — variant 2

## Explanation

1. The notice is closed: the required vest is the one on the hook rail, so Ben's home vest does not satisfy it even though the visit falls inside the opening hours 9:00–17:00.
2. Cara is 17, and the notice forbids persons under 18; the cabin does not help because the no-smoking perimeter includes it.
3. Three short bursts with pauses are the intermittent alarm, and the notice says to stay where you are.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Split the rules into allowed / forbidden / procedure.

1. The vest rule names the hook rail, not the colour of the fabric. Ben’s home vest fails the second requirement.
2. “Under 18” has no companion exception. 17 < 18. Cara does not pass.
3. The cabin is named inside the perimeter. It is not a neutral zone.
4. Bursts with pauses are the intermittent alarm, not the continuous one. Procedure: stay and wait — not the east gate.
5. The yellow lane would be correct for an eligible pedestrian; Ben is not yet eligible.

The method of the course: do not humanise the notice. If the exception is not written, it does not exist in the problem. The notice defines its terms; we apply them.

## Result

**Answer.** Ben may not enter in the home vest (the rail vest is required). Cara may not enter: under 18; the cabin is still inside the perimeter. The intermittent alarm means stay put.
