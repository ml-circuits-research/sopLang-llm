# 9 — The warehouse notice — variant 9

On the service door of a warehouse in Long Hill this notice is posted:

“ENTRY ALLOWED only to staff wearing a vest taken from the hook rail by the gate. A vest brought from home does not replace the warehouse vest. Persons under 18 are FORBIDDEN to enter. Smoking is FORBIDDEN in the whole perimeter, including the porter’s cabin. Continuous alarm longer than 10 seconds: leave by the east gate. Intermittent alarm: stay where you are and wait for the loudspeaker. Pedestrians use the yellow lane. The warehouse opens for visits at 16:00 and closes at 24:00. Night staff use the west gate.”

Jules is 34, wears a vest brought from home, and wants to walk in at 17:10 on the yellow lane. Kara is 17 and asks whether they may wait in the porter’s cabin beside an employed parent. During the visit the alarm sounds three short bursts with pauses, then stops.

The notice lists no exceptions for “a similar vest”, “almost 18”, or “only waiting”.

Question. Who may enter by the service door, and what should anyone already inside do at this kind of alarm?
