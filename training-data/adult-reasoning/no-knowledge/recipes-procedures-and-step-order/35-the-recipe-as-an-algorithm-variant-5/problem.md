# 35 — The recipe as an algorithm — variant 5

Recipe from Ines’s kitchen in Harbour Town:

“For 2 tins: 280 g flour, 140 g lukewarm water, 7 g dry yeast, 8 g salt, 20 g oil.
S1: yeast + water, 10 minutes. S2: flour and salt, then oil. S3: knead 8 minutes. S4: rise 60 min at 24–26 °C. S5: two tins, another 20 min. S6: 18 min at 220 °C.
One tin: halve ALL ingredients, but NOT the waiting times in S1, S4, S5. Below 20 °C, S4 becomes 90 min. Do not put salt into the yeast water before S2.”

Ines wants one tin, the room is 18 °C, puts salt with the yeast, halves everything including kneading (4 min) and rising (30 min), then bakes.

Question. What did Ines respect, and what is the correct sequence for one tin at 18 °C?
