# Explanation 883 — The cupboard Leo opened — variant 3

## Explanation

1. Jules placed the gift, so Jules knows where it is regardless of what was said afterwards.
2. Leo opened the cupboard, saw, and said nothing: silence changes who else learns, not what Leo knows.
3. Kara stayed in another room and the report never reached Kara.
4. Jules never saw the opening, so the claim “nobody knows” is wrong: Jules and Leo know. Kara does not.
