# Explanation 886 — The cupboard Drew opened — variant 6

## Explanation

1. Ben placed the gift, so Ben knows where it is regardless of what was said afterwards.
2. Drew opened the cupboard, saw, and said nothing: silence changes who else learns, not what Drew knows.
3. Cara stayed in another room and the report never reached Cara.
4. Ben never saw the opening, so the claim “nobody knows” is wrong: Ben and Drew know. Cara does not.

A table person × knows. “I didn’t say” ≠ “nobody knows”. The other’s knowledge is not only what we have told.
