# Explanation 890 — The cupboard Ugo opened — variant 10

## Explanation

1. Sam placed the gift, so Sam knows where it is regardless of what was said afterwards.
2. Ugo opened the cupboard, saw, and said nothing: silence changes who else learns, not what Ugo knows.
3. Tess stayed in another room and the report never reached Tess.
4. Sam never saw the opening, so the claim “nobody knows” is wrong: Sam and Ugo know. Tess does not.

A table person × knows. “I didn’t say” ≠ “nobody knows”. The other’s knowledge is not only what we have told.
