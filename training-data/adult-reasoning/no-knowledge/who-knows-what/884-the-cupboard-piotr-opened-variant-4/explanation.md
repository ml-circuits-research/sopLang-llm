# Explanation 884 — The cupboard Piotr opened — variant 4

## Explanation

1. Ned placed the gift, so Ned knows where it is regardless of what was said afterwards.
2. Piotr opened the cupboard, saw, and said nothing: silence changes who else learns, not what Piotr knows.
3. Olga stayed in another room and the report never reached Olga.
4. Ned never saw the opening, so the claim “nobody knows” is wrong: Ned and Piotr know. Olga does not.

Reference material as printed in the source:

A table person × knows. “I didn’t say” ≠ “nobody knows”. The other’s knowledge is not only what we have told.

## Result

**Answer.** Ned and Piotr know. Olga does not. Ned did not see Piotr’s act and said “nobody”.
