# Explanation 882 — The cupboard Hugo opened — variant 2

## Explanation

1. Farid placed the gift, so Farid knows where it is regardless of what was said afterwards.
2. Hugo opened the cupboard, saw, and said nothing: silence changes who else learns, not what Hugo knows.
3. Gina stayed in another room and the report never reached Gina.
4. Farid never saw the opening, so the claim “nobody knows” is wrong: Farid and Hugo know. Gina does not.
