# Explanation 1000 — Three fragments, one decision — variant 10

## Explanation

1. 2 kg at 27 per kg costs 54, which is over the budget of 50, so the money half of the notebook is settled.
2. The bus reaches the stop at 16:50 and the walk adds 8 minutes, putting Farid at the door at 16:58, while Wells Village closes at 17:00.
3. That leaves 2 minutes in the shop, and no fragment states how long buying 2 kg takes, so the time is not given as enough.
4. The notebook demands time and money together, so the synthesis is not a sure yes.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Logical AND. Money is clear. Till time is missing. An adult does not invent “2 minutes will do”. The right answer can be “not sufficiently secured”. The method of the whole course: gather what is written, refuse what is missing, decide on the intersection.

A last note to the reader

If you arrived here in order, you have walked through a thousand closed worlds. The method does not change in the open world: ask what is written, in which unit, for whom, until when, with what exception, from which base the percent is taken, who knows what. When the text is silent, be silent with the conclusion — not with courage.

Return to the problems you missed after a month. The memory of a short rulebook and the discipline of not embroidering it are muscle.

The end of the course is not a diploma. It is the reflex of reading to the footnote.
