# Explanation 648 — 11 centimetres on paper — variant 8

## Explanation

1. The numerical scale 1:50000 means one centimetre on the map is 50000 centimetres on the ground, which is the printed reading of 500 m or 0.5 km.
2. Drew measures 11 cm between the two wells, so the real distance is 11 times the reading of 0.5 km.
3. That gives 5.5 km, which is the same distance as 5500 m when the kilometre answer is written in metres.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Rule of three. Scale is a paper–ground exchange rate. Do not guess “about 2 km”.

## Result

**Answer.** 11 × 0.5 km = 5.5 km (5500 m).
