# Explanation 644 — 7 centimetres on paper — variant 4

## Explanation

1. The numerical scale 1:50000 means one centimetre on the map is 50000 centimetres on the ground, which is the printed reading of 500 m or 0.5 km.
2. Hugo measures 7 cm between the two wells, so the real distance is 7 times the reading of 0.5 km.
3. That gives 3.5 km, which is the same distance as 3500 m when the kilometre answer is written in metres.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Rule of three. Scale is a paper–ground exchange rate. Do not guess “about 2 km”.

## Result

**Answer.** 7 × 0.5 km = 3.5 km (3500 m).
