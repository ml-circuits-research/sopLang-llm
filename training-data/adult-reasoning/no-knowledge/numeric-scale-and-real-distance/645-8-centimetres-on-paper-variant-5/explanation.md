# Explanation 645 — 8 centimetres on paper — variant 5

## Explanation

1. The numerical scale 1:50000 means one centimetre on the map is 50000 centimetres on the ground, which is the printed reading of 500 m or 0.5 km.
2. Leo measures 8 cm between the two wells, so the real distance is 8 times the reading of 0.5 km.
3. That gives 4.0 km, which is the same distance as 4000 m when the kilometre answer is written in metres.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Rule of three. Scale is a paper–ground exchange rate. Do not guess “about 2 km”.

## Result

**Answer.** 8 × 0.5 km = 4.0 km (4000 m).
