# Explanation 532 — The bell in the jar — variant 2

## Explanation

1. The jar loses its air, so the medium sound needs is gone, while the lamp keeps shining: Leo sees the motion of the bell but hears nothing.
2. The bell itself is not broken, because the recording shows the clapper moving; only the sound that should have followed is missing.
3. Light did not need the air that was removed, so the lamp is still seen in the vacuum and the two observations separate sound from light.

Reference material as printed in the source:

The experiment separates two channels. Vacuum cuts the mechanical, not the optical.

## Result

**Answer.** Sound needed air; light did not. The bell is not “broken”: the motion is seen.
