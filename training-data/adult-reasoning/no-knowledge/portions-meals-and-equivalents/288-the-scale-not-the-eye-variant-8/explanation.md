# Explanation 288 — The scale, not the eye — variant 8

## Explanation

1. The guide at the canteen in Mill Hamlet fixes one side at 180 g, one protein at 120 g, and one slice at 30 g for the standard lunch of 1+1+1.
2. Leo's tray carries 120 g of protein, which is within the guide, but 270 g of side is 90 g over and 2 slices are 30 g over.
3. The guide's only doubling is on the protein at +8, and it gives no unit called “one and a half meals”, so the claim to the standard price has nothing to stand on.
4. The scale, not the eye, is the authority, and on the scale the tray is over on two of its three lines.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Compare by component. Allowed doubling is on protein, not side/bread. An undefined unit cannot be used at the till.

## Result

**Answer.** Protein fine; side +90 g; bread +30 g. “One and a half meals” is not defined.
