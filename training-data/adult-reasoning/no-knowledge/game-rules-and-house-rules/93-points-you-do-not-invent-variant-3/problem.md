# 93 — Points you do not invent — variant 3

Hall rules, game “Bridges”, Maple Ward:

“4 players. First to 12 points wins. A correct bridge = 3 points. An incomplete bridge at the end of the turn = 0 and is taken down. Forbidden to take pieces from another player’s reserve. Extra piece from the common pile: put it back and lose the turn. Talk about your own hand is allowed. “Don’t put it there” about another’s plan = −2, once per turn. The referee has the last word on correct pieces.”

Kara has 10 points. Builds a bridge; the referee calls it incomplete. Had taken an extra piece, put it back, but continued the turn and said “don’t put it there”. Claims 13 points and the win.

Question. How many points does Kara have after the turn, and why is there no win?
