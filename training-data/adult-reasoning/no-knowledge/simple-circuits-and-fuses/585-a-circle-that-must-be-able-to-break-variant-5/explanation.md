# Explanation 585 — A circle that must be able to break — variant 5

## Explanation

1. Two bulbs in series sit on a single path, so the burnt filament opens the circle for both: Ines sees no light from either bulb.
2. The fuse exists to break at high current and open the circle, and the sheet forbids replacing it with a wire, so bridging the break removes the protection the workshop requires.
3. The quoted reason "so the light works" is beside the point: the ban is stated directly, even though the wire would light the bulbs.

Reference material as printed in the source:

The workshop’s aim is not light at any price. A fuse is an intentional break.

## Result

**Answer.** Series = one path; one break cuts all. The wire cancels protection; the ban is direct, even if it “would light”.
