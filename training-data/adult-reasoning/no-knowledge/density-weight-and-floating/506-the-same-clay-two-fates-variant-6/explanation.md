# Explanation 506 — The same clay, two fates — variant 6

## Explanation

1. The club of Station Quarter measures density as mass divided by volume, so the 25 g of clay divided by the 13 cm³ of the solid cube gives 1.92, above the water density of 1 g/cm³, and the cube sinks.
2. The same 25 g shaped into a boat displaces 35 cm³, so the average density falls to 0.71, below water, and the boat floats.
3. The trapped air is what lowers the average density of the whole: the mass never changes, but the volume the shape pushes aside grows from 13 cm³ to 35 cm³, and the sheet reads a density equal to water as suspended.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Same mass, different volume. Shape changes the volume of the whole. No outside textbook is needed: the density rule is on the sheet.

## Result

**Answer.** Cube 1.92 > 1 → sinks. Boat 0.71 < 1 → floats.
