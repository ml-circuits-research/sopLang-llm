# 80 — Pronouns in the minutes — variant 10

Stairwell minutes, Harbour Town:

“Ines proposed painting the banister. Jules objected, saying the roof had already been paid for. Kara will vote for painting if the price stays under 980. The chair asked that this be put on next month’s list. Ines asked that theirs be voted today. A vote was held. Four for, two against, one abstention. It was noted that he will request three quotes.”

Attached rule: “this” = the last proposal stated; “theirs” = the speaker’s; “he” = the last man named before. Decisions apply only if the text says what was voted.
