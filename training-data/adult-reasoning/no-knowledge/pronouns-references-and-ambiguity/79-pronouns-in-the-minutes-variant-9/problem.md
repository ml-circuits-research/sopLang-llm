# 79 — Pronouns in the minutes — variant 9

Stairwell minutes, Bridge City:

“Elena proposed painting the banister. Farid objected, saying the roof had already been paid for. Gina will vote for painting if the price stays under 960. The chair asked that this be put on next month’s list. Elena asked that theirs be voted today. A vote was held. Four for, two against, one abstention. It was noted that he will request three quotes.”

Attached rule: “this” = the last proposal stated; “theirs” = the speaker’s; “he” = the last man named before. Decisions apply only if the text says what was voted.
