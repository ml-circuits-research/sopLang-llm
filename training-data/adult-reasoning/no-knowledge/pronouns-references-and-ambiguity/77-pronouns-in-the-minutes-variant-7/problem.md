# 77 — Pronouns in the minutes — variant 7

Stairwell minutes, Stadium District:

“Rita proposed painting the banister. Sam objected, saying the roof had already been paid for. Tess will vote for painting if the price stays under 920. The chair asked that this be put on next month’s list. Rita asked that theirs be voted today. A vote was held. Four for, two against, one abstention. It was noted that he will request three quotes.”

Attached rule: “this” = the last proposal stated; “theirs” = the speaker’s; “he” = the last man named before. Decisions apply only if the text says what was voted.
