# 231 — Water in the pack — variant 1

Day-hike guide, Harbour Town: “In the pack: 0.4 l per 10 kg of body mass, rounded up to 10 kg. Above 28 °C at noon: +0.5 l. Over 15 km: +0.5 l. 0.5 l drunk in the morning does NOT count in the pack. 150 ml pauses are drunk from the pack, not extra reserve.”

Tess is 55 kg, route 12 km, noon 30 °C.

Question. How much water in the pack? Why not the morning 0.5 l?
