# Explanation 529 — The shadow that does not move into the book — variant 9

## Explanation

1. The book is an opaque screen placed between the lamp and the mug, so the sheet leaves the mug in shade; Olga reads the vanished patch as a shadow that moved into the book.
2. A shadow needs a lit object, and the mug is no longer lit, so the patch it threw on the wall disappears while the book throws its own shadow over the mug.
3. Because light travels straight in uniform air, taking the book away puts the lamp, the mug, and the wall back on one line and the mug’s shadow returns.

Reference material as printed in the source:

A shadow is the absence of light behind a lit obstacle. The moving-shadow story is replaced by the chain source–obstacle–object–screen.

## Result

**Answer.** The mug is no longer lit. The book has its own shadow; the mug sits in it. Without the book, the mug’s shadow returns in a straight line.
