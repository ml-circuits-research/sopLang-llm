# 50 — Three plates and a footnote — variant 10

The town sign register for Forest Parish describes a post by a school, top to bottom:

Top: red circle with a lorry — “goods vehicles forbidden”.
Middle: “except deliveries between 6:00 and 8:00”.
Bottom: a cancelled P — “no parking”.
Note: the delivery exception amends only the lorry ban, not the parking ban. Bicycles are not goods vehicles. A private car with shopping bags is not “delivery”.

Ned brings bread in a delivery van at 7:19 and wants to stand 15 minutes at the post. A neighbour parks a car “for one minute”. A pupil rides past on a bicycle.

Question. For each of the three, what is allowed and what is forbidden under the register?
