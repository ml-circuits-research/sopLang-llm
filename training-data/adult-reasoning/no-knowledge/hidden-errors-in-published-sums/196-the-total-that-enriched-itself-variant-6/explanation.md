# Explanation 196 — The total that enriched itself — variant 6

## Explanation

1. Farid rebuilds the product in the notice from Wells Village, and 11 jars at 17 each give 187.
2. The printed total is 202, which is 15 higher, not lower than the product, so the notice overstates the sum rather than cutting it.
3. A saving against the each price would have to be a total below 187, and the advertised 15 is exactly the discrepancy in the other direction, so the "saving" is the sign flipped.
