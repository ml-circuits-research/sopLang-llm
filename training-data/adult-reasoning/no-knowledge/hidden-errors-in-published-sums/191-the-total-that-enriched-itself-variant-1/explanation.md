# Explanation 191 — The total that enriched itself — variant 1

## Explanation

1. Farid rebuilds the product in the notice from Wells Village, and 6 jars at 12 each give 72.
2. The printed total is 87, which is 15 higher, not lower than the product, so the notice overstates the sum rather than cutting it.
3. A saving against the each price would have to be a total below 72, and the advertised 15 is exactly the discrepancy in the other direction, so the "saving" is the sign flipped.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Always rebuild n × p. If the notice does not match, it does not get the benefit of the doubt. Here the “saving” was added instead of subtracted.

## Result

**Answer.** The correct product is 72. The notice is 15 higher, not lower. The “saving” is the sign flipped.
