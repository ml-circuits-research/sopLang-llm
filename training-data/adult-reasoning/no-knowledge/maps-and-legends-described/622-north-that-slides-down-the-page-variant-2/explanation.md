# Explanation 622 — North that slides down the page — variant 2

## Explanation

1. The legend in Long Hill draws the park as the green triangle and the path as the dotted line, so the first leg of the printed route runs from the well to the park on the EAST cardinal.
2. Turning the sheet until north lies down on the page drags every cardinal with it, and the side that then lies left of the rotated page is east.
3. That is the cardinal of the path to the park, so Jules walks east and arrives at the green triangle.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Cardinals rotate with the page. It is not “never rotate”; it is “rotate north too”.

## Result

**Answer.** With north down, east is to the left of the rotated page. Yes, they walk east on the path and reach the park.
