# Explanation 12 — The e-bike clauses — variant 2

## Explanation

1. The contract runs for 8 calendar days and Gina returned after 10, so 2 calendar days were begun and Art. 5 charges 25 for each: Late fee 2×25=50.
2. The battery came back at 40%, below the 50% that Art. 7 requires, so 30 stays with the desk.
3. Gina keeps the 8 cm scratch, and an impact mark on the frame is exactly what removes the duty to return the deposit in full, so the demand for the whole deposit in cash the same day fails.
4. Hugo spoke for the desk, but Art. 9 allows changes only in writing signed by both parties, so the offer has no force.

Reference material as printed in the source:

Apply the articles in a chain, not by sympathy.

The term is 8 days; handover is 2 begun calendar days late → 50 (Art. 5).
Art. 7 is numeric: 40 < 50 → 30 from the deposit.
Art. 4 joins two conditions with “and”. A mark on the frame → full return is no longer obligatory. The text gives no tariff per centimetre of scratch, so we do not invent the damage figure; we only note that “in full” falls.
Art. 4 allows 3 working days, not cash at once.
Art. 9 voids Hugo’s spoken bargain, even though Hugo is staff: the power to amend is not in the contract.

When the text is silent on a tariff, an adult notes the silence. They do not fill it with “50 feels fair”.

## Result

**Answer.** Late fee 2×25=50. Battery 40%<50% → 30 from the deposit. The scratch cancels the duty to return the deposit in full (Art. 4). Speech does not amend Art. 9. Return is not “same day cash”.
