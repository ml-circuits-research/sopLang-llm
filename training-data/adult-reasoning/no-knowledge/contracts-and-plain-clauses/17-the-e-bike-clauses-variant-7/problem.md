# 17 — The e-bike clauses — variant 7

Hire contract for an electric bicycle, a desk in Stadium District, signed by Gina:

Art. 1. Object: bicycle serial B-17, with charger. Art. 2. Term: 13 calendar days from 10:00 on the day of signing. Art. 3. Price: 150 paid in advance. Art. 4. Deposit: 450, returned within 3 working days if handover is without missing parts and without impact marks on the frame. Art. 5. Late return: 25 for each calendar day begun. Art. 6. Cancellation before collection: 20% of the price is kept; the deposit is returned in full. Art. 7. Battery must be handed back above 50%; below 50%, 30 is kept from the deposit. Art. 8. The helmet is optional. Art. 9. Changes only in writing, signed by both parties. Amounts are in the local currency of the desk.

Gina collects Monday at 10:00. Returns after 15 days with an 8 cm scratch on the frame and the battery at 40%. Demands the whole deposit the same day, in cash. Hugo, a staff member, offers verbally to ignore the scratch for 50 extra.

Question. Which withholdings follow safely from the contract, and what force does Hugo’s spoken offer have?
