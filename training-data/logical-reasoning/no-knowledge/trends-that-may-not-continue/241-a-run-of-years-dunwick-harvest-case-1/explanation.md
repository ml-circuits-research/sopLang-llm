# Explanation 241 — A run of years — Dunwick harvest — case 1

## Explanation

1. The notes of Dunwick report 4 rises, from a base of 100 baskets to a last year of 120 baskets.
2. Sam reads year 5 as forced, but a chart is not a deduction: extrapolation adds the unwritten premise that the mechanism behind the climb continues.
3. Owen turns 4 rises into a law of nature, while Tess keeps the expectation cautious until a mechanism is given; four points can be a path or a bump.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 25, 5 steps):

1. Extrapolation adds “the mechanism continues.”
2. Numbers alone do not name the mechanism.
3. “Must” is the wrong adverb.
4. Four points can be a path or a bump.
5. Separate the chart from the prophecy.

## Result

**Answer.** Nothing as a deduction. As induction it is a climb with a hidden premise: that whatever lifted the yield continues. The page did not write that premise.
