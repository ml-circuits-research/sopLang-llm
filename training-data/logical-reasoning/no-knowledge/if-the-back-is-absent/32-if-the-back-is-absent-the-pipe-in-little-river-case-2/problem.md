# 32 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Little River — case 2

Caretaker card in Little River: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Ben opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Cara says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Nia says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
