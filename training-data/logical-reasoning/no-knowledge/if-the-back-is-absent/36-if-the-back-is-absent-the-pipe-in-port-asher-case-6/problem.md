# 36 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Port Asher — case 6

Caretaker card in Port Asher: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Ade opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Bela says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Chris says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
