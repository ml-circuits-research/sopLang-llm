# 33 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Mill Hamlet — case 3

Caretaker card in Mill Hamlet: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Farid opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Gina says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Pavel says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
