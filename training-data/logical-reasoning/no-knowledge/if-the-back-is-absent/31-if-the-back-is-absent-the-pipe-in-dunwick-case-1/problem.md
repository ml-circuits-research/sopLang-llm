# 31 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Dunwick — case 1

Caretaker card in Dunwick: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Sam opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Tess says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Owen says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
