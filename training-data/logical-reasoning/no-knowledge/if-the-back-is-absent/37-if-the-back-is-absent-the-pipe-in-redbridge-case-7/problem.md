# 37 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Redbridge — case 7

Caretaker card in Redbridge: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Dora opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Eli says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Fran says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
