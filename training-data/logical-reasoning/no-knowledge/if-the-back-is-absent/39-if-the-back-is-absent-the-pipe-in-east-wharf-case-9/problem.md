# 39 — If the back is absent — the pipe in East Wharf — case 9

Caretaker card in East Wharf: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Omar opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Pia says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Rafi says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
