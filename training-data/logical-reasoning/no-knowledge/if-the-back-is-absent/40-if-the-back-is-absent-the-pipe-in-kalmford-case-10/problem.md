# 40 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Kalmford — case 10

Caretaker card in Kalmford: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Sol opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Una says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Wes says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
