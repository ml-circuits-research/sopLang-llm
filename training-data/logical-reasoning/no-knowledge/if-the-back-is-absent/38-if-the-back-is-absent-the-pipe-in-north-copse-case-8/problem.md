# 38 — If the back is absent — the pipe in North Copse — case 8

Caretaker card in North Copse: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Jules opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Kat says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Ned says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
