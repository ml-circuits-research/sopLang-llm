# 34 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Wells Village — case 4

Caretaker card in Wells Village: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Harun opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Iona says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Ruth says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
