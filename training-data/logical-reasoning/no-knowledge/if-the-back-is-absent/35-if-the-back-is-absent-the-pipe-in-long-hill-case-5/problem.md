# 35 — If the back is absent — the pipe in Long Hill — case 5

Caretaker card in Long Hill: “IF the street pipe is frozen solid, THEN every ground-floor tap gives no water.” Leo opens the kitchen tap and a steady stream comes. Mira says the street pipe cannot be frozen solid, given this card. Quinn says a dry tap tomorrow would, by the same card, prove a freeze.

Question. Whose sentence is forced? Where does the third speaker overreach?
