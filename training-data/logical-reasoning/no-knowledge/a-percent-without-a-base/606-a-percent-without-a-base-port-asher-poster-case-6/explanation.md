# Explanation 606 — A percent without a base — Port Asher poster — case 6

## Explanation

1. The poster in Port Asher announces a cut of 15%, but a percent is a sentence with a hole: it is always a percent of something, and no original price, date, or basket is listed.
2. Ade treats the poster as a finished quantity, which reads a number the page never printed.
3. Bela keeps the hole open and asks for the base, and the “down” also needs the date it is measured from.
4. Chris fills the hole with a base of 150 to be helpful, but a helpful invention is still an invention: it writes a second poster instead of reading this one.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 61, 5 steps):

1. A percent is of something.
2. That something is missing.
3. Helpful invention is still invention.
4. “Down” also needs “from when.”
5. Write “unknown base,” not a fake number.
