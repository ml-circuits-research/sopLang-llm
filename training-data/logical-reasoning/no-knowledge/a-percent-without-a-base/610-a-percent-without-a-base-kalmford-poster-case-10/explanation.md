# Explanation 610 — A percent without a base — Kalmford poster — case 10

## Explanation

1. The poster in Kalmford announces a cut of 19%, but a percent is a sentence with a hole: it is always a percent of something, and no original price, date, or basket is listed.
2. Sol treats the poster as a finished quantity, which reads a number the page never printed.
3. Una keeps the hole open and asks for the base, and the “down” also needs the date it is measured from.
4. Wes fills the hole with a base of 190 to be helpful, but a helpful invention is still an invention: it writes a second poster instead of reading this one.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 61, 5 steps):

1. A percent is of something.
2. That something is missing.
3. Helpful invention is still invention.
4. “Down” also needs “from when.”
5. Write “unknown base,” not a fake number.
