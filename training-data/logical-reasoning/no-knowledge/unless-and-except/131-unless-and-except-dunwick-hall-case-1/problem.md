# 131 — Unless and except — Dunwick hall — case 1

Hall board in Dunwick: “The hall stays open until 22:00 unless a storm warning is posted. All evening classes run as planned except the roof-repair class, which is cancelled.” No storm warning is posted. Sam says the hall may already be shut because storms exist in the world. Tess treats the roof-repair class as cancelled and the other evening classes as running. Owen treats “except” as permission to cancel anything awkward.

Question. What do “unless” and “except” do here?
