# 136 — Unless and except — Port Asher hall — case 6

Hall board in Port Asher: “The hall stays open until 22:00 unless a storm warning is posted. All evening classes run as planned except the roof-repair class, which is cancelled.” No storm warning is posted. Ade says the hall may already be shut because storms exist in the world. Bela treats the roof-repair class as cancelled and the other evening classes as running. Chris treats “except” as permission to cancel anything awkward.

Question. What do “unless” and “except” do here?
