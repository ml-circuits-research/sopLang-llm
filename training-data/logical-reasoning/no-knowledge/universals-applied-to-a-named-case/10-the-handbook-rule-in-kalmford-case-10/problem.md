# 10 — The handbook rule in Kalmford — case 10

An evening class in Kalmford posts one closed rule: “All birds listed in this handbook have feathers and lay eggs. The sparrow is listed as a bird. The oak is listed as a tree.” Sol points at the listed sparrow and says it lays eggs. Una points at the listed oak and says it lays eggs. Wes asks the group not to add animals from memory.

Question. Which pointing is forced by the handbook?
