# 1 — The handbook rule in Dunwick — case 1

An evening class in Dunwick posts one closed rule: “All birds listed in this handbook have feathers and lay eggs. The sparrow is listed as a bird. The oak is listed as a tree.” Sam points at the listed sparrow and says it lays eggs. Tess points at the listed oak and says it lays eggs. Owen asks the group not to add animals from memory.

Question. Which pointing is forced by the handbook?
