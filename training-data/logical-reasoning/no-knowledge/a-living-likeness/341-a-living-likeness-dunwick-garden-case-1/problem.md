# 341 — A living likeness — Dunwick garden — case 1

Garden card in Dunwick: green plants make sugars from carbon dioxide and water using light; oxygen is released as a by-product. That process is called photosynthesis. Sam says a plant in a dark cupboard will still make new sugar because “living things find a way.” Tess maps the card: no light, no listed process. Owen says the neighbour likeness is the better science.

Question. Which likeness is doing honest work?
