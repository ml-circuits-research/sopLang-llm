# 316 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Port Asher — case 6

Stall in Port Asher. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Ade says this proves ice cannot float. Bela says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Chris says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
