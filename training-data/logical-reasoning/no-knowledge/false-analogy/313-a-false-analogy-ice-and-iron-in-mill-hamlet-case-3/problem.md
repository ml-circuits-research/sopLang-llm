# 313 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Mill Hamlet — case 3

Stall in Mill Hamlet. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Farid says this proves ice cannot float. Gina says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Pavel says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
