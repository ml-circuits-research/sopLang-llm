# 315 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Long Hill — case 5

Stall in Long Hill. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Leo says this proves ice cannot float. Mira says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Quinn says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
