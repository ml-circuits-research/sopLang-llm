# 319 — A false analogy — ice and iron in East Wharf — case 9

Stall in East Wharf. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Omar says this proves ice cannot float. Pia says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Rafi says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
