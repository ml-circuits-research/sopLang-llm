# 318 — A false analogy — ice and iron in North Copse — case 8

Stall in North Copse. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Jules says this proves ice cannot float. Kat says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Ned says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
