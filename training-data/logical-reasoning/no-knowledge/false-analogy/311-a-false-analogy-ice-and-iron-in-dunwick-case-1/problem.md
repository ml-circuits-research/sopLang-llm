# 311 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Dunwick — case 1

Stall in Dunwick. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Sam says this proves ice cannot float. Tess says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Owen says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
