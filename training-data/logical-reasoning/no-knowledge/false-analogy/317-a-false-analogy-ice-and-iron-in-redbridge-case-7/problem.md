# 317 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Redbridge — case 7

Stall in Redbridge. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Dora says this proves ice cannot float. Eli says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Fran says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
