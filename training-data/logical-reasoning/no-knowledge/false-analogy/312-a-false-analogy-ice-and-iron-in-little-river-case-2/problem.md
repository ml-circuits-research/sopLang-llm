# 312 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Little River — case 2

Stall in Little River. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Ben says this proves ice cannot float. Cara says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Nia says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
