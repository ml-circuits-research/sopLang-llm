# 320 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Kalmford — case 10

Stall in Kalmford. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Sol says this proves ice cannot float. Una says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Wes says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
