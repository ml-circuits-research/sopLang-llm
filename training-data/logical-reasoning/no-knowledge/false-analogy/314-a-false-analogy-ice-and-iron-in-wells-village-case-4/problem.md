# 314 — A false analogy — ice and iron in Wells Village — case 4

Stall in Wells Village. A school card: ice floats on a pond because it is less dense than liquid water. A performer drops an iron bar into a bucket; it sinks. Harun says this proves ice cannot float. Iona says iron and ice do not share the property that does the floating work. Ruth says any two hard things dropped in water are the same lesson.

Question. Does sinking iron refute floating ice?
