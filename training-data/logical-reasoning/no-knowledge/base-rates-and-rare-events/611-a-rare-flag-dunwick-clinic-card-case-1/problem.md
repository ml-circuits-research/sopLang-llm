# 611 — A rare flag — Dunwick clinic card — case 1

Teaching card in Dunwick: in a crowd of 1000, about 1 person has condition K. A flag test catches 99 of 100 real K and also flags 1 of 100 people without K. Sam says a flagged person almost surely has K. Tess counts about one true flag and about 10 false flags, so most flags are false. Owen says a test that catches 99 of 100 cannot mostly be wrong when it rings.

Question. Among flagged people in this arithmetic, are most K?
