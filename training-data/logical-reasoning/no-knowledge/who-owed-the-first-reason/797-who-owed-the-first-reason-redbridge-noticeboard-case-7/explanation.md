# Explanation 797 — Who owed the first reason — Redbridge noticeboard — case 7

## Explanation

1. Dora pinned the claim “The well is unsafe.” on the Redbridge board with no reason listed, so the claim entered the room bare.
2. Dora then answered the request for a reason with “prove it is safe,” which asks the room to disprove an unsupported claim instead of supplying one.
3. undefined states the rule correctly: the person who asserted owed the first reason, and Dora is that person.
4. undefined is wrong that silence from others is already evidence; silence is not a premise.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 80, 5 steps):

1. Assertion without support is a bare claim.
2. Bare claims do not flip a duty onto hearers.
3. “Prove me wrong” is not a first reason.
4. Silence ≠ confirmation.
5. High stakes may justify caution as policy — still not a finished proof.

## Result

**Answer.** Dora, the assertor. Shifting the burden asks the room to disprove an unsupported claim. Silence is not a premise.
