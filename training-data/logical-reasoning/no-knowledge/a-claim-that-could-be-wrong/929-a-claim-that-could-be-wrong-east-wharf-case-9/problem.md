# 929 — A claim that could be wrong — East Wharf — case 9

Omar in East Wharf says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Pia says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Rafi says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.
