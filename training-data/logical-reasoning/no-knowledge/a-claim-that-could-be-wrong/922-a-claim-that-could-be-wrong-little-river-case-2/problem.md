# 922 — A claim that could be wrong — Little River — case 2

Ben in Little River says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Cara says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Nia says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.
