# 923 — A claim that could be wrong — Mill Hamlet — case 3

Farid in Mill Hamlet says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Gina says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Pavel says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.
