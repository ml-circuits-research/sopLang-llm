# Explanation 925 — A claim that could be wrong — Long Hill — case 5

## Explanation

1. Leo of Long Hill offers a stone whose every miss is relabelled a secret success, so no observation could count against the claim.
2. Mira names what that costs: a claim that cannot be shown wrong is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book.
3. Quinn calls the claim the strongest kind because nothing can touch it, but untouchable is not strong; it has left the game.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 93, 5 steps):

1. Science-as-ethic includes “this could be wrong.”
2. Every miss was relabelled as secret success.
3. Untouchable ≠ strong.
4. Name a possible miss.
5. If nothing could count against it, nothing counts for it either.

## Result
