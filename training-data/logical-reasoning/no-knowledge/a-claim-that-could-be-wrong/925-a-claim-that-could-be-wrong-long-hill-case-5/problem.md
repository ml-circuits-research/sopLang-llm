# 925 — A claim that could be wrong — Long Hill — case 5

Leo in Long Hill says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Mira says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Quinn says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.

Question. What kind of claim was offered?
