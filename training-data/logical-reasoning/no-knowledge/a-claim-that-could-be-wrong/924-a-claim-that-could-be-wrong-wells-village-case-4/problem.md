# 924 — A claim that could be wrong — Wells Village — case 4

Harun in Wells Village says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Iona says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Ruth says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.

Question. What kind of claim was offered?
