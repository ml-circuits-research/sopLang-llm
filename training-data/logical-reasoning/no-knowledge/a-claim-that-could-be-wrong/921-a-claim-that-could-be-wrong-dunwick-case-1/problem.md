# 921 — A claim that could be wrong — Dunwick — case 1

Sam in Dunwick says “this stone always points north, and if it points south that would only prove the south is secretly north.” Tess says the claim cannot be shown wrong, so it is not doing scientific work in the modest sense of this book. Owen says an unfalsifiable claim is the strongest kind because nothing can touch it.

Question. What kind of claim was offered?
