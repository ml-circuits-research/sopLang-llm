# 48 — Bus or train — North Copse day plan — case 8

Printed plan in North Copse for one clerk: “Today you travel by BUS or by TRAIN. No third means is listed.” The only morning bus is cancelled. The train is on time. Jules says the clerk takes the train. Kat says the clerk may now walk, because a cancellation opens every road. Ned says “or” always keeps the cancelled bus as a live option.
