# 50 — Bus or train — Kalmford day plan — case 10

Printed plan in Kalmford for one clerk: “Today you travel by BUS or by TRAIN. No third means is listed.” The only morning bus is cancelled. The train is on time. Sol says the clerk takes the train. Una says the clerk may now walk, because a cancellation opens every road. Wes says “or” always keeps the cancelled bus as a live option.
