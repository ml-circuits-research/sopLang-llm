# 41 — Bus or train — Dunwick day plan — case 1

Printed plan in Dunwick for one clerk: “Today you travel by BUS or by TRAIN. No third means is listed.” The only morning bus is cancelled. The train is on time. Sam says the clerk takes the train. Tess says the clerk may now walk, because a cancellation opens every road. Owen says “or” always keeps the cancelled bus as a live option.
