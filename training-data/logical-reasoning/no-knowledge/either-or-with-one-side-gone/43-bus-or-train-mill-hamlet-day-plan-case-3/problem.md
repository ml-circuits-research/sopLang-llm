# 43 — Bus or train — Mill Hamlet day plan — case 3

Printed plan in Mill Hamlet for one clerk: “Today you travel by BUS or by TRAIN. No third means is listed.” The only morning bus is cancelled. The train is on time. Farid says the clerk takes the train. Gina says the clerk may now walk, because a cancellation opens every road. Pavel says “or” always keeps the cancelled bus as a live option.
