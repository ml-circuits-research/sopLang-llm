# 44 — Bus or train — Wells Village day plan — case 4

Printed plan in Wells Village for one clerk: “Today you travel by BUS or by TRAIN. No third means is listed.” The only morning bus is cancelled. The train is on time. Harun says the clerk takes the train. Iona says the clerk may now walk, because a cancellation opens every road. Ruth says “or” always keeps the cancelled bus as a live option.
