# 769 — A word that changes meaning — East Wharf light — case 9

Notice in East Wharf: “Use only light tools on this floor.” A later line on the same notice uses “light” to mean illumination (“keep a light on”). Omar carries a heavy lamp because “light is required.” Pia says the word shifted: first “not-heavy,” then “illumination.” Rafi says a repeated word always keeps one meaning.

Question. What happened to the word?
