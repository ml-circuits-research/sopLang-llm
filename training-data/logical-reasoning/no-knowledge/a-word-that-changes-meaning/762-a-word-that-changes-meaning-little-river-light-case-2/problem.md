# 762 — A word that changes meaning — Little River light — case 2

Notice in Little River: “Use only light tools on this floor.” A later line on the same notice uses “light” to mean illumination (“keep a light on”). Ben carries a heavy lamp because “light is required.” Cara says the word shifted: first “not-heavy,” then “illumination.” Nia says a repeated word always keeps one meaning.

Question. What happened to the word?
