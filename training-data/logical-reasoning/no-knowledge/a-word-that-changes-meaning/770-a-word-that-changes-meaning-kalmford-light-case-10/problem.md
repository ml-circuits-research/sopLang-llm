# 770 — A word that changes meaning — Kalmford light — case 10

Notice in Kalmford: “Use only light tools on this floor.” A later line on the same notice uses “light” to mean illumination (“keep a light on”). Sol carries a heavy lamp because “light is required.” Una says the word shifted: first “not-heavy,” then “illumination.” Wes says a repeated word always keeps one meaning.

Question. What happened to the word?
