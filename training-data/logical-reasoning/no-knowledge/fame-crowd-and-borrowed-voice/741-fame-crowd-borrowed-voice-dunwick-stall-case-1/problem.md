# 741 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — Dunwick stall — case 1

Stall in Dunwick. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Sam treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Tess says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Owen says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.

Question. What did fame and the queue actually show?
