# 748 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — North Copse stall — case 8

Stall in North Copse. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Jules treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Kat says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Ned says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.

Question. What did fame and the queue actually show?
