# 744 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — Wells Village stall — case 4

Stall in Wells Village. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Harun treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Iona says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Ruth says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.

Question. What did fame and the queue actually show?
