# 747 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — Redbridge stall — case 7

Stall in Redbridge. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Dora treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Eli says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Fran says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.

Question. What did fame and the queue actually show?
