# 750 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — Kalmford stall — case 10

Stall in Kalmford. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Sol treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Una says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Wes says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.
