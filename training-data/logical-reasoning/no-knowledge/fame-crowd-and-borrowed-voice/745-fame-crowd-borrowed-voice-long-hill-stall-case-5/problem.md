# 745 — Fame, crowd, borrowed voice — Long Hill stall — case 5

Stall in Long Hill. A famous singer says the pies are the best in the county. A long queue stands in the rain. Leo treats fame plus queue as a proof of quality. Mira says the singer is not a listed pie expert and a queue measures demand or shelter, not flavour. Quinn says borrowed fame is the adult form of evidence.
