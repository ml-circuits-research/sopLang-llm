# 120 — A necessary condition — Kalmford gate — case 10

Gate notice in Kalmford: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Sol wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Una wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Wes says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
