# 111 — A necessary condition — Dunwick gate — case 1

Gate notice in Dunwick: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Sam wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Tess wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Owen says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
