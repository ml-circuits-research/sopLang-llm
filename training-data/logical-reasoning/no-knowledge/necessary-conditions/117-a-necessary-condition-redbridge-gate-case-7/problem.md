# 117 — A necessary condition — Redbridge gate — case 7

Gate notice in Redbridge: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Dora wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Eli wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Fran says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.
