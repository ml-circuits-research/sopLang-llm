# 118 — A necessary condition — North Copse gate — case 8

Gate notice in North Copse: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Jules wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Kat wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Ned says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
