# 113 — A necessary condition — Mill Hamlet gate — case 3

Gate notice in Mill Hamlet: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Farid wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Gina wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Pavel says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
