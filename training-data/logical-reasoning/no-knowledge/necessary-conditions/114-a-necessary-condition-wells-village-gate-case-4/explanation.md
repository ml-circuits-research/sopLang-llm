# Explanation 114 — A necessary condition — Wells Village gate — case 4

## Explanation

1. The notice in Wells Village admits a visitor only if the vest comes from the named hook rail, so a rail vest is necessary for entry.
2. Iona offers a home-made vest of the same colour, but the notice names the source of the vest, not its colour, so that vest fails the condition.
3. Harun wears a rail vest and that satisfies the condition, yet "only if" gives no direction from the condition back to entry, so sufficiency is untouched.
4. Ruth keeps the two questions apart and reads the rail vest as required without inventing extra permissions, which is the scope the sentence fixes.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 12, 5 steps):

1. “Only if V” makes V necessary for entry.
2. Necessary ≠ sufficient.
3. The notice names the hook rail, not the colour.
4. Granting necessity does not invent extra permissions.
5. Write “enough?” and “required?” as two questions.

## Result

**Answer.** The rail vest is necessary. A home-made vest fails the named source. Wearing the rail vest is not yet shown to be sufficient. Ruth has the scope right.
