# 116 — A necessary condition — Port Asher gate — case 6

Gate notice in Port Asher: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Ade wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Bela wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Chris says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
