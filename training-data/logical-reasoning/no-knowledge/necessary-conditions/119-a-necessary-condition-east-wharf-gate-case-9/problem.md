# 119 — A necessary condition — East Wharf gate — case 9

Gate notice in East Wharf: “You may enter the inner yard only if you wear a vest taken from the hook rail.” Omar wears a rail vest and asks whether that sentence already guarantees entry. Pia wears a home-made vest of the same colour and says colour should be enough. Rafi says the rail vest is required, and the notice has not said it is the only remaining door.

Question. What exactly is forced?
