# 510 — Ranking the stories — Kalmford dark lamp — case 10

A lamp in Kalmford is dark. Listed: the switch is on; the neighbouring lamp on the same listed socket-strip is lit. Sol offers “the bulb is dead.” Una offers “the town’s whole grid has failed.” Wes offers “a rare moth jammed a hidden second switch,” with no moth listed.

Question. Which story should start first, given the listed signs?
