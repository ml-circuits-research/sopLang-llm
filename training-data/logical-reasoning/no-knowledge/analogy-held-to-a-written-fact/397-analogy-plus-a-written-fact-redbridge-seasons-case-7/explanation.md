# Explanation 397 — Analogy plus a written fact — Redbridge seasons — case 7

## Explanation

1. The school card in Redbridge chooses tilt over changing distance from the Sun as the reason the seasons change, so that fact is the authority both pictures answer to.
2. Dora offers the tilted lamp, which teaches the chosen tilt and therefore keeps the card's engine.
3. Eli prefers the nearer-in-summer picture because it feels warmer, but that picture asserts changing distance, which the card denies.
4. Fran is wrong that two analogies cannot disagree: they can, and the written fact breaks the tie in favour of the map of tilt.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 40, 5 steps):

1. The card chose tilt over distance.
2. An analogy is a servant of that fact, or it is a rival.
3. Rivals to a listed fact do not get extra marks for charm.
4. Two pictures can disagree; the text breaks the tie.
5. Teach with the map that keeps the engine.

## Result

**Answer.** The tilted lamp, as a map of tilt. The nearer-in-summer picture contradicts the listed fact about distance.
