# 400 — Analogy plus a written fact — Kalmford seasons — case 10

School card in Kalmford: Earth’s axis is tilted about 23.5 degrees relative to its orbit; that tilt, not changing distance from the Sun, is why seasons change. A speaker analogises a tilted lamp over a globe. Sol says the lamp picture may teach the tilt, but a second picture of “Earth moving nearer in summer” fights the card. Una prefers the nearer-in-summer picture because it feels warmer. Wes says two analogies cannot disagree.

Question. Which analogy is allowed to survive the card?
