# 51 — Two kinds of or — Dunwick canteen — case 1

Sign One in Dunwick: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Sam takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Tess asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Owen says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
