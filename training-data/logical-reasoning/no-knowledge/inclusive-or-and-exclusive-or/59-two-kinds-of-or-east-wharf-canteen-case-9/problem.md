# 59 — Two kinds of or — East Wharf canteen — case 9

Sign One in East Wharf: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Omar takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Pia asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Rafi says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
