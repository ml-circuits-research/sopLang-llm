# 60 — Two kinds of or — Kalmford canteen — case 10

Sign One in Kalmford: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Sol takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Una asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Wes says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
