# Explanation 60 — Two kinds of or — Kalmford canteen — case 10

## Explanation

1. Sign One in Kalmford lists SOUP or SALAD and adds "OR both", so its "or" is inclusive and allows both sides together.
2. Sign Two lists MEAT or FISH with "not both" and no third protein, so its "or" is exclusive.
3. Sol imports the exclusive reading into the inclusive sign, and Una imports the inclusive reading into the exclusive sign, so each of them denies a sign its own words allow.
4. Only Wes lets each sentence define its own "or", so the two signs keep the two senses they write.

Reference solution as printed in the source (section 6, 5 steps):

1. Inclusive or allows both sides together.
2. Exclusive or forbids the pair and any unlisted third.
3. Extra words (“or both”, “not both”) are the definition.
4. Do not import a private habit.
5. Two signs, two senses.
