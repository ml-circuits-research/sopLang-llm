# 58 — Two kinds of or — North Copse canteen — case 8

Sign One in North Copse: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Jules takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Kat asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Ned says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
