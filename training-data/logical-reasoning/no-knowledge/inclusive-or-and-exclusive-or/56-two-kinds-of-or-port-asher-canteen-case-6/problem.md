# 56 — Two kinds of or — Port Asher canteen — case 6

Sign One in Port Asher: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Ade takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Bela asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Chris says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
