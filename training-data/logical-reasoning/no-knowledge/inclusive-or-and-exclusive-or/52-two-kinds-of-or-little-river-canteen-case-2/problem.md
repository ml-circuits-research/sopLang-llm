# 52 — Two kinds of or — Little River canteen — case 2

Sign One in Little River: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Ben takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Cara asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Nia says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
