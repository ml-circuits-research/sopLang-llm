# 57 — Two kinds of or — Redbridge canteen — case 7

Sign One in Redbridge: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Dora takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Eli asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Fran says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
