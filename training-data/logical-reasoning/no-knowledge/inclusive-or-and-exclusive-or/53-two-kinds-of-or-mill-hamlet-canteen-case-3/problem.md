# 53 — Two kinds of or — Mill Hamlet canteen — case 3

Sign One in Mill Hamlet: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Farid takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Gina asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Pavel says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
