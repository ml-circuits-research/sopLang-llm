# 54 — Two kinds of or — Wells Village canteen — case 4

Sign One in Wells Village: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Harun takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Iona asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Ruth says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
