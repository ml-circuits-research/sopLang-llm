# 55 — Two kinds of or — Long Hill canteen — case 5

Sign One in Long Hill: “Lunch includes soup OR salad OR both.” Sign Two: “This special tray is MEAT or FISH, not both, and not a third protein.” Leo takes soup and salad and says Sign One forbids the pair. Mira asks for meat and fish on the special tray because “or” always includes both. Quinn says the two signs use “or” in two written ways.

Question. Who read both signs correctly?
