## Machine Learning

The publisher explicitly accepts Word manuscripts without providing a Word template. It specifies a 150–250-word abstract, 4–6 keywords, author-date references, native tables, numbered figures, and a 1–2-page contribution information sheet. The prepared document uses a readable one-column review layout; it does not imitate a published issue or invent received dates. The contribution sheet is [prepared separately](machine-learning-contribution-sheet.md). Sources: [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10994/submission-guidelines), [publishing options](https://link.springer.com/journal/10994/how-to-publish-with-us).

The fit is experimental representation learning and a negative-result boundary. The paper does not offer a new learning algorithm, a fresh external benchmark, or replicated causal estimates. Those are genuine editorial risks at this venue. The letter states the actual contribution rather than inflating it to compensate.

## Informatica

The journal publishes technical papers as well as regular research articles. Its regular-article range is 8–20 pages including references; other categories should be proportionate to their content. The prepared five-page technical paper uses the official template's page geometry, columns, and imported paragraph styles. Template auto-numbering is removed in favor of explicit source numbering, and publication-only placeholders are omitted. Main title is in title case; section headings are in sentence case. Source: [author guidelines](https://www.informatica.si/index.php/informatica/about/submissions).

The Normal track has no mandatory APC, submission fee, page charge, or color-figure fee under the current published policy. An optional paid Ultra-Fast service exists and is not selected. A no-fee community-review Fast Track also exists, but no reviewer commitment is made on the authors' behalf. Source: [APC and fees](https://www.informatica.si/index.php/informatica/fees).

The required submission file is a PDF exported from the template-based source. Both the requested DOCX and its inspected PDF preview are provided locally. A cover-letter draft and a self-review applying the journal's published review questions are included. The official template and review prompt request a short Slovenian abstract. Repository instructions require English on disk, so no unapproved translation is invented; the author/editor must arrange that small journal-specific addition before submission. The manuscript's main abstract is English and complete.

Official local preparation sources: [Word template](https://www.informatica.si/public/site/Informatica2026.dotx), [sample DOCX](https://www.informatica.si/public/site/word_example.docx), [review prompt](https://www.informatica.si/public/site/LLM_prompt_informatica.txt). Their downloaded copies are local author-preparation material, excluded from redistribution because no redistribution license was established. The DOCX build manifest records the exact template SHA-256. Re-download the template from its official URL if rebuilding a checkout without the local copy.

## Open Research Europe: the European Union venue

Open Research Europe is a European Commission-established publishing platform with open peer review, rather than a conventional subscription journal. It is the portfolio's EU venue. The official CERN-hosted transition page states that publication is free for authors and readers, that EU-framework-funded researchers remain eligible, and that eligibility is expanding from autumn 2026 to researchers in participating countries. Source: [official ORE transition and eligibility page](https://ore.eu/).

No EU grant, institutional affiliation, or eligibility has been established for this project. A timezone or machine location is not evidence of eligibility. The transition announcement does not by itself prove the active submission system accepts a particular author on 28 September 2026. The manuscript is prepared as a research article with a structured abstract, methods, complete outcome reporting, reuse information, data/software availability, and declarations. The detailed legacy research-article guideline endpoint could not be retrieved during this audit; fine-grained platform-specific formatting and the live eligibility check remain unverified. Source endpoint attempted: [legacy research-article guidelines](https://open-research-europe.ec.europa.eu/for-authors/article-guidelines/research-articles).

The data availability statement is deliberately accurate: this is a local evidence package, not an already deposited public dataset. A trusted public deposit, appropriate source-rights review, and persistent identifier are prerequisites for a complete open-research submission. No Horizon deliverable number or grant acknowledgement is invented.

## Overlap and submission order

Changing the audience does not make shared experiments independent. Manuscripts 01, 02, and 04 substantially overlap in their empirical basis. Manuscript 03 adds an artifact-audit question and manuscript 05 a normative argument, but both still reuse the case. The preparation therefore supports alternative routes, with one substantially overlapping manuscript under review at a time. If the authors later seek separate publications, they must disclose related manuscripts, cite any prior publication, identify the additional contribution, and follow the receiving editors' decisions. This follows the explicit [Machine Learning overlap policy](https://link.springer.com/journal/10994/submission-guidelines) and the other journals' originality requirements.

For a technical account, Informatica is the most direct scope match. For an eligible author able to release the evidence, ORE offers the clearest open-data route. The other manuscripts support decisions based on the contribution the authors most want reviewed. No acceptance probability or guaranteed outcome is assigned.
