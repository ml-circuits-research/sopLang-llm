# Portfolio plan (revision of 28 September 2026, reviewed and revised 29 September)

The earlier five manuscripts all rested on the same narrow comparison from the last experimental arms and presented it as an audit. This revision starts from the whole experimental history (27 training arms, the diagnostic suites, the base comparisons, the benchmark sanity check, and the decision records) and asks which distinct lessons the evidence actually supports. It supports four papers with different questions and different conclusions. A fifth paper would restate one of these four, so the Open Research Europe route is not used for a research article in this revision (see the last section).

## Overlap

All four papers use the same experimental programme and describe SOP Lang briefly. Papers 01 and 02 share two observations from generated programs, the first-series compile failures and the first-exposure failures on newly added operators, and read them differently: 01 as evidence of plan retrieval and operator confusion, 02 as evidence about scaffolding and assertions. The compact-versus-split and declarative-command comparisons appear only in 02. Papers 03 and 04 share the identity-error case: 03 treats it as an instrumentation seam with a mechanical check, 04 as an accountability failure that arose from goal pressure. Cover letters must disclose the related manuscripts.

## Why not a fifth paper now

The previous Open Research Europe manuscript compared two adapted models by task family and applied restricted answer parsers. Its lessons are contained in papers 01 (model comparison) and 03 (answer comparison). A genuinely distinct ORE contribution would be a data note or software tool article describing the verified synthetic dataset, runtime, and evaluation harness. That requires a public deposit and a rights review of the seed books first, so it is listed as a future option rather than written now. The previous Open Research Europe manuscript and the previous AI and Ethics manuscript are kept under `archive/2026-09-28-first-portfolio/` as provenance and are withdrawn from the portfolio.
