How to read this page

Each table states one question and the artifact that answered it. Rows marked in flight are measured by a run that was still writing when this page was last updated; their registry folders are named next to them. Nothing here ranks the arms or explains them: the failure mechanisms, the diagnoses of DS009-fine-tuning-and-evaluation, and the decisions that follow from them are recorded in evaluation/registry/phase4-analysis.md, and the per-arm recipes in evaluation/registry/phase4-first-series.md.
